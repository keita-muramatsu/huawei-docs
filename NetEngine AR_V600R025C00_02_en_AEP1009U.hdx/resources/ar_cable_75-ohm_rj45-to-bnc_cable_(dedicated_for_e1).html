Connection

A 75-ohm RJ45-to-BNC cable is connected as follows:

  • The RJ45 connector is connected to a router.
  • The BNC connectors at the other end are connected to a network device.

If the default length is not enough for cable connection, you can use a 75-ohm trunk extension cable as an extension cable.

When using a 75-ohm trunk extension cable to prolong a 75-ohm RJ45-to-BNC cable, connect one BNC connector of the trunk cable to a coaxial connector and the other BNC connector to the remote device, as shown in Figure 2.
