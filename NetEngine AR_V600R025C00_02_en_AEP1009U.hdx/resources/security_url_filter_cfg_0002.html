Overview of URL Filtering

Definition

Uniform resource locator (URL) filtering, also known as web filtering, is a core function for web security. It regulates online behavior by controlling users' URL access requests, and permitting or denying users' access to certain web resources.

Purpose

As Internet applications develop rapidly and computer networks become ever-more common, it has become much easier to obtain and share information. However, this also poses unprecedented threats to enterprises. For example:

  • Visiting irrelevant websites during working hours reduces work efficiency.
  • Visiting illegitimate or malicious websites may expose confidential information or even incur threats such as worms, viruses, and Trojan horses.
  • During times of high network activity leading to intranet congestion, employees may be unable to access work-related websites (such as the company homepage and search engines), reducing work efficiency.

URL filtering mitigates the preceding threats by controlling which URLs users can access, permitting users to access authorized websites while blocking access to unauthorized websites.
