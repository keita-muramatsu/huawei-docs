License Control Items in the N1 Model

N1 licenses supported by AR5700, AR6700, and AR8000 series routers consist of software licenses and SnS services, both of which are mandatory.

The license-control software packages in N1 mode are classified into the following types:

  • Foundation package: provides controller-based device management and basic network O&M functions, the basic intelligent O&M functions of iMaster NCE-CampusInsight, the data compression function and Adaptive QoS function.
  • Advanced package: includes all functions of the Foundation package and also adds the Intelligent Policy Recommendation functions of iMaster NCE-Campus, , intelligent application analysis function of iMaster NCE-CampusInsightbasic IFIT functions of AR series devices, ITP Function, and IP Multicast over SD-WAN Functions.
  • Upgrade package: upgrades a Foundation package to an Advanced package. This package applies to customers who have already purchased a Foundation package and require the Intelligent Policy Recommendation functions of iMaster NCE-Campus, , intelligent application analysis function of iMaster NCE-CampusInsight basic IFIT functions of AR series devices, ITP Function, and IP Multicast over SD-WAN Functions.
  • Add-On package: adds the SRv6, enhanced IFIT, functions. The Add-On package for the SRv6 function applies to customers who have purchased a Foundation or Advanced package. The Add-On package for enhanced IFIT functions applies to customers who have purchased an Upgrade package.
