Configuring Net Assistant

  • During network deployment, you need to specify a device as the master device. You can log in to the device through the console port and enable the net assistant function through the CLI. For details, see Enabling the Net Assistant Function.
  • After the net assistant function is enabled, you can log in to WebMaster to configure and manage devices on the network without using the CLI. However, configurations mentioned in section "Configuring the Master Device" are mandatory on the CLI (all other configurations can be performed on WebMaster).
  • In this chapter, "entire network" refers to all devices on the network. Network-wide configurations performed on the master device are automatically delivered to and take effect on all member devices. Such configurations include the network-wide username and password.
