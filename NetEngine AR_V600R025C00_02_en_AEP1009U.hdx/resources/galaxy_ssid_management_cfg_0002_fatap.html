Overview of SSID Management

Definition

A service set identifier (SSID) identifies a wireless local area network (WLAN). For example, when you search for available WLANs on a station (STA), the displayed WLAN names are SSIDs.

Purpose

With the development of WLAN services, a WLAN provided by a single access point (AP) becomes unable to meet the increasing service requirements. However, if multiple WLANs are required in a space, deploying an AP for each WLAN is expensive and requires a lot of channel resources. To address this, SSID management allows you to create multiple virtual access points (VAPs) on an AP to provide wireless access services for different user groups.
