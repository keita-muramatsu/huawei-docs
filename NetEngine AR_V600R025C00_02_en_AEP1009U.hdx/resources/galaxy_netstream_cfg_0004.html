NetStream System Components

A typical NetStream system consists of the NetStream Data Exporter (NDE), NetStream Collector (NSC), and NetStream Data Analyzer (NDA).

  • NDE: analyzes and processes network flows, extracts the flows that meet the conditions for statistics collection, and exports the statistics to the NSC. The NDE can process the statistics (including aggregating statistics) before exporting them to the NSC. A NetStream-enabled device functions as the NDE in the NetStream system.

  • NSC: parses the packets received from the NDE and saves statistics to the database for the NDA to analyze. The NSC can collect data exported from multiple NDEs for further processing.

  • NDA: a traffic analysis tool that extracts statistics from the NSC, processes the statistics, and generates a report. This report provides a basis for services such as traffic accounting, network planning, and attack monitoring. The NDA provides a graphical user interface (GUI) for users to easily obtain, check, and analyze collected data.

In real-world situations, the NSC and NDA are deployed on the same NetStream server, and the NDE needs to work with the NetStream server, as shown in Figure 1. The NDE samples packets to obtain information about outgoing traffic on interface 1, and creates NetStream flows based on specified conditions. When the NetStream cache is full or a NetStream flow is aged out, the NDE encapsulates NetStream packets with traffic statistics, and sends the packets to the NetStream server. Subsequently, the NetStream server analyzes the NetStream packets and displays the analysis result.

Figure 1 Networking diagram of a NetStream system

As shown in Figure 1, the NetStream system works as follows:

  1. The NDE samples service traffic in a certain sampling mode and creates NetStream flows.
  2. The NDE ages out NetStream flows that meet aging conditions.
  3. After NetStream flows are aged out, the NDE exports detailed statistics about the flows to the NSC.
  4. The NSC processes the flow statistics and sends them to the NDA.
  5. The NDA analyzes the traffic statistics to help implement accounting and network planning.
