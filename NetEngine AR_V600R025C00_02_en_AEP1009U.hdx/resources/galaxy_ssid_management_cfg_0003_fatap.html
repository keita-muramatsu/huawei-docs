Understanding SSID Management

Basic Concepts of SSID Management

  • VAP

    A VAP is a functional entity on an AP. Multiple VAPs can be created on an AP to provide different wireless access services for different user groups.

  • Basic service set (BSS)

    A BSS is an area covered by a VAP and also the basic service unit of a WLAN. Typically, a BSS consists of a VAP and STAs connected to the VAP.

  • Basic service set identifier (BSSID)

    A BSSID is used as the data link–layer MAC address that identifies each VAP. It also uniquely identifies a BSS.

  • Extended service set (ESS)

    An ESS consists of multiple BSSs that use the same SSID. The ESS extends the coverage of a BSS and ensures service continuity when a STA moves from one BSS to another.

  • Extended service set identifier (ESSID)

    An ESSID is used to identify one or a group of WLANs. When you search for available WLANs on a STA, the WLAN names displayed are actually ESSIDs. In most cases, the SSID is the same as the ESSID.

Relationship Between Basic Concepts

Relationship between the VAP and BSS

Figure 1 shows the relationship between the VAP and BSS. VAP1 and VAP2 are created on the AP. STA1 connects to the SSID guest, and STA2 connects to the SSID internal.

  • VAP1 and STA1 form a BSS whose BSSID is 00e0.fc45.24a0 and ESSID is guest.
  • VAP2 and STA2 form a BSS whose BSSID is 00e0.fc45.24a9 and ESSID is internal.
Figure 1 Relationship between the BSSID and VAP

Relationship between the BSS, BSSID, ESS, and SSID

Figure 2 shows the relationship between the BSS, BSSID, ESS, and SSID. AP1 and STAs form a BSS whose BSSID is 00e0.fc45.24a0. AP2 and STAs form another BSS whose BSSID is 00e0.fc45.3100. The SSIDs of the two BSSs are both huawei. Therefore, the two BSSs form an ESS.
