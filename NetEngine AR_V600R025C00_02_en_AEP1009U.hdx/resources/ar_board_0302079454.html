Ports

Figure 3 Ports on the SIC-1V35B-V2

1. VDSL interface

-
Table 4 Ports on the SIC-1V35B-V2
Port Connector Type Description Available Components
VDSL interface RJ11

A VDSL 35B interface provides high-speed WAN access through twisted pairs. It supports only the PTM mode. In this mode, VDSL lines carry Ethernet packets.

Standards compliance:

  • ITU G.993.2 (VDSL2) and supported profiles: 8a, 8b, 8c, 8d, 12a, 12b, 17a, 30a, and 35b
  • VDSL2 Vectoring (G993.5): Only VDSL2 vectoring over POTS is supported.

Rate: Asymmetric uplink and downlink rates are supported, with a maximum uplink of 40 Mbit/s and downlink of 350 Mbit/s.

Universal telephone cable

Overview

A 1V35B-V2 card is a WAN connection module that provides bidirectional DSL services for enterprises, transmitting data at a high speed on the Internet.
