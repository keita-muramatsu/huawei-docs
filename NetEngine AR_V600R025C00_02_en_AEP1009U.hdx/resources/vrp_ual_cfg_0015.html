UAL Application Scenarios

On small- and medium-sized campus networks, the NMS deployment rate is low, with many still relying on traditional manual methods for deployment, configuration, and O&M. However, such an approach lacks usability and efficiency.

On the network shown in Figure 1, the WebMaster network management function can be deployed on the AR router, eliminating the need for a dedicated server for deploying the NMS. WebMaster uses the UAL protocol to automatically discover and manage devices on the entire network, implementing network-wide management, batch configuration, and visualized O&M.
