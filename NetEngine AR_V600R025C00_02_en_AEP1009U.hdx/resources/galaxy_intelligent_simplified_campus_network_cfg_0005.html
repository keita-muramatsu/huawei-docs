Power supply mode
NOTE:

An RU whose name contains P is a PoE-capable RU. Only PoE-capable RUs can be powered by the central device and supply power to terminals through PoE. Whether an RU supports PoE depends on the RU hardware. A PoE-incapable RU cannot be changed to a PoE-capable RU by upgrading the RU firmware.

The default power supply mode of the following models is PoE++:

S5731-L4P2HW-RUA, S5731S-L4P2HW-RUA, S5731-L4P2S-RUA, S5731S-L4P2S-RUA, S5731-L4P2ST-RUA, S5731S-L4P2ST-RUA, S5731-L4P2HT-RUA, S5731S-L4P2HT-RUA

The default power supply mode of the following models is PoE+:

S5731-L8P2ST-RUA, S5731S-L8P2ST-RUA, S5731-L8P2HT-RUA, S5731S-L8P2HT-RUA, S5751-L8LP2X-RUA, S5751-L8LP2J-RUA, S5751-L8P2X-RUA, S5751-L8P2J-RUA, S5751-L8P2HX-RUA, S5751-L8P2HJ-RUA, S5751-L8P2PN2J-RUA, S5751-L8P2PN2X-RUA, S5751-L8P2PN2HX-RUA, S5751-L24P2J-RUA, S5751-L24P2X-RUA, S5751-L24P2PN2X-RUA
