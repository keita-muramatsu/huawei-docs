Data Forwarding Mode

Two types of packets are exchanged between the WAC and APs on a WLAN: control packets (management packets) and data packets. Control packets are forwarded through CAPWAP tunnels. Data packets are forwarded in direct forwarding mode.

Direct Forwarding

In direct forwarding mode, APs forward user data packets to the upper-layer network without encapsulating them over CAPWAP tunnels. Figure 1 shows the direct forwarding process.
Figure 1 Direct forwarding

When the WAC and APs are connected across Layer 3 and the APs work in direct forwarding mode, tunnel forwarding is enabled for HTTP, EAP, and WAPI packets by default, which cannot be configured.
