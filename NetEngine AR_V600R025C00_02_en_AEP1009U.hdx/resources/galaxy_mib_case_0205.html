Downloading MIB Files

You can download MIB files from the Huawei technical support website. The procedure is as follows:

  1. Log in to Huawei technical support website for enterprise users.
  2. In the Enterprise Network area, click Routers, and select a product series or model to access the corresponding product page. You can also enter the product model in the search box on the home page to go to the corresponding product page.
  3. Click the Software tab and select the corresponding version for Select Version.
  4. Click the version under Version and Patch Number to go to the software download page.
  5. Obtain the XXXX_MIB.zip file.
