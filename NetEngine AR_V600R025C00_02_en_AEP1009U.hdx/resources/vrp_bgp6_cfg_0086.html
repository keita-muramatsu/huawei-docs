Understanding BGP4+ Peer Groups

A large-scale BGP4+ network has a large number of peers. It is difficult to configure and maintain them. Peer groups can be configured to simplify the management and improve the efficiency of route advertisement. Peer groups are classified into IBGP peer groups and EBGP peer groups based on whether the peers reside in the same AS. If all the peers in an EBGP peer group reside in the same external AS, the group is a pure EBGP peer group; otherwise, the group is a mixed EBGP peer group.
