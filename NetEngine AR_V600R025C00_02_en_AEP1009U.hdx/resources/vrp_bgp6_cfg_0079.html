Understanding BGP4+ Route Advertisement

BGP4+ adopts the following policies to advertise routes:
  • In the case of multiple valid routes, a BGP4+ speaker advertises only the optimal route to its peers.
  • A BGP4+ speaker advertises the routes received from EBGP peers to all its BGP4+ peers, including EBGP and IBGP peers.
  • A BGP4+ speaker does not advertise the routes received from an IBGP peer to other IBGP peers.

    This rule does not apply to the scenario where the peer from which a route is learned and the peer to which the route needs to be advertised belong to different address families.

  • A BGP4+ speaker advertises the routes received from IBGP peers to its EBGP peers.
  • A BGP4+ speaker advertises all its optimal BGP4+ routes to new peers once the peer relationships are established.

A large number of routes typically exist in a BGP4+ routing table, and transmitting such extensive routing information brings heavy burden to a device. In order to address this problem, it is necessary to filter those routes to be advertised. You can configure a device to advertise only necessary routes or those that its peers require.

Multiple routes to the same destination may exist and traverse different ASs. To direct traffic to specific ASs, routes to be advertised also need to be filtered.

Filters can be used to filter routes to be advertised by BGP4+, and BGP4+ can filter routes to be advertised to a specific peer or peer group. If multiple filtering policies are configured, BGP4+ advertises only those routes that match all the configured policies.
