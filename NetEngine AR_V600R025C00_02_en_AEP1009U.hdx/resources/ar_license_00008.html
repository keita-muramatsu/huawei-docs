Obtaining Temporary Licenses

Temporary licenses provide a trial of new features and apply to POC tests during the market expansion, product demonstration, and R&D test before product release.

Temporary licenses are provided according to the following rules:

  • Only one user is authorized to use a temporary license, and the user name is unchangeable.
  • The validity period of a temporary license is fixed.

To obtain a temporary license, contact Huawei technical support personnel.
