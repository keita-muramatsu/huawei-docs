Managing Wireless Link Quality

Context

On a WLAN, wireless radio, as the transmission media, is prone to surrounding interference. The transmission quality of service data changes greatly depending on the interference. Therefore, you must evaluate and check the transmission quality of wireless links to ensure better service data transmission and efficient cooperation between densely deployed WLANs, thereby reducing signal interference.

The retransmission rate determines whether the radio environment is normal. When the retransmission rate of a radio exceeds the specified threshold, the system considers that the radio environment deteriorates. When this occurs, you can perform radio calibration or take measures to avoid signal interference.

The RF ping function can be used to exchange data packets between APs and STAs to check the transmission quality of wireless links. The link check result includes the signal strength, air interface rate, and packet transmission delay, which can comprehensively show the transmission quality of wireless links.

Procedure

  • Manage the retransmission rate.
    1. Enter the system view.

      system-view

    2. Enter the WLAN view.

      wlan

    3. Create an RRM profile and enter its view.

      rrm-profile name profile-name

    4. Adjust the retransmission rate threshold.

      calibrate retransmission-rate-threshold retransmission-rate-threshold

      By default, the retransmission rate threshold is 60%.

  • Detect wireless link quality.
    1. Enter the system view.

      system-view

    2. Enter the WLAN view.

      wlan

    3. Detect wireless link quality.

      rf-ping [ -m time | -c number | -p { be | bk | vi | vo } ] * mac-address
