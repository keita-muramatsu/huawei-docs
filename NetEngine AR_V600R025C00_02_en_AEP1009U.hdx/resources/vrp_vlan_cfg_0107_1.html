Understanding VLAN Termination

Definition

VLAN termination enables a device to identify VLAN tags in received packets, remove single or double VLAN tags from the packets based on subsequent forwarding behaviors, and then forward the packets at Layer 3 or perform other actions. These VLAN tags take effect only before termination, and are not used in subsequent Layer 3 forwarding or other actions.

VLAN termination involves the following aspects:
  • The device removes VLAN tags from packets received on an interface, and then forwards the packets at Layer 3 or performs other actions.
  • The device adds VLAN tags to packets to be sent from an interface, and then sends the packets.

Classification

Depending on the modes in which VLAN-tagged packets are processed, VLAN termination is classified into the following types:

  • Dot1q termination: The device removes the outer VLAN tag from the received single-tagged or double-tagged packets, and adds a VLAN tag to the packets sent from an interface.
  • QinQ termination: The device removes double VLAN tags from the received double-tagged packets, and adds double VLAN tags to the packets sent from an interface.

Typically, VLAN termination is performed on a sub-interface. A sub-interface that terminates a single VLAN tag in packets is called a dot1q termination sub-interface, whereas one that terminates double VLAN tags in packets is called a QinQ termination sub-interface.

Dot1q and QinQ termination sub-interfaces do not support transparent transmission of untagged packets and therefore discard received untagged packets.

Purpose

After VLANs are assigned, hosts in the same VLAN can communicate with each other at Layer 2, whereas hosts in different VLANs cannot. You can configure VLANIF interfaces or termination sub-interfaces on the device to implement Layer 3 communication between VLANs. A Layer 3 Ethernet sub-interface does not support VLAN-tagged packets. When receiving VLAN-tagged packets, the Layer 3 Ethernet sub-interface considers the packets as invalid packets and discards them. Therefore, you need to configure VLAN termination on the Layer 3 Ethernet sub-interface so that the sub-interface can remove VLAN tags from packets.
