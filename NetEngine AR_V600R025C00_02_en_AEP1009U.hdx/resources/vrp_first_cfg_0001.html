First Login to a Device Configuration

  • This section describes how to log in to a device for the first time through a console port.
  • If you do not have a console cable or your PC does not have available serial ports, you can log in to the device for the first time through the web system and configure the device. By default, the HTTPS service is enabled on the device. For details, see "Web Configuration Guide > Before You Start > Logging In to the Device for the First Time Through the Web System".
