Connection

A DC power cable is connected as follows:

  • One end is connected to the matching DC power terminal on the DC power module of a router.
  • The other end is connected to an external power source.

Appearance and Structure

Figure 1 Structure of the DC Power Cable

DC power cables for a router include a -48 V return ground cable (blue) and a -48 V power cable (black), which are connected to the RTN(+) and NEG(-) terminals on the router's DC power module respectively.
