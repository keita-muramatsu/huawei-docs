Powering On or Off a Board

Context

When a board is idle, you can power it off without affecting services to ensure stable system operation and save energy. Likewise, you can also power on a specified board if the service volume increases.

A board will be automatically powered off due to insufficient resources. In this case, you can run the power on command to restart the board.

Procedure

  1. (Optional) Check the status of boards on a device.

    display device

  2. Power on or off a specified board.

    • Power on a specified board.
      power on slot slot-id
    • Power off a specified board.
      power off slot slot-id
