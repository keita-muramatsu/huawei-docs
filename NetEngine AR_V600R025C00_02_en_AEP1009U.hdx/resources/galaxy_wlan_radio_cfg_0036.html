Understanding Spectrum Analysis

Definition

In spectrum analysis, WLAN devices detect different types of interference sources on WLANs, and the spectrum analysis server analyzes the characteristics of collected wireless signals. Based on the swept spectrogram and fast Fourier transform (FFT) information about interference signals, interference devices can be identified and located, eliminating the impact of interference on WLANs. During this process, FFT is used to convert time-domain signals into frequency-domain signals, identify non-Wi-Fi interference devices, and display the frequency components of signals.

Purpose

802.11 wireless technology has been widely applied, allowing users to easily access the Internet over WLANs. However, this technology uses public spectrum resources, which are also used by Bluetooth devices, cordless phones, and many other wireless devices. This causes severe radio signal conflict and interference on WLANs, resulting in poor user experience.

Spectrum analysis allows users to view the swept spectrogram and FFT information about interference signals, helping users locate the interference sources to eliminate interference and improve user experience.
