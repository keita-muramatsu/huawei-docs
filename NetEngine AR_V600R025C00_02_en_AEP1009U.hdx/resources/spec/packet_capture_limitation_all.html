Feature Requirements

If only the function of obtaining hardware-forwarded packets is configured, other service or protocol packets sent to the host cannot be obtained. To obtain the packets sent to the host, run the capture-packet local-host command.

The packet capture function is restricted by the maximum packet length of the channel. The packets whose frame length exceeds the upper limit of the register cannot be obtained.

The inner packets of GRE packets have no source or destination MAC address. Therefore, when such inner packets are obtained based on ACLs, the source and MAC addresses of packets cannot be obtained.

If a packet carries a VLAN tag, the device can parse only packets with a VLAN tag whose TPID is 0x8100.

Limitations on obtaining MPLS packets:

- A maximum of five labels can be parsed for MPLS packets.

Obtaining software-forwarded packets:

1. Packets can be obtained based on Layer 2 and Layer 3 information on the ingress, transit, and egress nodes of an MPLS tunnel.

2. When penultimate hop popping (PHP) is configured on the transit node, non-MPLS packets are displayed on the egress node.

3. Original non-MPLS packets are obtained in the inbound direction of the ingress node and outbound direction of the egress node of the MPLS tunnel. MPLS packets are obtained on other nodes.

Obtaining packets on hosts:

1. Packets can be obtained on the egress node of the tunnel based on Layer 3 information. In this case, a Layer 2 packet header, rather than the original packet header, is displayed.

Number of packet capture interfaces that can be specified:

- Host packet obtaining (local-host): Four interfaces can be specified.

- Software-forwarded packet obtaining: One interface can be specified.

The obtained packet information is temporarily saved into a .cap file in the flash memory. When the available space of the flash memory is less than or equal to 10 MB, the obtained packet information can no longer be saved and the packet capture function becomes unavailable.

Limitation on obtaining packets on Eth-Trunk member interfaces:

The functions of obtaining hardware-forwarded packets and packets on the local host do not apply to Eth-Trunk member interfaces.

For the AR5710-H4T4N2X, AR5710-H8T2TS1-T, AR6710-L8T3TS1X2, AR6710-L8T3TS1X2-T, AR5710-H2T4PN2X-NRGL, AR5710-H4T4N2X-LTE4EA, AR5710-H8T2TS1, AR5710-H2T4N2X-NRGL:

In software-based forwarding mode:

- Hardware-forwarded packets on the cellular interface in the downlink direction can be obtained.

- On the cellular interface in the uplink direction or other types of interfaces, only hardware-forwarded broadcast, multicast, and unknown unicast packets can be obtained.
