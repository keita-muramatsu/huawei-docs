Precautions for RUs:

  • RUs must be directly connected to central devices. One interface of a central device can be connected to only one RU. RUs cannot form a ring or chain network.
  • An RU can directly connect to a central device only through a fixed uplink interface.
  • All uplink interfaces of an RU can only be connected to the same central device. In addition, when an RU is connected to a central device through two uplink interfaces, you need to manually add the two connected interfaces on the central device to the same Eth-Trunk, and the Eth-Trunk can only work in manual mode.
  • RUs can connect to only central devices through uplink interfaces, and cannot connect to terminals or APs through uplink interfaces. RUs cannot connect to central devices through downlink interfaces.
  • RUs can connect to only terminals and APs through downlink interfaces, and cannot connect to hubs or switches through downlink interfaces.
  • After the number of RUs connected to a central device exceeds the specification, the central device cannot manage the excess RUs, but can forward data traffic of the RUs.
  • Phones connected to an RU must belong to the same voice VLAN.
  • During the BIOS firmware upgrade of RUs, do not power off or disconnect the RUs. Otherwise, the RUs cannot be started.
  • Downlink interfaces of an RU are isolated from each other. When users access the network through an RU, proxy ARP must be enabled on the Layer 3 gateway device.
  • Uplink optical interfaces on RUs do not support copper modules.
