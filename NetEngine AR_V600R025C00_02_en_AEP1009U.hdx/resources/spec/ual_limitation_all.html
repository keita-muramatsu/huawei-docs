Feature Requirements

Feature Requirements

STP orchestration starts from the management gateway. If a third-party NE exists or existing devices do not support orchestration, the orchestration effect cannot be achieved. The blocked ports may not be eastbound or westbound ports.

In the hybrid networking scenario, you are advised to manually configure STP to specify blocked ports.

STP orchestration cannot be performed for the following devices:

1. Switches running V200

2. AR routers running V300

3. CloudEngine S5731I series switches

4. APs

The network-wide orchestration has the highest priority. If you delete the local commands that are orchestrated and delivered through STP, the commands will be delivered again after the topology changes and the node restarts.

Orchestrate the STP priority of instance 0. Multi-instance orchestration is not supported.

Managed devices support orchestration. After orchestration, STP reconvergence is performed, causing temporary traffic interruption.

1. PEN interfaces do not support Eth-Trunk auto-negotiation.

2. Eth-Trunk auto-negotiation is not supported in M-LAG scenarios.

3. Interconnection with switches running V200 or CloudEngine S5731I series devices is not supported.

4. Layer 3 interfaces do not support Eth-Trunk auto-negotiation.

If the Eth-Trunk auto-negotiation function is required during device capacity expansion or replacement, the device must be unconfigured.

After the negotiation is complete at the local end, the system starts to check the negotiation result at the peer end. If the Eth-Trunk interface remains down, negotiation rollback is performed (to prevent device disconnection in exception scenarios).

Topology information collection depends on LLDP. Therefore, LLDP must be enabled on NEs.

After an NE is managed, orchestration is proactively performed to enable LLDP. If LLDP is manually disabled, the topology may be incorrectly displayed. In this case, LLDP needs to be enabled again.

If an egress gateway exists, the topology is displayed by layer starting from the egress gateway. If no egress gateway exists, the topology is displayed by layer starting from the master device.

Eth-Trunk self-orchestration sequence requirements

Manually configure Eth-Trunk on devices from near to far depending on their topology levels, that is, their distances from the LC (root of the tree).

Unapproved NEs are displayed only in the topology and not in the device list. In addition, topology information about these NEs is not collected. If topology information about their neighbors is also not reported, these NEs are displayed as isolated devices.

In the version downgrade scenario, if the CoAP protocol is supported before the downgrade but not supported after the downgrade, the NE is displayed as offline on the GUI.

You can manually delete the NE after the downgrade. If the NE needs to be managed, you can import the NE.

The native WAC capability must be enabled on the active device.
