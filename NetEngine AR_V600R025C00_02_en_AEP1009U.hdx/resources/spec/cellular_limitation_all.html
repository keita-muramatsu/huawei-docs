Precautions for Cellular Support

The hardware that provides the cellular function includes the cellular card and cellular model.

  • Cellular cards include 3G & LTE cards and 5G cards. For details about the device-card mapping and the support of 5G, 4G, and 3G functions by cards, see "Version Mapping" and "Ports" in Get to Know the Product > Hardware Description > Cards > 5G Card or Get to Know the Product > Hardware Description > Cards > 3G&LTE Card, respectively. The device must have a cellular card installed to support the cellular function.
  • Cellular models include LTE models and 5G models. If the name of a device contains LTE, the device is a 4G model. If the name of a device contains NR, the device is a 5G model. For details about whether a device supports 5G, 4G, or 3G, see "Ports" in Get to Know the Product > Hardware Description > Chassis > XXX Model.
