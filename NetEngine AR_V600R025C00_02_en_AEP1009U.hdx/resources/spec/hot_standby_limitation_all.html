Generally, hot standby runs in load balancing mode. If hot standby runs in active/standby mode, the VRRP negotiation of the standby device is restricted. If the function of adjusting the cost of dynamic routing protocols based on the active/standby status is enabled, the costs of dynamic routing protocols such as OSPF, OSPFv3, and BGP on the standby device increase. The following configurations may cause hot standby to switch to active/standby mode: hrp device, hrp switch, hrp track bfd-session, hrp track interface, and hrp track trunk-member enable.
