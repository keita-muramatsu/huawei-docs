Function specifications of packet replication

In a dual-gateway scenario, interlink disconnections may occur in the following situations:

1. The interlink interface fails.

2. The heartbeat link is faulty.

3. One device is restarted.

4. An SD-WAN EVPN site is configured as a dual-gateway site, but one of the gateways does not go online.

After the interlink is disconnected, the packet replication function becomes inactive at both the transmit and receive ends. The transmit end no longer replicates packets, while the receive end sends only original packets after removing packet replication headers and discards other types of packets.

Function specifications of packet replication

When symmetric path selection is used for SPR and the master device is the transmitting end of packet replication, symmetric path selection does not take effect. That is, the slave device does not use the traffic steering result of the master device.

Function specifications of packet replication

For traffic that has been replicated, the device no longer performs data compression and FEC on the traffic.

Function specifications of packet replication

Packet replication does not take effect on fragments. For flows with packet replication enabled, if a fragmented packet is received, replication is not performed for any subsequent packets in the flow.
