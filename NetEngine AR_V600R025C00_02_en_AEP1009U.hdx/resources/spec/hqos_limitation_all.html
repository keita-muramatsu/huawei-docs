Basic HQoS Functions

The minimum rate limit of HQoS queues is 64 kbps. When the configured queue rate limit is less than the minimum rate limit (calculated based on the percentage), the minimum rate limit is delivered, including the rate limit of flow queues and subscriber queues.

MQC forwarding statistics: excluding the CRC header and tunnel header

Basic HQoS Functions

MQC-based HQoS configuration limitations:

- HQoS cannot be configured together with redirection and deny.

- You can configure HQoS by configuring both parent and child traffic policies or configuring subscriber queues or flow queues in a parent traffic policy.

Basic HQoS Functions

In each parent traffic policy, a maximum of four subscriber queues using PQ scheduling can be configured. In each child traffic policy, a maximum of four flow queues using PQ scheduling can be configured.

Basic HQoS Functions

For the AR5710-H4T4N2X, AR5710-H8T2TS1-T, AR6710-L8T3TS1X2, AR6710-L8T3TS1X2-T, AR5710-H2T4PN2X-NRGL, AR5710-H4T4N2X-LTE4EA, AR5710-H8T2TS1, AR5710-H2T4N2X-NRGL:

If an HQoS (including CBQ) policy is applied to an interface, the sum of CIRs of subscriber queues must be less than or equal to the interface bandwidth. Otherwise, the CIR of each subscriber queue cannot be ensured.

Basic HQoS Functions

For the AR8700-8, AR6710-H4T4X2Y7, AR6710-L14T2X4:

DRR queue scheduling limitations:

For the AR8700-8, AR6710-H4T4X2Y7:

When there are more than seven DRR queues under the same scheduling node, the weight scheduling among DRR queues is inaccurate. It is recommended that the number of traffic classifier-behavior pairs in each child policy of HQoS be less than or equal to 7 and the number of traffic classifier-behavior pairs of the CBQ of the flow-queue type be less than or equal to 7.

For the AR6710-L14T2X4:

When there are more than three DRR queues under the same scheduling node, the weight scheduling among DRR queues is inaccurate. It is recommended that the number of traffic classifier-behavior pairs in each child policy of HQoS be less than or equal to 3 and the number of traffic classifier-behavior pairs of the CBQ of the flow-queue type be less than or equal to 3.

Intelligent Traffic Policing

1. If the UDP traffic ratio is high in Internet access application services, the expected effect cannot be achieved.

2. After intelligent traffic policing is enabled, the experience of low-priority applications may deteriorate due to periodic detection and backpressure on the bandwidth of low-priority applications.

3. In the child policy of intelligent traffic policing, at least one high-priority traffic classifier-behavior pair and one low-priority traffic classifier-behavior pair must be configured, and a maximum of seven high-priority traffic classifier-behavior pairs and one low-priority traffic classifier-behavior pair can be configured.

4. After intelligent traffic policing is enabled, the frame freezing rate of the video conference application is within 2%, if only one high-priority traffic classifier-behavior pair is configured in the child policy of intelligent traffic policing and the traffic classifier of the traffic classifier-behavior pair contains only the video conference application. If the traffic classifier of the high-priority traffic classifier-behavior pair contains multiple applications or if multiple high-priority traffic classifier-behavior pairs are configured, the frame freezing rate of the video conference application cannot be ensured.

Hardware Offload

For the AR5710-H4T4N2X, AR5710-H8T2TS1-T, AR6710-L8T3TS1X2, AR6710-L8T3TS1X2-T, AR5710-H2T4PN2X-NRGL, AR5710-H4T4N2X-LTE4EA, AR5710-H8T2TS1, AR5710-H2T4N2X-NRGL:

After hardware route-forwarding qos enable is configured, if DRR scheduling is configured for subscriber queues in HQoS and the CIR and PIR are configured, the bandwidth is allocated based on the DRR weight, instead of the ratio of the CIR.

It is recommended that the CIR ratio of subscriber queues be the same as the DRR weight.

Subscriber queue

For the AR8700-8, AR6710-L50T2X4, AR6710-L26T2X4, AR6710-L50T2X4-T, AR6710-L26T2X4-T:

For scheduling among multiple subscriber queues using PQ scheduling, DRR scheduling is used.

Queue capability

For the AR8000 series, AR5700 series, AR6710-L8T3TS1X2, AR6710-L50T2X4, AR6710-L8T3TS1X2-T, AR6710-H4T4X2Y7, AR6710-L26T2X4, AR6710-L50T2X4-T, AR6710-L26T2X4-T:

After QoS/HQoS scheduling and rate limiting are performed on logical interfaces (such as tunnel interfaces, Eth-Trunk interfaces, and dialer interfaces), queue scheduling is not performed on physical outbound interfaces. That is, scheduling on logical interfaces takes precedence.
