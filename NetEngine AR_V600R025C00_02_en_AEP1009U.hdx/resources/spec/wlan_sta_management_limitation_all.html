WLAN STA Access Management

If the 6 GHz frequency band is used, a 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz VAP must be configured. The Beacon frames of this VAP carry 6 GHz frequency band information so that 6 GHz users can discover radio signals. Without such VAP configuration, STAs may fail to scan Wi-Fi signals on the 6 GHz frequency band or the discovery is slow during scanning.
