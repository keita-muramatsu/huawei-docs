Configuring NSR

Context

As networks develop, the demand for data, audio, and video services grows. This poses higher requirements on IP network reliability. If a network node fails or an AMB/SMB switchover is performed by a maintenance operator, the device may fail to converge routes. In this case, packet loss or even a network breakdown may occur. NSR can address this problem and ensure uninterrupted forwarding of critical services.

Procedure

NSR is enabled on a device by default and does not need to be configured.
