Application Scenarios of CSI Sensing

CSI sensing technology accurately senses human activities in physical spaces. It supports intelligent device energy consumption management and security protection, enabling efficient campus operations.

Smart Lighting for Saving Energy in Conference Rooms

In conference rooms on smart campuses, device usage exhibits a clear tidal characteristic: surging when a meeting starts and dropping sharply after the meeting ends. With CSI sensing technology, the system can monitor the activity of people in conference rooms in real time and intelligently control the device status. When someone enters the meeting room, the system automatically wakes up and starts the meeting room devices, such as the smart screen, air conditioner, lighting, and curtain rail, to provide an appropriate working environment. If no one is present for a long time (for example, more than 10 minutes), the system automatically shuts down devices or reduces their power, saving energy and reducing the carbon footprint.

Intelligent Energy Saving and Guest Room Fraud Detection in Hotels

In hotels, energy saving is achieved through a key card switch. However, guests may leave the key card in the slot when going out, wasting energy. To address this issue, some hotels use infrared sensors in guest rooms to replace the key card switch. However, infrared sensors can cover only a limited area. Additionally, some employees may commit fraud by not recording guests' actual check-in information in the hotel management system or concealing guests' check-in information to embezzle room fees or for other improper benefits. CSI sensing technology can accurately monitor guests entering and leaving rooms by detecting the fluctuation of electromagnetic wave reflection signals, without any invasion of privacy. This helps hotels achieve energy saving and room fraud detection.

Saving Energy in Open Indoor Areas

In open indoor areas of office buildings, such as lobbies and corridors, device usage also exhibits tidal characteristics. In such areas, there are no obvious boundaries or area divisions, and so the CSI sensing result of a single AP is usually insufficient to accurately determine the human activity of the entire area. To address this, the system integrates CSI sensing data of multiple APs for a more complete picture of the human activity in the area. And by setting a long period (longer than 15 minutes recommended) for determining the unattended state in such areas, the system can reduce unnecessary device startups and shutdowns, improving user experience and consistently saving energy.

Intrusion Detection

In warehouses or R&D sites with high security requirements, CSI sensing technology provides an innovative non-contact intrusion detection method. The system can quickly identify typical intrusion behaviors (such as people entering and leaving the room, and doors and windows being opened and closed) and immediately trigger alarms to protect core assets and R&D sites. Compared with traditional monitoring devices, CSI sensing technology can better ensure privacy and security.
