(Optional) Configuring One-Label-per-Next-Hop Label Distribution on ASBRs

Context

In an inter-AS VPN Option B scenario, after one-label-per-next-hop label distribution is enabled on an ASBR, the ASBR assigns only one label to VPNv4 routes that share the same next hop and same outgoing label. Compared with on-label-per-route label distribution, one-label-per-next-hop label distribution significantly saves label resources on the ASBR.

Perform the following steps on each ASBR.

Procedure

  1. Enter the system view.

    system-view

  2. Enter the BGP view.

    bgp as-number

  3. Enter the BGP-VPNv4 address family view.

    ipv4-family vpnv4 [ unicast ]

  4. Enable one-label-per-next-hop label distribution for VPNv4 routes on the ASBR.

    apply-label per-nexthop

    After one-label-per-next-hop label distribution is enabled or disabled on an ASBR, the labels assigned by the ASBR to routes change. As a result, temporary packet loss may occur.
