Functions Supported by Net Assistant

Net assistant provides the following functions:

Automation

  • Automatic discovery: The master device automatically discovers information about all member devices on the network based on the UAL protocol and collects their LLDP neighbor information to generate a network topology. The master device automatically delivers network-wide configurations (such as network-wide VLAN orchestration, network-wide username and password, and basic wireless configurations) based on the topology to complete automatic management of network devices.
  • Configuration-free automatic replacement of member devices: If a member device is faulty or damaged, you can connect a new device of the same model to the network using the original cable connection of the old device. The new device then is automatically discovered. After you confirm the replacement, the original configuration is automatically synchronized to the new device and the device is automatically restarted to complete the replacement.
  • Eth-Trunk auto-negotiation: Member devices automatically add their interconnected physical interfaces to an Eth-Trunk interface based on the neighbor relationship. Link aggregation technology is used to bundle multiple Ethernet physical links into a logical link, which effectively improves the reliability of links between the devices.

One-click Batch Operation

  • One-click batch service provisioning: In just one click, you can create a VLAN or configure service network parameters and WLAN parameters such as WLAN SSIDs. This enables service networking configurations to be automatically implemented.
  • One-click network-wide upgrade/patching: In just one click, you can upgrade or patch devices on the entire network. After configuring the file server address (a master device or third-party device), username for file transfer, and files to be transferred on WebMaster, you can upgrade or patch all devices or a specified type of device on the network in just one click.
  • One-click batch configuration of network-wide devices: In just one click, you can configure the management accounts and passwords for all devices on the network in batches.
  • One-click batch restart of network-wide devices: In just one click, you can restart devices on the entire network in batches. The net assistant function can restart all devices on the network from bottom to top according to the topology.
  • One-click batch import of existing devices: In just one click, you can import existing devices on the network in batches. After the import, you can perform subsequent operations on these devices, such as approval and management.

Visualization

  • Topology visualization: Net assistant provides visualized network topologies, covering the physical network topology information (including device models, device status, and interface connections) and logical network topology information (such as the topology of each VLAN service network).
  • Health visualization: Net assistant measures the running status of all devices on the network, including the health status of NE resources such as the CPU usage, memory usage, and chassis temperature.
  • Alarm visualization: The active alarm information of member devices is visualized. After periodic alarm synchronization is enabled on the master device, this device periodically obtains active critical and major alarms from member devices.
