A GE electrical interface (10/100/1000 Mbit/s auto-sensing) transmits and receives services at 10 Mbit/s, 100 Mbit/s, or 1000 Mbit/s.

  • Standards compliance: IEEE802.3, IEEE802.3u, IEEE802.3ab
  • Working mode: Full-duplex (10/100/1000 Mbit/s auto-sensing)
  • Maximum transmission distance: 100 m

Ethernet cable
LAN interfaces: four 2.5GE electrical interfaces RJ45

A 2.5GE electrical interface (auto-sensing for 10 Mbit/s, 100 Mbit/s, 1000 Mbit/s, and 2.5 Gbit/s) transmits and receives services at 10 Mbit/s, 100 Mbit/s, 1000 Mbit/s, or 2.5 Gbit/s.

- Standards compliance: IEEE 802.3, IEEE 802.3u, and IEEE 802.3ab

– Working mode: full-duplex (auto-sensing for 10 Mbit/s, 100 Mbit/s, 1000 Mbit/s, and 2.5 Gbit/s)

- Maximum transmission distance: 100 m

NOTE:

All 2.5 GE LAN interfaces can be used as WAN interfaces.

Ethernet cable
WAN interfaces: two 10GE optical interfaces 10GE optical interface: LC/PC

The 10GE optical interfaces (1/10 Gbit/s auto-sensing) can work in GE mode and can transmit and receive service traffic at 1 Gbit/s or 10 Gbit/s.

  • Optical interface attributes: The optical interface attributes vary according to the optical module used.
  • Standards compliance: IEEE 802.3ae
NOTE:

All 10GE WAN interfaces can be configured as LAN interfaces.

USB 2.0 port (Host): one USB 2.0 port USB 2.0 Type A

A USB 2.0 interface provides up to 480 Mbit/s upload and download rates.

  • Standards compliance: USB 2.0
  • Working mode: Host

USB 2.0-compliant USB flash drive

NOTE:

The AR does not support SSD USB flash drives.
LTE antenna interface SMA-K (screw threads outside and a hole inside)

LTE antenna interfaces of the device include a primary interface (MAIN) and a secondary interface (DIV). The primary and secondary interfaces work at the same time. The primary interface transmits and receives LTE signals, while the secondary interface only receives LTE signals. They work together to improve the downlink rate (1T2R).

Interface standards and supported frequency bands:

  • LTE FDD: B1/B2/B3/B4/B5/B7/B8/B18/B19/B20/B26/B28/B66
  • LTE TDD: B38/B40/B41
  • WCDMA: B1/B5/B8
  • GSM: GSM 850/GSM 900/GSM 1800

Supported rate:

  • LTE FDD: 50 Mbit/s in the uplink and 150 Mbit/s in the downlink
  • LTE TDD: 30 Mbit/s in the uplink and 130 Mbit/s in the downlink
  • DC-HSPA+: 5.76 Mbit/s in the uplink and 42 Mbit/s in the downlink
  • HSPA+: 5.76 Mbit/s in the uplink and 21.6 Mbit/s in the downlink
  • WCDMA PS: 384 kbit/s in the uplink and 384 kbit/s in the downlink
  • WCDMA CS: 64 kbit/s in the uplink and 64 kbit/s in the downlink
  • EDGE: 236.8 kbit/s in the uplink and 236.8 kbit/s in the downlink
  • GPRS: 85.6 kbit/s in the uplink and 85.6 kbit/s in the downlink
  • GSM: 9.6 kbit/s in the uplink and 14.4 kbit/s in the downlink
NOTE:

The preceding performance data is theoretical peak values of one-way transmission obtained in a specific test environment (length of test data packets: ≥ 1400 bytes) in Huawei labs. The actual performance data may vary depending on the network environment and purchased carrier package.

Whip antenna (delivered with the device by default)

LTE whip antenna (27011207)

LTE indoor remote antenna (27012152)
