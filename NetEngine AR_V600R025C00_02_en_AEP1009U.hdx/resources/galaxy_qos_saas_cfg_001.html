Overview of SaaS Traffic Steering

Definition

Software as a Service (SaaS) traffic steering is a link selection mechanism that enables a device to proactively detect the quality of SaaS links and then select the optimal link based on quality requirements.

Purpose

SaaS is a software delivery mode based on cloud technologies. Specifically, cloud technology providers develop and maintain cloud technology application software, and provide software to users through the Internet. As SaaS applications increase and grow in popularity, users gradually shift their focus from SaaS link connectivity to SaaS link availability, such as the delay and packet loss rate of links. These complex requirements pose challenges to traditional traffic steering solutions. Such solutions cannot be used to detect the SaaS link quality. Even if the quality of a SaaS link is poor, the link is still selected to forward packets as long as the route is reachable. As a result, services deteriorate or even become unavailable.

SaaS traffic steering can be deployed to proactively detect the quality of links between users and SaaS service providers and select the optimal link to forward service data based on service quality requirements, implementing intelligent traffic steering.
