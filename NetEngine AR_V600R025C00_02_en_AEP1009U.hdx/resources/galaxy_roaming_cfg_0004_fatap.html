Network Architecture of WLAN Roaming

As shown in Figure 1, a Fat AP and multiple Fit APs are deployed on a WLAN. The WAC module of the Fat AP manages the built-in AP and Fit APs. STAs can access the WLAN through the Fat AP and Fit APs. When the STA roams from the coverage area of a Fat AP to that of a Fit AP during mobile office, network services are not interrupted.
