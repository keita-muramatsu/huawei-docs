(Optional) Adjusting the 802.1p and DSCP Priorities of the Voice VLAN

Context

Voice services need higher real-time network performance than data services. You can adjust the 802.1p and DSCP priorities of the voice VLAN to enable the device to transmit voice packets in the voice VLAN with a higher priority. This will ensure a high transmission quality of voice services.

For details about the 802.1p and DSCP priorities, see "Re-marking" in the CLI Configuration Guide > QoS Configuration.

Procedure

  1. Enter the system view.

    system-view

  2. Configure the 802.1p and DSCP priorities of the voice VLAN.

    voice-vlan remark { 8021p 8021p-value | dscp dscp-value }*

    By default, the 802.1p priority and DSCP priority of a voice VLAN are 5 and 46, respectively.
