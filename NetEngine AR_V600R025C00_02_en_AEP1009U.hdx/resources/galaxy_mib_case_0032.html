Querying Packet Statistics on an Interface

Description of MIB Objects

You can use the following objects in ifTable to obtain statistics on incoming and outgoing packets on all interfaces, and then use ifIndex to query statistics on incoming and outgoing packets on an interface.

Object

OID

ifIndex

ifInUcastPkts

1.3.6.1.2.1.2.2.1.11

ifInNUcastPkts

1.3.6.1.2.1.2.2.1.12

ifInDiscards

1.3.6.1.2.1.2.2.1.13

ifInErrors

1.3.6.1.2.1.2.2.1.14

1.3.6.1.2.1.2.2.1.16

1.3.6.1.2.1.2.2.1.17

Procedure

  1. Search for an object in the MIB tree. However, it is difficult to locate an object if a large number of MIB files are imported to the MIB Browser. In this case, you can press Ctrl+F to locate an object.

  2. Perform the Walk operation on ifDescr to query the entity index ifIndex. As shown in Figure 1, the index of 10GE8/0/1 is 9.

    Figure 1 Obtaining the mapping between an interface and its index

  3. Perform the Walk operation on ifInOctets and ifOutOctets to query the total number of bytes of incoming and outgoing packets on the corresponding interface based on the value of ifIndex. As shown in Figure 2, the total numbers of bytes of incoming and outgoing packets on 10GE8/0/1 with the index of 9 are both 0.

    Figure 2 Total numbers of bytes of incoming and outgoing packets

  4. Perform the Walk operation on ifInUcastPkts and ifOutUcastPkts to query the number of unicast packets on the corresponding interface based on the value of ifIndex. As shown in Figure 3, the numbers of incoming and outgoing unicast packets on 10GE8/0/1 with the index of 9 are both 0.

    Figure 3 Numbers of incoming and outgoing unicast packets

  5. Perform the Walk operation on ifInNUcastPkts and ifOutNUcastPkts to query the number of non-unicast packets on the corresponding interface based on the value of ifIndex. As shown in Figure 4, the numbers of incoming and outgoing non-unicast packets on 10GE8/0/1 with the index of 9 are both 0.

    Figure 4 Numbers of incoming and outgoing non-unicast packets

  6. Perform the Walk operation on ifInDiscards and ifOutDiscards to query statistics on packets discarded on the corresponding interface based on the value of ifIndex. As shown in Figure 5, the numbers of incoming and outgoing packets discarded on 10GE8/0/1 with the index of 9 are both 0.

    Figure 5 Numbers of discarded incoming and outgoing packets

  7. Perform the Walk operation on ifInErrors and ifOutErrors to query the number of error packets on the corresponding interface based on the value of ifIndex. As shown in Figure 6, the numbers of incoming and outgoing error packets on 10GE8/0/1 with the index of 9 are both 0.

    Figure 6 Numbers of incoming and outgoing error packets
