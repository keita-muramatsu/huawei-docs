Clearing Hot Standby Statistics

Context

If the hot standby relationship fails to be established, session information fails to be backed up, or configuration commands fail to be backed up between the active and standby devices, you can check packet statistics on the backup channel. Pay attention to the data of received and sent packets of each module to determine the packet loss point. To obtain accurate statistics, you need to clear the existing statistics. After a certain period of time, you can view the statistics about HRP packets sent and received by the device.

Statistics cannot be restored after being deleted. Exercise caution when clearing the statistics.

Table 1 Clearing hot standby statistics

Operation

Command

Clear hot standby statistics.

reset hrp statistic

Display statistics about HRP backup packets.

display hrp statistic
