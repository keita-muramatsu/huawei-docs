(Optional) Adjusting the Priority of CAPWAP Management Packets

Context

To improve link reliability and prevent CAPWAP control tunnels from being terminated upon heavy service traffic, configure a high priority for CAPWAP management packets.

When there is heavy service traffic between links, you can increase the DSCP priority of CAPWAP management packets to ensure that CAPWAP management packets are preferentially processed, thereby improving the reliability of the CAPWAP control tunnels.

Procedure

  1. Enter the system view.

    system-view

  2. Configure the priority of CAPWAP management packets.

    capwap control-link-priority { local | remote } priority-value

    By default, the priority of CAPWAP management packets is 7.

    A larger priority value indicates a higher priority and higher link reliability. The default value is recommended.

    If CAPWAP management packets are sent from the WAC to APs, set the priority of the packets to a value in the range of 4 to 7 using the local priority-value command. This prevents the management tunnels from being terminated upon heavy service traffic.
