Device Panel

Context

The device panel area displays information about interfaces on the front panel, including the number of interfaces and status of each interface. When you move the mouse to an interface, the interface number and status are displayed.

Procedure

  1. Choose Monitoring > Overview. The device panel is displayed, as shown in Figure 1.

    Figure 1 Device panel information

  2. Click a service card on the panel to display the interface status area, as shown in Figure 2.

    Figure 2 Ports on the card
