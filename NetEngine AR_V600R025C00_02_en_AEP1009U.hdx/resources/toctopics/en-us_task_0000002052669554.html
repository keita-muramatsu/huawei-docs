Device Information

Procedure

  1. Choose Monitoring > Overview. The Device Information area is displayed, as shown in Figure 1.

    Figure 1 Device Information area

    To change the device name, click next to Device name.

    To access the System Upgrade page and upgrade the software or patch, click next to Software version or Patch version.
