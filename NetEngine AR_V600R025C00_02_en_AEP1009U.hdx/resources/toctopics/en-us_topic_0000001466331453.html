Installing a Router in a Cabinet Using Front Mounting Brackets (AR8700-8)

AR8700-8
  • Leave at least 50 mm of clearance around the router for heat dissipation.
  • Seen from the front panel of the router, the airflow of the router is from the left and front toward the rear.
  • If multiple routers are installed in a cabinet, it is recommended that at least 1 U of space (1 U = 44.45 mm) be kept between each two routers. These routers must have the same airflow direction; otherwise, heat dissipation is affected by hot air circulation.

Before installation, ensure that:

  • The cabinet is securely fixed.
  • A suitable position has been determined in the cabinet for the router.
  • The router to be installed is available and located near the cabinet for convenient movement.
  • Before installing the AR8700, you are advised to install L-shaped guide rails (separately purchased) in the cabinet to support the chassis.

Tools and Accessories

  • Phillips screwdriver (separately purchased)
  • Flat-head screwdriver (separately purchased)
  • Floating nuts (four, separately purchased)
  • Mounting brackets
  • L-shaped guide rails (separately purchased)
  • M4 screws (eight, used to secure mounting brackets to both sides of the router)
  • M5 screws (four, separately purchased, used to fix L-shaped guide rails on both sides of the cabinet)
  • M6 screws (four, separately purchased)

Procedure

  1. Use a Phillips screwdriver and M4 screws to attach the mounting brackets to both sides of the router.

    When installing mounting brackets on both sides of the front panel, install the one with the ground point on the side with the R mark.

    Figure 1 Installing mounting brackets

  2. Install floating nuts and L-shaped guide rails.

    a. Use a flat-head screwdriver to install two floating nuts on each of the front mounting rails. Leave four mounting holes between the two floating nuts on the same mounting rail.

    b. Use a Phillips screwdriver and M5 screws to secure the L-shaped guide rails to the left and right sides of the cabinet. Align the bottom of the L-shaped guide rails with the lower floating nuts on the mounting bars.
    • The length of three adjacent mounting holes may not be 1 U. Observe the scale ticks on the mounting rails when installing floating nuts.
    • The floating nuts on the rear mounting rails must be aligned with those on the front mounting rails.
    • You can use a flat-head screwdriver to install floating nuts.

  3. Install the router into the cabinet.

    a. Move the router into the cabinet, place the router on the L-shaped guide rails, and gently push the router into the cabinet until the mounting brackets are in full contact with the front mounting rails.

    b. Use M6 screws to fix the mounting brackets on the cabinet.
