AP List

Context

On this page, you can view AP information, including the AP ID, AP name, MAC address, AP group, IP address, AP type, number of online users, software version, serial number, RTU license status, and AP status. In addition, you can restart online APs one by one or in batches, or restart them all in one click, or delete them.

Searching and filtering APs

You can enter a keyword in the search box to filter APs. AP names, AP groups, IP addresses, and software versions can be used for fuzzy match.

Sorting APs

You can sort data in the table by column in ascending or descending order, so that you can find the information you need. By clicking the sort icon in the desired column, you can change the sorting mode as needed.

Procedure

  1. Choose Monitoring > AP. The AP List area is displayed, as shown in Figure 1.
