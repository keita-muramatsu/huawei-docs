(Optional) Installing a SIM Card

Context

  • The device supports Nano SIM cards. To prevent poor SIM card contact, you are advised not to use a SIM card adapter.
  • The SIM cards are not hot-swappable. After replugging a SIM card, you need to restart the RF module or restart the device.
  • Ensure that the SIM card slot is locked after a SIM card is removed or installed.

Table 1 describes the dimensions of a SIM card.

Tools

  • Phillips screwdriver
  • SIM card (prepared by the customer)

Procedure

  1. Use a Phillips screwdriver to loosen the screw on the SIM card slot cover, and remove the SIM card slot cover from the device. Keep the SIM card slot cover in an appropriate place for later use.

  2. Place the SIM card into the SIM card slot.

    • When installing a SIM card, determine its installation direction according to the silkscreen on the card slot.
    • Press the SIM card in place to lock it.
    • When removing a SIM card, push it inwards to eject it.

  3. Install the SIM card slot cover back on the device and tighten the screw to fix the cover.
