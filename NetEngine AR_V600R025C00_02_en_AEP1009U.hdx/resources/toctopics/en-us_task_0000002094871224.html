Device Quantity Statistics

Context

On this page, you can view the total number of devices on the current network, number of devices of each type, and number of devices to be approved.

Procedure

  1. Click Topology. The topology information page is displayed. In the lower right corner of the page, you can view the number of devices on the entire network, as shown in Figure 1.

    Figure 1 Device quantity statistics

    During device statistics collection, one stack is counted as one device.

  2. Click View Now to go to the Device Management page and view the devices to be approved.

    This step is required if there are devices to be approved on the network.
