Installing the Cord End Terminal

Procedure

  1. Hold a cord end terminal upright and place it on a terminal jack, as shown in Figure Placing a terminal on a terminal jack vertically. To ensure bump contact and dense connection, place the plain side of the terminal outwards.

    Figure 1 Placing a terminal on a terminal jack vertically

  2. Insert the terminal into the jack vertically, and turn the screw clockwise to fasten the terminal, as shown in Figure Feeding the terminal into the jack.

    Figure 2 Feeding the terminal into the jack
    • Ensure that the exposed section of the terminal is less than 2 mm (0.079 in.) in length.
    • Do not press the insulation of the terminal.
    • Insert only one terminal into one jack.

  3. Move the cable slightly and ensure that it is securely connected.
  4. Before you remove a cord end terminal, loosen the screw counterclockwise.
