(Optional) Configuring the MRRU

Context

The maximum receive reconstructed unit (MRRU) of an interface is the maximum size of MP packets reassembled by the receive end. It is applicable to the LCP negotiation phase of MP.

The MRRU is related to the maximum transmission unit (MTU) of the interface.

  • If the MRRU of the remote MP-Group interface is greater than or equal to the MTU of the local MP-Group interface, the local MP-Group interface keeps its MTU unchanged during LCP negotiation.
  • If the MRRU of the remote MP-Group interface is smaller than the MTU of the local MP-Group interface, the local MP-Group interface sets its MTU to the MRRU of the remote MP-Group interface during LCP negotiation.

Procedure

  1. Enter the system view.

    system-view

  2. Create an MP-Group interface and enter the MP-Group interface view.

    interface mp-group number

  3. Configure the MRRU of the interface.

    mrru mrru

    By default, the MRRU of an MP-Group interface is 1500 bytes.
