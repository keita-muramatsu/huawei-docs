(Optional) Configuring the v43 Carrier Band

Context

The v43 carrier band on a VDSL interface allows the local device to connect to and negotiate with a DSLAM. By default, carrier bands are enabled on a VDSL interface and the local device selects one through auto-negotiation with the DSLAM. If the DSLAM does not support the v43 carrier band, disable the v43 carrier band on the CPE.

Procedure

  1. Enter the system view.

    system-view

  2. Enter the VDSL interface view.

    interface Ethernet interface-number

  3. Enable or disable the v43 carrier band on the VDSL interface.

    vdsl band v43 { off | on }

    By default, the v43 carrier band is enabled on a VDSL interface.
