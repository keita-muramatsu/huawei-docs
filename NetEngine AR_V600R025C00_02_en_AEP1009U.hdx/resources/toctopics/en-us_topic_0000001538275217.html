Example for Configuring MPLS VPN

Applicable Products and Versions

This example applies to AR routers running V600R022C10 and later versions.

Overview

MPLS VPN can connect different branches of a VPN to form a unified network and isolate VPN services.

Networking Requirements

As shown in Figure 1, a company needs to use MPLS VPN to implement communication between the HQ and branch and isolate two types of services. The HQ is dual-homed to PE1 and PE2 through an MCE, and the branch is directly connected to PE3. To improve network reliability, VPN FRR can be configured to implement fast VPN service switching if a PE fails. The implementation is as follows:

  • The MCE at the HQ is dual-homed to PE1 and PE2. PE1 is preferentially selected to forward traffic and accesses services of VPN 1 and VPN 2 at the HQ.
  • PE3 accesses services of VPN 1 and VPN 2 at the branch. A backup next hop is configured on PE3 so that PE2 functions as the backup of PE1.
  • Users in the same VPN can communicate with each other, but users in different VPNs cannot.
Figure 1 Networking diagram

Configuration Roadmap

  1. Configure OSPF on the P, PE1, PE2, and PE3 for them to communicate on the MPLS backbone network.
  2. Configure basic MPLS capabilities and MPLS LDP to establish LDP LSPs on the MPLS backbone network.
  3. Configure VPN instances on the MCE and PEs (PE1, PE2, and PE3) and establish MP-IBGP peer relationships between the PEs.
  4. Configure OSPF multi-instance between the MCE and PE1 and IS-IS multi-instance between the MCE and PE2 to exchange VPN routing information.
  5. Enable VPN FRR on PE3.

Procedure

  1. Configure IP addresses for interfaces on the P, PE1, PE2, and PE3.

    <HUAWEI> system-view
