Contacting Huawei Technical Support

If you find it difficult to determine or solve the problem during maintenance or troubleshooting by referring to related documents, you can collect the fault information and then contact the technical support.

Contacting Technical Support

For enterprise customer:

  • Log in to Global Service Hotline to obtain the Huawei global service hotline or Email address.

  • Visit Huawei technical support website https://support.huawei.com/enterprise to search for troubleshooting cases or post your questions on BBS.
