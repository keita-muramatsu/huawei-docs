Installing a Router in a Cabinet Using Mounting Brackets and L-shaped Guide Rails

AR6710-H4T4X2Y7
  • Leave at least 50 mm of clearance around the router for heat dissipation.
  • Seen from the front panel of the router, the airflow of the router is left to right.
  • If multiple routers are installed in a cabinet, it is recommended that at least 1 U of space (1 U = 44.45 mm) be kept between each two routers. These routers must have the same airflow direction; otherwise, heat dissipation is affected by hot air circulation.
  • It is recommended that cables be routed above or below the AR6710-H4T4X2Y7 and be kept away from pluggable modules on both sides, facilitating future maintenance.

Before installation, ensure that:

  • The cabinet is securely fixed.
  • A suitable position has been determined in the cabinet for the router.
  • The router to be installed is available and located near the cabinet for convenient movement.

Tools and Accessories

  • Phillips screwdriver
  • Flat-head screwdriver
  • Floating nuts
  • Mounting brackets
  • M4 screws
  • M6 screws
  • L-shaped guide rails (separately purchased)

Procedure

  1. Use a Phillips screwdriver and M4 screws to attach the mounting brackets to both sides of the router.

    The mounting brackets can be installed on both sides close to the front panel or rear panel.

  2. Mark the positions of L-shaped guide rails and floating nuts.

  3. Install two floating nuts on each of the front mounting rails. Leave a mounting hole between the two floating nuts on the same mounting rail.

    • The length of three adjacent mounting holes may not be 1 U. Observe the scale ticks on the mounting rails when installing floating nuts.
    • You can use a flat-head screwdriver to install floating nuts.

  4. Install the floating nuts and L-shaped guide rails.

    The methods for installing floating nuts and L-shaped guide rails in the cabinet are the same. The positions of floating nuts and L-shaped guide rails vary with routers.

    The L-shaped guide rails will affect heat dissipation and must be installed facing downwards. For details, see the figure below.

    a. Use a flat-head screwdriver to install two floating nuts on each front mounting rail. Ensure that the floating nuts on the left and right are on the same horizontal plane.

    b. Use the Phillips screwdriver to fix the L-shaped guide rails in the marked positions with M5 screws. The bottom edges of the L-shaped guide rails must be in line with the bottom edges of the marked mounting holes.

  5. Install the router into the cabinet.

    a. Move the router into the cabinet, place the router on the L-shaped guide rails, and gently push the router into the cabinet until the mounting brackets are in full contact with the front mounting rails.

    b. Use M6 screws to fix the mounting brackets on the cabinet.
