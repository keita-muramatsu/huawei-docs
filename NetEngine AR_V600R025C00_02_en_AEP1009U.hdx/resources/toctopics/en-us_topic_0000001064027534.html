Installing an MPO Connector

Procedure

  1. Remove the dustproof cap of the MPO fiber connector and store it for future use.
  2. Align the core pin of the male connector with that of the female connector, as shown in Figure Aligning the male connector with the female connector.

    Figure 1 Aligning the male connector with the female connector

  3. Hold the shell labeled "PUSH" and feed the male connector into the female connector until you hear a clicking sound. The male and female connectors are securely installed, as shown in Figure Installed MPO fiber connector.

    Figure 2 Installed MPO fiber connector

  4. To disassemble an MPO fiber connector, hold the shell labeled "PULL" and remove the male connector, as shown in Figure Disassembling an MPO fiber connector.

    Figure 3 Disassembling an MPO fiber connector
