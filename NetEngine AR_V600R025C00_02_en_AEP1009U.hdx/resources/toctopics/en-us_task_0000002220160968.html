Entire-Network Topology Collection

Context

One-click entire-network topology collection enables you to collect information about devices, interfaces, and neighbors on the network and recalculate the topology. After the topology is collected, you can choose Topology > Network Topology to view the new topology.

Procedure

  1. Choose Entire-Network > System Settings. The System Settings page is displayed, as shown in Figure 1.

    Figure 1 System Settings page

  2. Click Collect next to Topology Re-collection. In the dialog box that is displayed, click OK.
