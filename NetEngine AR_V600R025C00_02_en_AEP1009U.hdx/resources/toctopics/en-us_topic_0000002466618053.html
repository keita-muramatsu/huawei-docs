What Are the Differences Between a Subscriber Queue (SQ) and a Flow Queue (FQ) Configured in a Traffic Behavior of a CBQ Policy?

When an SQ (configured using the user-queue command) is configured in a traffic behavior, you can configure the CIR (specified by the cir parameter) and PIR (specified by the pir parameter) to enable PQ queues to preempt idle bandwidth. When an FQ (configured using the flow-queue or queue command) is configured in a traffic behavior, the preceding function cannot be implemented, and only shaping can be configured to limit the bandwidth. In addition, the specifications of SQs are lower than those of FQs. Therefore, you can select them based on the number of FQs supported by the device at a site. In normal cases, the specifications of SQs can meet project requirements. Therefore, you are advised to preferentially select SQs when using a CBQ policy.
