Assembling an Unshielded RJ45 Connector and Ethernet Cable

Context

Figure Components of an unshielded RJ45 connector and cable shows the components of an unshielded RJ45 connector and cable.

Figure 1 Components of an unshielded RJ45 connector and cable

A. Plug of connector

B. Jacket

C. Twisted-pair wires

-

Procedure

  1. Remove a 16-mm (0.63 in.) long section of the jacket, as shown in Figure Removing the jacket of a twisted-pair cable (unit: mm (in.)).

    When you remove the shield layer, do not damage the insulation of the twisted-pair cable.

    Figure 2 Removing the jacket of a twisted-pair cable (unit: mm (in.))

  2. Align the four pairs of wires and cut the ends neatly, as shown in Figure Connections between wires and pins (unit: mm (in.)). The connections between the wires and the pins are listed in Table Connections between wires and pins (using a straight-through cable as an example).

    Figure 3 Connections between wires and pins (unit: mm (in.))
    Table 1 Connections between wires and pins (using a straight-through cable as an example)

    Matching Pins of Wires

    Wire Color

    1

    White-Orange

    2

    Orange

    3

    White-Green

    4

    Blue

    5

    White-Blue

    6

    Green

    7

    White-Brown

    8

    Brown

  3. Feed the cable into the plug, and crimp the connector, as shown in Figure Crimping the connector.

    When inserting the cable, check from the side or bore of the plug to ensure that the cable is completely seated in the plug.

    Figure 4 Crimping the connector

  4. To complete the assembly of the other end, repeat steps 1 to 3.
