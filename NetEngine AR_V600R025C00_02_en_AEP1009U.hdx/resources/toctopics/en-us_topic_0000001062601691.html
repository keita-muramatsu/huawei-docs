Preparing Installation Tools

Table Installation tools lists installation tools to be prepared before router installation.

Table 1 Installation tools

Tool

Function

Picture

Measuring tape

Measures distances.

ESD wrist strap

Prevents ESD damages when you touch or operate the device or components.

Utility knife

Cuts cartons or paper.

Flat-head screwdriver

Tightens or loosens slotted screws.

Phillips screwdriver

Tightens or loosens cross recessed screws.

Marker

Draws lines and marks labels.

Diagonal pliers

Cut insulation tubes and cable ties.

Multimeter

Tests cabinet insulation, cable connectivity, and device electrical performance indicators including voltage, current, and resistance.

Claw hammer

Used to knock or shape a workpiece, or extract a nail.

Hammer drill

Drills installation holes on a wall.
