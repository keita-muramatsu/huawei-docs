Installing an LTE Whip Antenna

Context

  • Do not power on the router before you finish connecting cables.
  • When connecting cables, pay attention to port identifiers to ensure correct cable connections. Incorrect cable connections may cause damage to the port module or the router.

Whip antennas are included in the installation accessory package.

Tools and Accessories

  • Whip antennas

Procedure

  1. Connect the whip antennas to the SMA connectors on the front panel of the router.

  2. Adjust positions of the antennas to better transmit and receive radio signals. If the router is mounted on a desk, make the whip antennas vertical to the desk.
