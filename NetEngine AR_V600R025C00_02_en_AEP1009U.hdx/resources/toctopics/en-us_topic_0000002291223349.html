Overview of Packet Replication

Definition

Packet replication is a packet loss concealment technology. It enables the transmitting device to replicate packets and send both the original and replicated packets over two respective links with the best quality. If packet loss occurs on one link, the receiving device can still reconstruct the complete flow using packets received from the other link without requiring retransmission, ensuring service continuity.

Purpose

The packet replication function applies to services that have high reliability requirements and a small amount of traffic, such as voice services.
