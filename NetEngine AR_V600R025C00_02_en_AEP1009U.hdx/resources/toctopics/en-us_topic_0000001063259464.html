  • Leave at least 50 mm of clearance around the router for heat dissipation.
  • Seen from the front panel of the router, the airflow of the router is left to right.
  • If multiple routers are installed in a cabinet, it is recommended that at least 1 U of space (1 U = 44.45 mm) be kept between each two routers. These routers must have the same airflow direction; otherwise, heat dissipation is affected by hot air circulation.

Before installation, ensure that:

  • The cabinet is securely fixed.
  • A suitable position has been determined in the cabinet for the router.
  • The router to be installed is available and located near the cabinet for convenient movement.

Tools and Accessories

  • Phillips screwdriver
  • Flat-head screwdriver
  • Floating nuts
  • Front mounting brackets
  • Rear mounting brackets (including guide rails)
  • M4 screws
  • M6 screws

    The front mounting brackets, rear mounting brackets (together with guide rails), and M4 screws are included in the installation accessory package. All other accessories are excluded and need to be purchased separately. Procedure

  1. Use a Phillips screwdriver and M4 screws to attach the mounting brackets to both sides of the router.

    The mounting brackets can be installed on both sides close to the front panel or rear panel.

    Figure 4 Installing front mounting brackets
    Figure 5 Installing rear mounting brackets

  2. Install two floating nuts on each of the front and rear mounting rails. Leave a mounting hole between the two floating nuts on the same mounting rail.

    • The length of three adjacent mounting holes may not be 1 U. Observe the scale ticks on the mounting rails when installing floating nuts.
    • The floating nuts on the rear mounting rails must be aligned with those on the front mounting rails.
    • You can use a flat-head screwdriver to install floating nuts.

  3. Attach the mounting bracket guide rails on the rear mounting rails.

  4. Install the router into the cabinet.

    1. Move the router into the cabinet, align the rear mounting brackets with the guide rails, and gently push the router into the cabinet until the front mounting brackets are completely attached to the front mounting rails.
    2. Use M6 screws to attach the front mounting brackets on the cabinet.
