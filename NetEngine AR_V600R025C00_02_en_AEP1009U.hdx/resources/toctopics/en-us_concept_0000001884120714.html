Overview of L2TP

Definition

The Layer 2 Tunneling Protocol (L2TP) is a type of virtual private dial-up network (VPDN) tunneling protocol. It extends the application of the Point-to-Point Protocol (PPP) and is an important VPN technology used to quickly establish connections between branches and the headquarters (HQ).

Purpose

As enterprises grow and their services increase, branches in different regions need to establish fast, secure, and reliable network connections with the HQ. Traditional dial-up networks need to rent telephone lines from Internet service providers (ISPs) and apply for public dial strings or IP addresses from the ISPs. This results in high costs, and fails to provide remote users with the convenient access services they need. With the emergence of L2TP, PPP packets can be transmitted over L2TP tunnels on the Internet to solve the preceding problems. L2TP can provide remote access services regardless of whether branches access the Internet through traditional dial-up or Ethernet.
