Enable WebMaster

Context

When the WebMaster function is enabled on a device, the device is configured as the master device on the campus network. Then, you can log in to WebMaster to centrally configure and manage the entire campus network.

Procedure

  1. Choose Config > Enable WebMaster. The Enable WebMaster page is displayed, as shown in Figure 1.

    Figure 1 Enabling the WebMaster function

  2. Toggle on Enable WebMaster to enable the WebMaster function.

    If the PC is connected to a service interface of the device, click Entire Network Management to go to the WebMaster operation page.

    If the PC is connected to the management interface of the device, connect the PC to a service interface of the device and log in to WebMaster.

Follow-up Procedure

After the WebMaster function is enabled, the device functions as the master device on the campus network. You can log in to WebMaster to perform configurations. For details, see the WebMaster Configuration Guide.
