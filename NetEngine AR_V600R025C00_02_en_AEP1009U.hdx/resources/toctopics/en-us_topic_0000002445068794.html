Installing 5G Indoor Remote Antennas for a 5G Card

Context

5G indoor remote antennas are applicable to the NRGL-V2 card (part number: 0302086941).

  • Do not power on the router before you finish connecting cables.
  • When connecting cables, pay attention to port identifiers to ensure that cables are connected correctly. Incorrectly connected cables may damage port modules or the device.
  • Do not place 5G indoor remote antennas on the device.

Tools and Accessories

  • 5G indoor remote antennas
  • RF extension cables (optional, separately purchased)

    5G indoor remote antennas are delivered with the device, but RF extension cables are not included in the installation accessory package and need to be purchased separately.

    • By default, 5G indoor remote antennas (part number: 27013849) are configured for 5G cards. RF extension cables (part number: 04133554) are optional based on actual requirements, and the card-to-extension cable ratio is 1:8.
    • For details about the requirements for the connectors of the RF jumpers and RF extension cables, see 5G Indoor Remote Antenna.

Procedure

  1. (Optional) Connect the SMB-K connectors of the RF extension cables to the SMB-J connectors (ANT0 to ANT7) on the 5G card.

  2. Connect the SMB-K connectors of the 5G indoor remote antennas to the SMB-J connectors of the RF extension cables.

  3. Secure the 5G indoor remote antennas to magnetizable metal surfaces.
