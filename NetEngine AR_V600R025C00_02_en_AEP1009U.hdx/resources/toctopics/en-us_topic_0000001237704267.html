(Optional) Installing the SIM Card on a SIC Card

Context

This method applies to SIC cards with 3G/LTE/5G functions. The methods for installing the SIM card on a SIC card are the same.

  • To prevent poor SIM card contact, you are advised not to use a SIM card adapter.
  • The SIM cards are not hot-swappable. After replugging the SIM card, you need to restart the RF module or restart the device.
  • Ensure that the SIM card slot is locked after the SIM card is removed or installed.

Tools

  • Phillips screwdriver
  • ESD wrist strap
  • SIM card (self-provided)

Procedure

  1. Wear an ESD wrist strap. Ensure that the ESD wrist strap is grounded and in a close contact with your wrist.
  2. Loosen the screw on the SIM card holder.

  3. Slide the SIM card holder horizontally towards the direction specified by the OPEN arrow and open the SIM card holder.

  4. Insert a SIM card into the SIM card holder.

    When inserting a SIM card, keep the notch on the SIM card in the same direction as the notch marked on the slot.

  5. Slide the SIM card holder towards the direction specified by the LOCK arrow, close the SIM card holder, and tighten the screw.
