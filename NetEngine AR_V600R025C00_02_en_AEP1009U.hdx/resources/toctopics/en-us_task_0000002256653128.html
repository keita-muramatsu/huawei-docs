Configuring Packet Replication

Prerequisites

  • You have completed SD-WAN EVPN configurations, and SD-WAN EVPN site connections have been established. For details about SD-WAN EVPN configurations, see "SD-WAN EVPN Configuration" in "VPN Configuration."
  • You have completed smart policy routing (SPR) configurations, and multiple links are available for traffic steering. For details about SPR configurations, see "Smart Policy Routing Configuration" in "QoS Configuration."

Procedure

  1. Enter the system view.

    system-view

  2. Enter the SPR configuration view.

    smart-policy-route

  3. Enter the policy profile view.

    spr-policy node node-index

  4. Set the traffic steering mode of the SPR policy to path-prefer.

    routing-mode path-prefer

  5. Enable the packet replication function.

    replication enable

    By default, the packet replication function is disabled.
