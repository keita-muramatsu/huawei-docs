How Is the Bandwidth Allocated to the Default Queue in HQoS?

The default queue in HQoS is of the WDRR type, its weight is 1, and its bandwidth upper limit is 100%.

For example, if a PQ queue with the bandwidth of 20% and a WDRR queue with the weight of 30 are configured in an HQoS policy, the 20% bandwidth is first allocated to the PQ queue, and then the remaining 80% bandwidth is scheduled between the WDRR queue and the default queue at a ratio of 30:1 in the final scheduling result.
