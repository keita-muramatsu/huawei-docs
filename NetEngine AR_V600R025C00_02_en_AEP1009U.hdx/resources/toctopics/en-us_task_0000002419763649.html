(Optional) Configuring the Decapsulation Mode of SD-WAN Tunnels

Context

All encapsulation information is decapsulated when IPsec tunnel packets are decapsulated. As a result, decrypted packets cannot be forwarded to the corresponding interface through the TNP interface. You can configure the device to check routes for service traffic and retain the inner IPsec encapsulation to ensure that the packets are correctly routed to the next-hop TNP.

Procedure

  1. Enter the system view.

    system-view

  2. Configure the forwarding mode of encrypted packets.

    ipsec-p2mp-policy encrypted-packets { decryption-precedence | routing-precedence }

    By default, the forwarding mode of encrypted packets is the decryption-first mode.

    The route-first mode can effectively solve the problem of packet loss in outloop link scenarios, but affects the service traffic forwarding performance of the device. Therefore, the decryption-first mode is recommended in non-outloop link scenarios.
