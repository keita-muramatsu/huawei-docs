MP Negotiation Process

Certain MP options, such as the maximum receive reconstructed unit (MRRU) and endpoint discriminator (ED), are determined through LCP negotiation.

MP negotiation involves:

  • LCP negotiation: The two devices negotiate LCP parameters and check whether the other is working in MP mode. If they work in different working modes, LCP negotiation fails. In this case, the common NCP negotiation of PPP is performed, rather than MP bundling.
  • NCP negotiation: The two devices perform NCP negotiation by using only NCP parameters (such as IP addresses) of the MP-Group interfaces (logical interfaces in MP); they do not use the NCP parameters of physical interfaces.

If NCP negotiation succeeds, an MP link is established.
