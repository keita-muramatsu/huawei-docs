Overview of FEC

Definition

Forward Error Correction (FEC) is a technique that adds redundancy to transmitted data packets so that the receive end can recover data packets lost during transmission.

Purpose

Real-time applications, such as video conferencing and live streaming, have stringent requirements on latency and packet loss. If packet loss occurs on the network, frame freezing and artifacts may occur in video applications, severely affecting user experience. In most cases, a packet loss rate of 1% is noticeable to users, but a rate of 10% will make applications almost unusable. FEC optimizes packet transmission.
