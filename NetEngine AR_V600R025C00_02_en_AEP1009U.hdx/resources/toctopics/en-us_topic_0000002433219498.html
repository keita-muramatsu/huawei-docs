How Do I Calculate the Bandwidth of a Tunnel or Dialer Interface?

Tunnel and dialer interfaces are virtual logical interfaces and cannot detect the actual bandwidth of physical interfaces. If the qos lr command is not configured, the bandwidth is limited to 1 Gbit/s by default.
