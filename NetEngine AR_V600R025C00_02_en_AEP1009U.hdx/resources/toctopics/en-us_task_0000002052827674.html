Device Status

Context

You can view the real-time status of a device in the Device Status area.

Procedure

  1. Choose Monitoring > Overview. The Device Status area is displayed, as shown in Figure 1.

    Figure 1 Device Status area

  2. Click CPU usage, Memory usage, or Temperature to view detailed status information, as shown in Figure 2.

    Figure 2 Detailed status information

    You can click to view detailed information about another status.
