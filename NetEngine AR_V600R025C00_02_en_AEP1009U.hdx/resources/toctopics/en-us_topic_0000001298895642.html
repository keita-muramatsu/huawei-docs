Installing a Router on a Wall Through Mounting Holes

Context

Table 1 lists the products to which the installation mode is applicable.

Table 1 Products that can be installed through mounting holes

Series

Model

AR5700

AR5710-H8T2TS1, AR5710-H8T2TS1-T

Before drilling holes in the wall, make sure there are no power cables in the wall to avoid electrical hazards.

  • Take water-proofing measures to protect a router from damage caused by water.
  • Ensure that there are no flammable or explosive materials near the router and no obstructions within 100 mm around the router.
  • Screws must be installed firmly in the wall; otherwise, the router may fall down after cables are connected.

Tools and Accessories

  • Measuring tape
  • Marker
  • Hammer drill
  • Claw hammer
  • Phillips screwdriver
  • Wall anchors and mounting screws (separately purchased)

Procedure

  1. Use a measuring tape to determine the two mounting bracket installation positions. Keep the two positions on a horizontal line and mark the positions with a marker.

  2. Drill holes and install mounting screws. Choose an appropriate drill bit according to the diameter of the mounting screws. The diameter of the mounting screws cannot exceed 4 mm. Hammer the plastic wall anchors into the mounting holes. Use a Phillips screwdriver to screw the mounting screws into the wall anchors. Leave the screws protruding 2 mm from the wall.

  3. Align the mounting holes on the rear of the router with mounting screws and hang the router on the mounting screws.
