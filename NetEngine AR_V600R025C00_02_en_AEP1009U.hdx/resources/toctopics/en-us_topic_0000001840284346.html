Installing 5G Indoor Remote Antennas for a 5G Router

Context

5G indoor remote antennas are applicable to the AR5710-H2T4N2X-NRGL, AR5710-H2T4PN2X-NRGL, AR5710-S8P2XE-NRGL, AR5710-S8T1XWE-NRGL, and AR5710-S8T2XE-NRGL.

  • Do not power on the router before you finish connecting cables.
  • When connecting cables, pay attention to port identifiers to ensure that cables are connected correctly. Incorrectly connected cables may damage port modules or the device.
  • Do not place 5G indoor remote antennas on the device.

Tools and Accessories

  • 5G indoor remote antennas
  • RF jumpers
  • RF extension cables (optional)

    5G indoor remote antennas, RF jumpers, and RF extension cables are not included in the installation accessory package and need to be purchased separately.

    • When 5G indoor remote antennas (part number: 27013849) are installed for a 5G router:
      • The device-to-antenna ratio is 1:2.
      • RF jumpers (part number: 04133435) are mandatory, and the device-to-jumper ratio is 1:8.
      • RF extension cables (part number: 04133554) are optional based on actual requirements, and the device-to-extension cable ratio is 1:8.
    • For details about the requirements for the connectors of the RF jumpers and RF extension cables, see 5G Indoor Remote Antenna.

Procedure

  1. Connect the male connectors (SMA-J) of the RF jumpers to the SMA-K connectors (ANT0 to ANT7) on the router and securely tighten each connector using an 8-mm SMA wrench. Afterward, gently wiggle each one. If any is loose, tighten the connector bolt.

  2. (Optional) Connect the SMB-K connectors of the RF extension cables to the SMB-J connectors of the RF jumpers.

  3. Connect the SMB-K connectors of the 5G indoor remote antennas to the SMB-J connectors of the RF extension cables.

  4. Secure the 5G indoor remote antennas to magnetizable metal surfaces.
