Powering On and Powering Off the Router

Tools and Accessories

  • ESD wrist strap or ESD gloves
  • Multimeter

Procedure

  1. Before powering on the router, perform the following checks:
    • Use a multimeter to check that there is no short-circuit condition between the phase wire (live wire), ground wire, and neutral wire in each power socket.
    • Use a multimeter to verify that the external power input voltage is within the operating range specified in the Hardware Description.
    • Confirm that the switch of the external power supply system connected to the device is in the OFF position. (If the power module has no switch, skip this step.)
    • Verify that power cables are connected correctly.
  2. Turn on the switch of the external power supply system to supply power to the router.
  3. After powering on the router, check the front-panel indicators to determine whether the router is operating normally. Refer to the Hardware Description for indicator status details.

Follow-up Procedure

To power off the device, perform the following steps:

  • Powering off the device will interrupt all the services of the router. Exercise caution when performing this operation.
  • You are advised to save and back up the device configuration before powering off the device. If the current configuration is not saved before the device is powered off, the device will restore the last saved configuration upon startup.
  • For routers with independent power module switches, turn off these switches before disconnecting power to protect the power modules. For devices without external power modules, power can be disconnected directly.
  • Unless required for special operations, frequently powering off the device is not recommended. In the absence of identified issues, prolonged operation will not lead to performance degradation, nor will rebooting the device improve speed or resolve forwarding issues. On the contrary, maintaining a stable power supply will help extend the device's lifespan and ensure its reliability.
  1. Save the device configuration file.
  2. Turn off the external power supply system connected to the device.
  3. Verify that the device is powered off.
