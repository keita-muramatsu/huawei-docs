Installing Power Supplies

Context

The power module of the router needs to be purchased separately. Prepare a power module and install it by following the instructions provided in this document.

  • AC and DC power modules cannot be used together in the same router.
  • A power module can be hot swapped.
  • Vacant slots in the router must be covered with filler panels.

Tools and Accessories

  • ESD wrist strap

Procedure

  1. Wear an ESD wrist strap. Ensure that the ESD wrist strap is grounded and in a close contact with your wrist.
  2. Install the power module on the router.

    1. Pull the handle of the filler panel in the slot for the power module and remove the filler panel.
    2. Push the power module horizontally into the power module slot PWR1 or PWR2 on the router until the panel of the power module is in close contact with the router.
