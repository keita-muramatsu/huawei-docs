Can a QoS Policy Be Configured Based on the Bandwidth of Multiple Links?

For example, if the bandwidth of MPLS1 is 100 Mbit/s and that of MPLS2 is 50 Mbit/s, can a policy be configured based on the total bandwidth of 150 Mbit/s?

Currently, QoS traffic scheduling can be performed only on a per-link basis. When the same policy is applied to MPLS1 and MPLS2, traffic scheduling is performed separately on the two links. Traffic cannot be scheduled based on the total bandwidth of the two links.
