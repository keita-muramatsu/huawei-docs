AP Group Management

Context

You can add multiple APs managed by a WAC to an AP group and perform the same configuration for these APs at a time. By default, the device has an AP group named default, and APs are added to this group.

This function can be configured only when a WAC running V600 is deployed on the network.

Procedure

  • Creating an AP group
    1. Choose Wireless > AP Group Management. The AP Group Management page is displayed, as shown in Figure 1.

      Figure 1 AP Group Management page

    2. Click Create. The Create dialog box is displayed.
    3. Enter the AP group name.

      If the name of a new AP group is the same as that of an existing AP group, only the existing AP group is displayed in the AP group list after the AP group is created.

    4. Click OK.
  • Deleting an AP group

    An AP group that contains APs cannot be deleted.

    1. Choose Wireless > AP Group Management. The AP Group Management page is displayed, as shown in Figure 1.
    2. Select the AP group to be deleted and click Delete or . The system asks you to confirm the deletion.
    3. Click OK.
  • Changing the AP group

    If you move an AP from one AP group to another, the AP will restart, interrupting services. Therefore, exercise caution when performing this operation.

    1. Choose Wireless > AP Group Management. The AP Group Management page is displayed, as shown in Figure 1.
    2. Click next to an AP group name to expand the list of all APs in the AP group.
    3. Select the AP whose AP group needs to be changed and click Change AP Group or .
    4. In the Change AP Group dialog box that is displayed, enter the name of the new AP group.
    5. Click OK. In the displayed dialog box, click OK.
