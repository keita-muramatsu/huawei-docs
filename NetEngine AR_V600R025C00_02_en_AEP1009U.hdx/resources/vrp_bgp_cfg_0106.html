Understanding BGP Next Hop Recursion Based on a Route-Policy

When a BGP route changes and its next hop is an indirect one, the BGP route must be recursed once again. If no route-policy is configured for a recursive route, the BGP route may be recursed to an incorrect forwarding path, leading to traffic loss. To address this problem, configure BGP next hop recursion based on a route-policy. If a recursive route does not match a specified route-policy, the BGP route is marked as unreachable, preventing traffic loss.
