Deleting APs

Context

To disconnect APs from a WAC or enable APs to go online on another WAC, you can delete the APs from the current WAC.

Deleting APs will interrupt services of STAs connected to the APs. Therefore, exercise caution when you perform this operation.

Procedure

  • Run the undo ap { ap-mac ap-mac | ap-group ap-group | ap-id ap-id | all } command in the WLAN view to delete APs.
