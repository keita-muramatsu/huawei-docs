Understanding AP-Offline Service Continuity

In a WAC + Fit AP network architecture, management packets between the WAC and APs are forwarded through CAPWAP tunnels. When an AP goes offline due to a CAPWAP link disconnection, online STAs on the AP are brought offline and new STAs cannot access the AP.

  • In tunnel forwarding mode, management packets and service packets are transmitted through CAPWAP tunnels. This means that service packets of STAs are interrupted, which cannot be restored.
  • In direct forwarding mode, service packets are not transmitted through CAPWAP tunnels. As such, you can configure a survival policy to enable the AP to continue providing WLAN services. In this manner, services of online STAs are not interrupted and new STAs are allowed to access the AP.

Ensuring Service Continuity for Online STAs on an Offline AP

After the AP-offline service continuity function is enabled, an AP can still forward data packets upon a CAPWAP link disconnection, ensuring nonstop data service transmission for STAs in direct forwarding mode. This reduces loss for users and improves service reliability.

As shown in Figure 1, service packets of a STA are forwarded to the Internet through DeviceA without passing through the WAC regardless of whether the CAPWAP link is disconnected. Therefore, the CAPWAP link disconnection has no impact on data services of the STA.

Allowing New STAs to Access an Offline AP

The AP-offline service continuity function takes effect only for online STAs on an offline AP but not for new STAs attempting to access the AP. You can enable the function of allowing access of new STAs so that the AP allows new STAs to go online to access all network resources that are available before the AP goes offline.

In Figure 2, before the function of allowing access of new STAs on an offline AP is enabled, association and key negotiation are performed between the WAC and STA. After this function is enabled, STA authentication, association, and key negotiation are performed between the AP and STA.

Figure 2 Network diagram of allowing new STAs to access an offline AP

On an agile distributed WLAN, for central APs running V200R019C00 or earlier, AP-offline service continuity and the function of allowing new STAs to access an offline AP take effect only when the CAPWAP link between the WAC and central AP is disconnected, but not when the CAPWAP link between the central AP and RU is disconnected. For central APs running V200R021C00 or later, these functions take effect only when the CAPWAP link between the WAC and RU is disconnected, but not when the CAPWAP link between the WAC and central AP is disconnected.
