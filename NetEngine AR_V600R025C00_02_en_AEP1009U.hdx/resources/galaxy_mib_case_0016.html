Querying the Power of Cards and Power Modules

Description of MIB Objects

You can use hwBoardCurrentPower and hwBoardRatedPower in hwBoardPowerMngtTable to obtain the real-time power and rated power of a card or power module.

Object

OID

entPhysicalName

1.3.6.1.2.1.47.1.1.1.1.7

entPhysicalClass

1.3.6.1.2.1.47.1.1.1.1.5

hwBoardCurrentPower

1.3.6.1.4.1.2011.6.157.2.1.1.4

hwBoardRatedPower

1.3.6.1.4.1.2011.6.157.2.1.1.5

Procedure

  1. Search for an object in the MIB tree. However, it is difficult to locate an object if a large number of MIB files are imported to the MIB Browser. In this case, you can press Ctrl+F to locate an object.
  2. Perform the SNMP Walk operation on entPhysicalClass to obtain the indexes and types of all entities. Find the entities of the type 9 or 6 from the Walk operation results. The value 9 indicates that the entity type is card, and the value 6 indicates that the entity type is power module.
    Figure 1 Querying the entity type through entPhysicalClass

    As shown in Figure 1, the value 17104897 indicates the entity index, and the value 9 indicates the entity type.

  3. Perform the SNMP Get operation on hwBoardCurrentPower and hwBoardRatedPower based on card or power module indexes to obtain the real-time power and rated power of these cards or power modules.
    Figure 2 Querying the real-time power of a card or power module based on the device index and hwBoardCurrentPower
    Figure 3 Querying the rated power of a card or power module based on the device index and hwBoardRatedPower

    As shown in Figure 2 and Figure 3, the real-time power and rated power of the card with the index 17104897 are 95000 and 150000, respectively, in mW.
