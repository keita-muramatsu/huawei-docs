LAN interfaces: eight GE electrical interfaces RJ45

A GE electrical interface (10/100/1000 Mbit/s auto-sensing) transmits and receives services at 10 Mbit/s, 100 Mbit/s, or 1000 Mbit/s.

  • Standards compliance: IEEE802.3, IEEE802.3u, IEEE802.3ab

  • Maximum transmission distance: 100 m
NOTE:

All GE LAN interfaces can be configured as WAN interfaces.

Ethernet cable
WAN interface: one 10GE optical/2.5GE electrical combo interface

2.5GE electrical interface: RJ45

10GE optical interface: LC/PC

A combo interface consists of an optical Ethernet interface and an electrical Ethernet interface on the panel. The two interfaces have only one internal forwarding interface. The electrical and optical interfaces are multiplexed, and only either of them can work at a time. When either of the Ethernet interfaces is working, the other interface is shut down.

  • The 10GE optical interface (1/10 Gbit/s auto-sensing) transmits and receives service traffic at 1 Gbit/s or 10 Gbit/s.
  • The 2.5GE electrical interface (100 Mbit/s/1000 Mbit/s/2.5 Gbit/s auto-sensing) transmits and receives service traffic at 100 Mbit/s, 1000 Mbit/s, or 2.5 Gbit/s.
NOTE:
  • By default, an interface works as a 2.5GE electrical interface. You can run the port combination-mode 10GE interface MultiGE0/0/0 command in the system view to configure the corresponding interface to work as a 10GE optical interface based on the actual networking requirements.
  • All 10GE/2.5GE WAN interfaces can be configured as LAN interfaces.

Eight 5G/LTE antenna interfaces SMA-K (screw threads outside and a hole inside)

5G NR:

  • 3GPP Rel-16 is supported.
  • Uplink 256QAM and downlink 256QAM are supported.
  • Downlink 4×4 MIMO is supported on the 5G bands N1, N3, N7, N38, N40, N41, N77, N78, and N79.
  • Downlink 2×2 MIMO is supported on the 5G bands N5, N8, N20, N28, and N75.
  • Uplink 2×2 MIMO is supported on the 5G bands N1, N38, N40, N41, N77, N78, and N79.
  • Both 15 kHz and 30 kHz Subcarrier Spacings (SCSs) are supported.
  • Both standalone (SA) and non-standalone (NSA) modes are supported.
  • Option 3x, 3a, and 2 are supported.

LTE:

  • Maximum supported configuration: 5CC downlink and 2CC uplink
  • Downlink: Cat20; uplink: Cat18
  • Downlink 4×4 MIMO is supported on the LTE bands B1, B3, B7, B38, B39, B40, B41, B42, and B43.
  • Downlink 2×2 MIMO is supported on the LTE bands B5, B8, B18, B19, B20, B26, B28, B32, and B34.
  • 1.4, 3, 5, 10, 15, and 20 MHz radio bandwidths are supported.
  • Uplink modulation schemes QPSK, 16QAM, 64QAM, and 256QAM are supported.
  • Downlink modulation schemes QPSK, 16QAM, 64QAM, and 256QAM are supported.

Supported rate:

- 5G SA: Downlink a maximum of 3CC (300M), 4x4 MIMO, 256 QAM, peak rate: 2.5Gbps (DL/UL subframe configuration 8:2).

Uplink a maximum of 2CC (200M) 2x2 MIMO, 256 QAM, peak rate: 500 Mbps (DL/UL subframe configuration 8:2)

- 5G NSA: Downlink a maximum of 3CC (300M), 4x4 MIMO, 256 QAM, peak rate: 2.5Gbps (DL/UL subframe configuration 8:2).

Uplink a maximum of 2CC (200M) 2x2 MIMO, 256 QAM, peak rate: 125 Mbps (DL/UL subframe configuration 8:2)

NOTE:

The preceding performance data is theoretical peak values of one-way transmission obtained in a specific test environment (length of test data packets: ≥ 1400 bytes) in Huawei labs. The actual performance data may vary depending on the slot bandwidth of the chassis, network environment, and purchased carrier package.

RESET Reset button

This button is used to reset the device.

  • Pressing and holding down the button for 5s or more: The device is reset and restored to factory defaults.
