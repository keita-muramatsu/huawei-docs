(Optional) Approving and Configuring a Member Device

Context

The master device must approve a member device before logging in and delivering network-wide configurations to it. This ensures that the member device can be effectively managed. The configurations delivered in the device approval view of the master device take effect only on a single member device.

This section describes how to configure a role, alias, and dedicated password for a single member device on the master device.

Procedure

  1. Enter the system view.

    system-view

  2. Enter the net assistant view.

    net-assistant

    By default, the net assistant view is not displayed.

  3. Enter the device view.

    device

    By default, the device view is not displayed.

  4. Approve the member device.

    device-granted mac-addr type type-paravalue

    By default, no device is approved.

    You can obtain the MAC address through the device approval list on WebMaster or by running the display bridge mac-address command on the member device.

  5. Configure a role for the approved member device.

    role { wan-gw | service-gw | lc | wac } *

    By default, no role is configured for the approved device.

  6. Set an alias for the approved member device.

    alias device-alias

    By default, no alias is configured.

  7. Change the dedicated network-wide management password of the approved member device.

    user name-paravalue password [ password-paravalue ]
