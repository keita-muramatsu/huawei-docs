LAN interfaces: four GE electrical interfaces RJ45

A GE electrical interface (10/100/1000 Mbit/s auto-sensing) transmits and receives services at 10 Mbit/s, 100 Mbit/s, or 1000 Mbit/s.

  • Standards compliance: IEEE802.3, IEEE802.3u, IEEE802.3ab
  • Working mode: Full-duplex (10/100/1000 Mbit/s auto-sensing)
  • Maximum transmission distance: 100 m
NOTE:

All GE LAN interfaces can be configured as WAN interfaces.

Ethernet cable
LAN interfaces: four GE combo interfaces

GE electrical interface: RJ45

GE optical interface: LC/PC

A GE combo interface consists of an optical Ethernet interface and an electrical Ethernet interface on the panel. The two interfaces have only one internal forwarding interface. The electrical and optical interfaces are multiplexed, and only either of them can work at a time. When either of the Ethernet interfaces is working, the other interface is shut down.

  • The GE electrical interface (10/100/1000 Mbit/s auto-sensing) transmits and receives service traffic at 10 Mbit/s, 100 Mbit/s, or 1000 Mbit/s.
  • The GE electrical interface complies with IEEE 802.3, IEEE 802.3u, and IEEE 802.3ab.
  • The GE optical interface (100/1000 Mbit/s auto-sensing) transmits and receives service traffic at 100 Mbit/s or 1000 Mbit/s.
  • The GE optical interface complies with IEEE 802.3z.

NOTE:

By default, a combo interface works as an electrical interface. You can run the combo enable fiber command to configure the combo interface to work as an optical interface.

All GE LAN interfaces can be configured as WAN interfaces.
  • GE electrical interface: Ethernet cable
  • GE optical interface: optical module

The following optical modules are supported:

FE SFP&eSFP optical modules

GE eSFP optical modules

GPON&EPON optical modules (supported in V600R023C10 and later versions)
WAN interfaces: two 10GE optical/2.5GE electrical combo interfaces

2.5GE electrical interface: RJ45

10GE optical interface: LC/PC

A combo interface consists of an optical Ethernet interface and an electrical Ethernet interface on the panel. The two interfaces have only one internal forwarding interface. The electrical and optical interfaces are multiplexed, and only either of them can work at a time. When either of the Ethernet interfaces is working, the other interface is shut down.

  • The 10GE optical interface (1/10 Gbit/s auto-sensing) transmits and receives service traffic at 1 Gbit/s or 10 Gbit/s.
  • The 2.5GE electrical interface (100 Mbit/s, 1000 Mbit/s, and 2.5 Gbit/s auto-sensing) transmits and receives service traffic at 100 Mbit/s, 1000 Mbit/s, or 2.5 Gbit/s.
NOTE:
  • By default, an interface works as a 2.5GE electrical interface. You can run the port combination-mode 10GE interface MultiGE0/0/0 or port combination-mode 10GE interface MultiGE0/0/1 command in the system view to configure the corresponding interface to work as a 10GE optical interface based on the actual networking requirements.
  • All 10GE/2.5GE WAN interfaces can be configured as LAN interfaces.
  • 2.5GE electrical interface: Ethernet cable
  • 10GE optical interface: optical module

The following optical modules are supported:

10GE SFP+ optical modules

GE eSFP optical modules

GPON&EPON optical modules (supported in V600R023C10 and later versions)

10GE SFP+ copper modules (supported in V600R023C10 and later versions)

  • Standards compliance: USB 2.0
  • Working mode: Host

USB 2.0 flash drive

NOTE:

The AR does not support SSD USB flash drives.
LTE antenna interface SMA-K (screw threads outside and a hole inside)

LTE antenna interfaces of the device include a primary interface (MAIN) and a secondary interface (DIV). The primary and secondary interfaces work at the same time. The primary interface transmits and receives LTE signals, while the secondary interface only receives LTE signals. They work together to improve the downlink rate (1T2R).

Interface standards and supported frequency bands:

  • LTE FDD: Band 1/3/5/7/8/20/28
  • LTE TDD: Band 38/40/41
  • WCDMA: Band 1/5/8
  • GSM: 900/1800 (MHz)

Supported rate:

  • LTE FDD: 40 Mbit/s in the uplink and 120 Mbit/s in the downlink
  • LTE TDD: 30 Mbit/s in the uplink and 120 Mbit/s in the downlink
  • DC-HSPA+: 5.76 Mbit/s in the uplink and 42 Mbit/s in the downlink
  • HSPA+: 5.76 Mbit/s in the uplink and 21.6 Mbit/s in the downlink
  • WCDMA PS: 384 kbit/s in the uplink and 384 kbit/s in the downlink
  • WCDMA CS: 64 kbit/s in the uplink and 64 kbit/s in the downlink
  • EDGE: 236.8 kbit/s in the uplink and 236.8 kbit/s in the downlink
  • GPRS: 85.6 kbit/s in the uplink and 85.6 kbit/s in the downlink
  • GSM: 9.6 kbit/s in the uplink and 14.4 kbit/s in the downlink
NOTE:

The preceding performance data is theoretical peak values of one-way transmission obtained in a specific test environment (length of test data packets: ≥ 1400 bytes) in Huawei labs. The actual performance data may vary depending on the network environment and purchased carrier package.

Whip antenna (delivered with the device by default)

LTE whip antenna (27011207)

LTE indoor remote antenna (27012152)

RESET Reset button

This button is used to reset the device.

  • Pressing and holding down the button for 5s or more: The device is reset and restored to factory defaults.

Resetting the device will interrupt services. Exercise caution when you press the reset button.
