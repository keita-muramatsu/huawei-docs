Configuring an SRv6 TE Policy (Controller-based Dynamic Delivery)

Prerequisites

Before configuring an SRv6 TE Policy, you have completed the following task:

  • Configure an IGP to implement network layer connectivity.

Context

An SRv6 TE Policy can be manually configured on a forwarder through the CLI or NETCONF. Alternatively, it can be delivered to a forwarder after being dynamically generated through a protocol (such as BGP) on a controller — this mode facilitates network deployment.

The process for a controller to deliver an SRv6 TE Policy is as follows:

  1. The controller collects information, such as network topology and SID information, through BGP-LS.

  2. The controller and headend forwarder establish an IPv6 SR Policy address family-specific BGP peer relationship.

  3. The controller computes an SRv6 TE Policy path and delivers it to the headend forwarder through the BGP peer relationship. The headend then generates an SRv6 TE Policy entry.
