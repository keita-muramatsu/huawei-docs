Overview of Net Assistant

Definition

The net assistant function allows for centralized management of devices on the entire network through one management device. It provides automated, visualized, and batch O&M capabilities, making the network visible, manageable, and controllable.

Purpose

As digital transformation advances, traditional networks face the following new challenges:

  • Low deployment efficiency: Most networks are configured and commissioned using traditional commands, involving time-consuming and labor-intensive operations.
  • Difficult O&M: Upgrading devices is a difficult process. If exceptions occur during an upgrade, no quick recovery method is available. And if a fault occurs, the lack of automated troubleshooting capabilities makes it necessary to check devices one by one and review logs, alarms, and device configurations, resulting in low efficiency.
  • Complex management: Networks often consist of diverse products, including wired, wireless, security, and terminal products, spread across multiple locations and lacking a visible network topology, presenting significant management challenges.

To address the preceding challenges, the net assistant function enables management, batch configuration, and visualized O&M of devices on the entire network through a local unified management platform, eliminating the need for dedicated servers.

After this function is enabled, you can log in to WebMaster to centrally configure and manage network devices through simple web operations. For details, see WebMaster Solution Overview.
