Understanding SRv6 TE Policy Attribute Templates

Configuring BFD and hot standby (HSB) on a per-policy basis for many SRv6 TE Policies is time-consuming and complex. To simplify configuration, you can configure an SRv6 TE Policy attribute template and apply it in the candidate-path view of the SRv6 TE Policy.
