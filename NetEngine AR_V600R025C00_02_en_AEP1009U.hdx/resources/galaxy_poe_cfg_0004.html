PoE Fundamentals

Components in a PoE System

A PoE power supply system consists of a power-sourcing equipment (PSE) and powered devices (PDs), as shown in Figure 1. The PSE and PDs are connected through network cables.

  • PSE: a PoE device that feeds power to a PD through an Ethernet. It provides functions such as detection, analysis, and intelligent power management.
  • PD: a device that receives power, such as an AP, portable device charger, POS machine, and camera. PDs are classified into standard and nonstandard PDs depending on whether they conform to IEEE standards.
  • PoE power module: provides power to a PoE system. The number of PDs that can be connected to a PSE is limited by the power output of a PoE power module. PoE power modules are classified as either built-in or external depending on whether they are pluggable.

Working Process of the PoE Power Supply

The PoE power supply process is as follows:

  1. PD detection: A PSE periodically transmits a low voltage with limited current through PoE interfaces in order to detect PDs. If the PSE detects a resistance ranging from 19 kΩ to 26.5 kΩ, IEEE-compliant PDs are connected to the PSE. Low voltages for feature resistances of 19 kΩ to 26.5 kΩ are often 2.7 V to 10.1 V, and the detection period is 2 seconds.
  2. Power supply capability negotiation: The PSE classifies PDs and negotiates the supplied power. There are two methods for negotiating the power supply capability: parsing the detected specific resistance and negotiating LLDP power capabilities.
  3. Power-on start: In the startup period (often less than 15 µs), the PSE starts to provide power to PDs from a low voltage, and then begins increasing the voltage until it reaches 48 V DC.
  4. Power-on: After the voltage reaches 48 V, the output voltage provided to the PDs is stabilized at 48 V, and the power consumption of all PDs does not exceed the maximum output power of the PSE.
  5. Power-off: The PSE keeps detecting the input current of PDs while providing power. Upon detecting that the current of PDs falls below the minimum value or increases sharply, the PSE stops providing power to the PDs and repeats PD detection. This situation occurs when PDs are disconnected from the PSE, encounter a power overload or short circuit, or their power consumption exceeds the power supply capacity of the PSE.

PoE supports non-stop power supply, meaning that a PoE device can still supply power to PDs during restart using the reboot command. Only PoE-capable models support non-stop power supply. For details on PoE support for each model, see "Technical Specifications" in the hardware description.

PoE Power Supply Mode

According to the IEEE standard, PSEs are used to supply power to PDs and are classified into MidSpan and Endpoint. MidSpan indicates that the PoE module is installed outside the device, whereas Endpoint indicates that the PoE module is integrated into the device. Huawei PSEs have PoE modules integrated inside, belonging to the Endpoint PSE category. Endpoint PSEs are compatible with 10BASE-T, 100BASE-TX, 1000BASE-T, and 2.5GE BASE-T interfaces and therefore can be more widely used than MidSpan PSEs.

Endpoint PSEs have two power supply modes: Alternative A (line pairs 1/2 and 3/6) and Alternative B (line pairs 4/5 and 7/8).

  • In Alternative A mode, power is transmitted over data line pairs.

    The PSE provides power for the PD over line pairs 1/2 and 3/6. Pins 1/2 use the negative voltage while pins 3/6 use the positive voltage. 10BASE-T and 100BASE-TX interfaces use line pairs 1/2 and 3/6 to transmit data, while 1000BASE-T interfaces use all four line pairs to transmit data. As DC power and data frequency are independent, power and data can be transmitted over the same pair of lines.

  • In Alternative B, power is transmitted over unused line pairs.

    The PSE provides power to the PD over line pairs 4/5 and 7/8. Pins 4/5 use the positive voltage and pins 7/8 use the negative voltage.

In general, standard PDs must support both modes, whereas PSEs can support only one. Huawei PSEs support only Alternative A.
