IoT Card Overview

Definition

The Internet of Things (IoT) is the interconnection of various information sensing equipment — such as radio frequency identification (RFID) devices, infrared sensors, Global Positioning System (GPS), and laser scanners — within the Internet infrastructure. IoT aims to connect all objects to the network for easier identification and management.

An IoT card is a module that extends IoT functions on an AP.

Purpose

IoT technologies are applicable to a range of industries, such as healthcare, shopping mall/supermarket, manufacturing, logistics, and warehousing. These industries require good wireless coverage and need to leverage IoT technologies (such as RFID) to implement applications including health monitoring, infant protection, asset management, and goods supply chain management.

IoT cards can be used to add IoT applications on the basis of wireless network coverage, achieving the goal of integrating wireless networks and IoT.

Benefits

  • Simplified network: Inserting IoT modules into an AP integrates all IoT access points on the AP. Because wireless terminals and IoT terminals work on the same network, the network architecture is much simpler.
  • Easy management: An administrator only needs to manage one network as the WLAN and IoT are integrated.
