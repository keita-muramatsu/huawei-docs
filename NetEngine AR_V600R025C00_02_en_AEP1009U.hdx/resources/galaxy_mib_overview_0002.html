Network Management

As networks scale up and grow more complicated, how to effectively manage them and ensure network service quality has become a huge challenge. Network management has become a critical part of a network solution.

Network management involves:

  • Managed node: device that is managed on the network.

  • Agent: software or hardware that traces the status of managed nodes.

  • Network management station (NMS): a workstation that manages network devices and resources.

  • Network management protocol: the protocol used by the network management station and agents to exchange messages. At present, the Simple Network Management Protocol (SNMP) is most widely used for network management over TCP/IP networks.
