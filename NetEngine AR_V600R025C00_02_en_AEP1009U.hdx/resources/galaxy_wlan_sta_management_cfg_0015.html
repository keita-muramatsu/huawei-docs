Configuring a Service VLAN

Context

Service VLANs transmit service data packets on a WLAN. Layer 2 data packets delivered from a VAP to an AP carry the service VLAN ID.

Because WLANs provide many different access modes, numerous STAs may connect to the same WLAN from the same place as each other (such as the entrance to an office area or stadium), and then roam to different APs. In this case, proper service VLAN planning is needed to ensure that STAs are successfully assigned IP addresses.

  • If single VLANs are configured as service VLANs, IP address resources may become insufficient in an area where a large number of STAs access the WLAN, yet those in the other areas may be wasted.

  • Super VLANs cannot be used as service VLANs.
  • For an AR with the built-in AP, add VGE0/0/0 to the service VLAN in tagged mode.

Procedure

  1. Enter the system view.

    system-view

  2. Enter the WLAN view.

    wlan

  3. Create a VAP profile view and enter its view.

    vap-profile name profile-name

  4. Configure a service VLAN.

    service-vlan vlan-id vlan-id
