Binding a Radio Profile

Context

After the configuration in a radio profile is complete, you need to bind the radio profile to an AP group, AP, AP radio, or AP group radio, so that the configuration in the radio profile can be delivered and take effect.

After a radio profile is bound to an AP group or specific AP, the parameter settings in the radio profile take effect on all radios of the AP group or AP. After a radio profile is bound to an AP group radio or AP radio, the parameter settings in the radio profile take effect on the specified radio of the AP group or specific AP. The configurations on the AP and its radios have a higher priority than those on the AP group and radios in the group.

A 2.4 GHz radio profile and a 5 GHz radio profile can be configured together. However, the 2.4 GHz radio profile takes effect only on 2.4 GHz radios, and the 5 GHz radio profile takes effect only on 5 GHz and 6 GHz radios.

Procedure

  1. Enter the system view.

    system-view

  2. Enter the WLAN view.

    wlan

  3. Bind the radio profile.

    • Bind the radio profile in the AP group view or AP view.
      1. Enter the AP group view.
        ap-group name group-name

        Or enter the AP view.

        ap-id ap-id
      2. Bind the radio profile.
        radio-2g-profile profile-name radio { radio-id | all }

        Or

        radio-5g-profile profile-name radio { radio-id | all }

        By default, the 2.4 GHz radio profile default and 5 GHz radio profile default are bound to an AP group, and no radio profiles are bound to an AP.

    • Bind the radio profile in the AP group radio view or AP radio view.
      1. Enter the AP group radio view.
        ap-group name group-name
radio radio-id

        Or enter the AP radio view.

        ap-id ap-id
radio radio-id
      2. Bind the radio profile.
        radio-2g-profile profile-name

        Or

        radio-5g-profile profile-name

        By default, the 2.4 GHz radio profile default and 5 GHz radio profile default are bound to AP group radios, and no radio profiles are bound to AP radios.
