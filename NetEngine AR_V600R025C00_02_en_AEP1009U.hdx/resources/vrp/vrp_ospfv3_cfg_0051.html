Understanding How to Control the Establishment of OSPFv3 Neighbor Relationships

OSPFv3 uses Hello packets to establish and maintain OSPFv3 neighbor relationships. Hello packets are periodically sent on OSPFv3 interfaces. OSPFv3 uses the Hello timer to control the interval for sending Hello packets. The intervals between two neighbors must be the same; otherwise, OSPFv3 neighbor relationships cannot be established. You can adjust the value of the Hello timer to change the speed at which an OSPFv3 neighbor relationship is established and thereby change the speed of network convergence.

To enable a device to detect faults on neighbors or changes in network topology quickly, OSPFv3 introduces the Dead timer. If no Hello packet is received from a neighbor within a Dead interval, the neighbor is considered down.
