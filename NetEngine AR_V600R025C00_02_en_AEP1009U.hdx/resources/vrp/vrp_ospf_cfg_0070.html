Understanding How to Control OSPF Route Calculation

In general, OSPF route calculation consists of two phases. First, OSPF neighbors exchange LSAs to synchronize LSDBs, and then OSPF uses algorithms to calculate routes based on these LSDBs. Currently, PRC and I-SPF are the only algorithms used by OSPF devices. You can control OSPF route calculation by managing LSA exchange between OSPF neighbors.
