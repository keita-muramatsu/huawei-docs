DSVPN on Small and Midsize Networks

A small or midsize network has only a few branches. They can dynamically establish VPNs with each other by using DSVPN in a non-shortcut scenario for direct communication.

Figure 1 DSVPN on a small or midsize network

On the DSVPN network shown in Figure 1, two branches (Spoke1 and Spoke2) connect to the HQ (Hub) through the Internet. Spoke1 and Spoke2 are configured to learn routes from each other. They can communicate with each other directly because they are each other's next hop.
