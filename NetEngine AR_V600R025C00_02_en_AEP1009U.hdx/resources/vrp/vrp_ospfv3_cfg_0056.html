Understanding How to Control OSPFv3 Route Calculation

In general, OSPFv3 route calculation consists of two phases. First, OSPFv3 neighbors exchange LSAs to synchronize LSDBs, and then OSPFv3 uses algorithms to calculate routes based on these LSDBs. Currently, partial route calculation (PRC) and incremental SPF (I-SPF) are the only algorithms used by OSPFv3 devices. You can control OSPFv3 route calculation by managing LSA exchange between OSPFv3 neighbors or adjusting the values of SPF timers.
