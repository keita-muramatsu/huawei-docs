Configuring a Device to Respond to UDP Tests Initiated by an IP-SLA-capable Third-Party Device

Prerequisites

The parameters of UDP tests have been set on the third-party device.

Context

When a device connects to a third-party device and needs to respond to the UDP-Echo or UDP-Jitter packets sent by the third-party device, you need to configure this function.

Procedure

  1. Enter the system view.

    system-view

  2. Enable the device to maintain compatibility with the IP-SLA function of non-Huawei devices and enter the NQA-compatible IP-SLA view.

    nqa compatible ip-sla

  3. Enable the device to respond to UDP-Echo or UDP-Jitter packets sent by a third-party device.

    responder enable

  4. Configure the device to respond to UDP-Echo or UDP-Jitter packets with a specified destination IP address, destination port number, and service type sent by the third-party device.

    udp ip ip-address [ vpn-instance vpn-name ] port port-number [ tos tos-value ]

Verifying the Configuration

  • Run the display nqa compatible ip-sla responder command to check the SLA reflector instance status of NQA-compatible third-party devices.
