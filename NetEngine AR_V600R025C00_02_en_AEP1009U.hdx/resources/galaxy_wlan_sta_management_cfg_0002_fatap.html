Overview of WLAN STA Management

Definition

A WLAN station (STA) is a terminal that complies with 802.11 standards, for example, a PC equipped with a wireless network adapter or a WLAN-capable mobile phone. WLAN STA management involves STA access configuration and O&M capabilities after APs go online.

Purpose

The WLAN access function of devices allows users to easily access a WLAN and move around within the WLAN coverage area.

Benefits

WLAN STAs can access the network freely, without being restricted by cables or port locations. This makes WLAN deployment ideal for scenarios such as office buildings, departure halls, resorts, hotels, stadiums, and cafes. The device can steer WLAN STAs to a proper WLAN based on the actual WLAN environment, improving service experience.
