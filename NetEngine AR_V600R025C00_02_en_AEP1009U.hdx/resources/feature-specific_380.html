Example for Configuring Intelligent Traffic Policing Based on Hierarchical CAR HQoS

Networking Requirements

On an SD-WAN EVPN network shown in Figure 1, an enterprise has the headquarters (HQ) and multiple branches, subscribes to 100 Mbit/s bandwidth, and has the following QoS requirements:

  • When multiple branch sites access the HQ site (RR), the traffic rate may exceed the bandwidth of the HQ site. In this case, inbound intelligent traffic policing needs to be implemented on the HQ site to dynamically adjust the bandwidth of low-priority services.
  • A branch site (CPE1) has two departments: finance department and production department. It is required that the two departments have independent bandwidth.
  • The finance department, production department, and other departments have multiple services, including voice, video conference, and data services. It is required that the bandwidth of key voice and video conference services be guaranteed.

Configuration Roadmap

The configuration roadmap is as follows:

  1. Configure parent and child traffic policies for hierarchical CAR on DeviceA. The parent traffic policy is used to differentiate departments (VPN1, VPN2, and other-VPN), and the child traffic policy is used to differentiate services (service1 to service3) in the departments.
    1. Configure a child traffic policy to differentiate services through application identification. (The application identification function needs to be enabled on the interface where the traffic policy is executed.) The enterprise uses WeLink to carry office applications. Table 1 lists the bandwidth allocated to the voice service (WeLink_VideoCall), video conference service (WeLink_Meeting), and other services. It is required that the financial department, production department, and other departments be allocated with the same service bandwidth.
    2. Configure a parent traffic policy and specify QoS local IDs for packets on the tunnel interface to differentiate departments. Table 1 lists bandwidth allocation for each department.
    3. Create an intelligent traffic policing policy and apply the policy to the traffic behavior of the parent traffic policy.
  2. On DeviceA, enable the function of executing a traffic policy after decapsulation on the SD-WAN tunnel interface.
  3. Configure a global CAR profile on DeviceA to limit the total bandwidth in the inbound direction of DeviceA.
  4. On the inbound interface of DeviceA, configure a global CAR profile and a parent traffic policy for hierarchical CAR to be applied after decapsulation.
  • This example describes only the configuration roadmap and procedure for configuring intelligent traffic policing based on hierarchical CAR HQoS. Assume that SD-WAN EVPN configurations have been completed. You need to ensure that the SD-WAN tunnel encapsulation protocol of the tunnel interface has been set to sd-wan. For details about SD-WAN EVPN configurations, see "SD-WAN EVPN Configuration" in CLI Configuration Guide > VPN Configuration.
  • In practice, if the application identification function is used to differentiate service traffic, you can query the names of related applications in Huawei security center or by running the display application command.

Procedure

  1. Configure a child traffic policy for hierarchical CAR on DeviceA.

    # Configure traffic classifiers of the child traffic policy.

    <HUAWEI> system-view
[HUAWEI] sysname DeviceA
[DeviceA] traffic classifier service1
[DeviceA-classifier-service1] if-match application WeLink_VideoCall
[DeviceA-classifier-service1] quit
[DeviceA] traffic classifier service2
[DeviceA-classifier-service2] if-match application WeLink_Meeting
[DeviceA-classifier-service2] quit
[DeviceA] traffic classifier service3
[DeviceA-classifier-service3] if-match any
[DeviceA-classifier-service3] quit

    # Configure traffic behaviors of the child traffic policy.

    [DeviceA] traffic behavior service1
[DeviceA-behavior-service1] car cir pct 10 pir pct 50 hierarchical-car share mode color-aware 
[DeviceA-behavior-service1] quit
[DeviceA] traffic behavior service2
[DeviceA-behavior-service2] car cir pct 50 pir pct 100 hierarchical-car share mode color-aware
[DeviceA-behavior-service2] quit
[DeviceA] traffic behavior service3
[DeviceA-behavior-service3] car cir pct 40 pir pct 100 hierarchical-car share mode color-aware
[DeviceA-behavior-service3] quit

    # Configure the child traffic policy.

    [DeviceA] traffic policy sub-policy1
[DeviceA-trafficpolicy-sub-policy1] classifier service1 behavior service1
[DeviceA-trafficpolicy-sub-policy1] classifier service2 behavior service2
[DeviceA-trafficpolicy-sub-policy1] classifier service3 behavior service3
[DeviceA-trafficpolicy-sub-policy1] quit

  2. Configure an intelligent traffic policing policy on DeviceA.

    # Configure an intelligent traffic policing policy.

    [DeviceA] itp policy test
[DeviceA -ItpPolicy-test1] bandwidth-threshold pct 90
[DeviceA -ItpPolicy-test1] quit

  3. Configure a parent traffic policy for hierarchical CAR on DeviceA.

    # Specify QoS local IDs for packets on tunnel interfaces. (Tunnel100 and Tunnel200 have been bound to VPN1 and VPN2, respectively.)
    [DeviceA] interface tunnel100
[DeviceA-Tunnel100] qos-local-id 10
[DeviceA-Tunnel100] quit
[DeviceA] interface tunnel200
[DeviceA-Tunnel200] qos-local-id 20
[DeviceA-Tunnel200] quit
    # Configure traffic classifiers of the parent traffic policy.
    [DeviceA] traffic classifier vpn1
[DeviceA-classifier-vpn1] if-match qos-local-id 10
[DeviceA-classifier-vpn1] quit
[DeviceA] traffic classifier vpn2
[DeviceA-classifier-vpn2] if-match qos-local-id 20
[DeviceA-classifier-vpn2] quit
[DeviceA] traffic classifier other-VPN
[DeviceA-classifier-other-VPN] if-match any
[DeviceA-classifier-other-VPN] quit

    # Configure traffic behaviors of the parent traffic policy and apply the intelligent traffic policing policy to the traffic behaviors.

    [DeviceA] traffic behavior vpn1
[DeviceA-behavior-vpn1] car cir pct 40 pir pct 100 hierarchical-car share mode color-aware
[DeviceA-behavior-vpn1] traffic-policy sub-policy1
[DeviceA-behavior-vpn1] itp-policy test
[DeviceA-behavior-vpn1] quit
[DeviceA] traffic behavior vpn2
[DeviceA-behavior-vpn2] car cir pct 20 pir pct 50 hierarchical-car share mode color-aware
[DeviceA-behavior-vpn2] traffic-policy sub-policy1
[DeviceA-behavior-vpn1] itp-policy test
[DeviceA-behavior-vpn2] quit
[DeviceA] traffic behavior other-VPN
[DeviceA-behavior-other-VPN] car cir pct 40 pir pct 100 hierarchical-car share mode color-aware
[DeviceA-behavior-other-VPN] traffic-policy sub-policy1
[DeviceA-behavior-vpn1] itp-policy test
[DeviceA-behavior-other-VPN] quit

    # Configure the parent traffic policy.

    [DeviceA] traffic policy hcar
[DeviceA-trafficpolicy-hcar] classifier vpn1 behavior vpn1
[DeviceA-trafficpolicy-hcar] classifier vpn2 behavior vpn2
[DeviceA-trafficpolicy-hcar] classifier other-VPN behavior other-VPN
[DeviceA-trafficpolicy-hcar] quit

  4. On DeviceA, enable the function of executing a traffic policy after decapsulation on the SD-WAN tunnel interface. Ensure that the tunnel-protocol sd-wan command has been run. Otherwise, the traffic policy cannot be executed after decapsulation.

    [DeviceA] interface tunnel100
[DeviceA-Tunnel100] tunnel-protocol sd-wan
[DeviceA-Tunnel100] sd-wan service enable
[DeviceA-Tunnel100] ip binding vpn-instance vpn1
[DeviceA-Tunnel100] traffic-policy decap-process enable
[DeviceA-Tunnel100] quit
[DeviceA] interface tunnel200
[DeviceA-Tunnel200] tunnel-protocol sd-wan
[DeviceA-Tunnel200] sd-wan service enable
[DeviceA-Tunnel200] ip binding vpn-instance vpn2
[DeviceA-Tunnel200] traffic-policy decap-process enable
[DeviceA-Tunnel200] quit

  5. On DeviceA, configure a global CAR profile named hcar_temp to limit the total bandwidth in the inbound direction of DeviceA to 100 Mbit/s.

    [DeviceA] qos car hcar_temp cir 100000

  6. Enable the application identification function on the inbound interface of DeviceA.

    [DeviceA] interface 10ge 0/0/1
[DeviceA-10GE0/0/1] sa enable

  7. On the inbound interface of DeviceA, configure a global CAR profile and a parent traffic policy for hierarchical CAR to be applied after decapsulation.

    [DeviceA-10GE0/0/1] qos car inbound hcar_temp decap-process
[DeviceA-10GE0/0/1] traffic-policy hcar inbound decap-process
[DeviceA-10GE0/0/1] quit
