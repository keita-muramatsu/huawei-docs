MIB Classification

MIBs are classified into the following two types:

  • Public MIBs: defined by RFCs and used for structure design of public protocols and standardization of interfaces. For example, OSPF-MIB (RFC 1850) and BGP4-MIB (RFC 1657) are typical public MIBs. Most vendors need to provide SNMP interfaces based on the specifications defined in RFCs.

  • Private MIBs: They are necessary supplements to public MIBs. When developing private protocols or special functions, enterprises can use private MIBs to improve the management function of SNMP interfaces. In addition, private MIBs enable third-party NMS software to manage devices that use private protocols or have special functions.
