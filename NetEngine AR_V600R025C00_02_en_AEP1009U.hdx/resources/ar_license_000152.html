Distributing a License

Context

Resellers distribute software entitlements online to lower-level resellers or end customers. In this way, lower-level resellers or end customers can enjoy the rights to manage software licenses online.
  • After an entitlement is distributed, its distribution status changes to Distributed. After the entitlement is rolled back, its distribution status changes to Undistributed.
  • In a sales chain, entitlement distribution is allowed only once for the same reseller.
  • For multiple entitlements distributed at the same time, all the entitlements must belong to the same contract and the recipient company must be the same.

Procedure

  1. Obtain the revocation codes of the deactivated and target devices.
  2. Log in to the ESDP at https://esdp.huawei.com/sdp2.
  3. On the top navigation bar, choose Entitlement Management > My Entitlements and click To be Distributed. The To be Distributed tab page is displayed.
  4. Enter a contract number and other required information, and click Search.

  5. Select several entitlements and click Distribute Selected Entitlement. To distribute all entitlements, click Distribute All Entitlements. To distribute a single entitlement, click Distribute in the Operation column.

  6. Enter the recipient company information and click OK. The information marked with * is mandatory.
