Ports

Figure 3 Ports on the SIC-NRGL-V2

1. Eight 5G/LTE antenna interfaces (ATN0 to ATN7)

NOTE:

The eight antenna ports support different frequency bands and operating modes. To meet the requirements of various standards and service performance, all eight ports must be connected to antennas during operation.

2. SIM card slots (SIM1 and SIM2)

NOTICE:
  • Nano-SIM cards are supported.
  • SIM cards are not hot-swappable. After replugging a SIM card, you need to restart the radio module or restart the device.
  • Ensure that the SIM card slot is locked after a SIM card is removed or installed.
NOTE:
  • The device supports double-SIM single-standby functionality, with SIM1 as the default primary card.
  • If only one SIM card needs to be installed, install it in slot SIM1.

Eight 5G/LTE antenna interfaces SMB-J

5G NR:

  • 3GPP Rel-16 is supported.
  • Uplink 256QAM and downlink 256QAM are supported.
  • Downlink 4×4 MIMO is supported on the 5G bands N1, N3, N7, N38, N40, N41, N77, N78, and N79.
  • Downlink 2×2 MIMO is supported on the 5G bands N5, N8, N20, N28, and N75.
  • Uplink 2×2 MIMO is supported on the 5G bands N1, N38, N40, N41, N77, N78, and N79.
  • Both 15 kHz and 30 kHz Subcarrier Spacings (SCSs) are supported.
  • Both standalone (SA) and non-standalone (NSA) modes are supported.
  • Option 3x, 3a, and 2 are supported.

LTE:

  • Maximum supported configuration: 5CC downlink and 2CC uplink
  • Downlink: Cat20; uplink: Cat18
  • Downlink 4×4 MIMO is supported on the LTE bands B1, B3, B7, B38, B39, B40, B41, B42, and B43.
  • Downlink 2×2 MIMO is supported on the LTE bands B5, B8, B18, B19, B20, B26, B28, B32, and B34.
  • 1.4, 3, 5, 10, 15, and 20 MHz radio bandwidths are supported.
  • Uplink modulation schemes QPSK, 16QAM, 64QAM, and 256QAM are supported.
  • Downlink modulation schemes QPSK, 16QAM, 64QAM, and 256QAM are supported.

Supported rate:

- 5G SA: Downlink a maximum of 3CC (300M), 4x4 MIMO, 256 QAM, peak rate: 5.0Gbps (DL/UL subframe configuration 8:2).

Uplink a maximum of 2CC (200M) 2x2 MIMO, 256 QAM, peak rate: 500 Mbps (DL/UL subframe configuration 8:2)

- 5G NSA: Downlink a maximum of 3CC (300M), 4x4 MIMO, 256 QAM, peak rate: 3.6Gbps (DL/UL subframe configuration 8:2).

Uplink a maximum of 2CC (200M) 2x2 MIMO, 256 QAM, peak rate: 125 Mbps (DL/UL subframe configuration 8:2)

NOTE:

The preceding performance data is theoretical peak values of one-way transmission obtained in a specific test environment (length of test data packets: ≥ 1400 bytes) in Huawei labs. The actual performance data may vary depending on the slot bandwidth of the chassis, network environment, and purchased carrier package.

5G indoor remote antenna

NOTE:

Indoor remote antennas (27013849) are configured for the card by default; RF extension cables (04133554) are optional; the card-to-extension cable ratio is 1:8.

Functions and Features

Table 5 Functions and features of the SIC-NRGL-V2
Functions and Features Description
Overview

An NRGL-V2 card is a high-speed wireless WAN access module that provides high-speed wireless data transmission for enterprises.

It can be installed in an SIC slot.
Basic function

It can dial up to the 5G/4G network to implement high-speed data transmission.

It is often used as a backup WAN link to improve the reliability of interconnection between enterprise branches and headquarters.
5G NR
  • Supports 3GPP Release 16
  • Supports uplink 256QAM and downlink 256QAM
  • Supports downlink 4x4 MIMO at 5G N1/N3/N7/N38/N40/N41/N77/N78/N79
  • Supports downlink 2x2 MIMO at 5G N5/N8/N20/N28/N75
  • Supports uplink 2x2 MIMO at 5G N1/N38/N40/N41/N77/N78/N79
  • Supports 15 kHz and 30 kHz SCSs
  • Supports SA and NSA working modes
  • Supports OptioN 3x, 3a, and OptioN 2
LTE
  • Supports a maximum of 5CC aggregation in the downlink and a maximum of 2CC aggregation in the uplink
  • Downlink: Cat20; uplink: Cat18.
  • Supports downlink 4x4 MIMO at LTE B1/B3/B7/B38/B39/B40/B41/B42/B43
  • Supports downlink 2x2 MIMO at LTE B5/B8/B18/B19/B20/B26/B28/B32/B34
  • Supports 1.4/3/5/10/15/20 MHz radio bandwidth
  • Supports uplink QPSK, 16QAM, 64QAM, and 256QAM
  • Supports downlink QPSK, 16QAM, 64QAM, and 256QAM
Flexible wireless standards Compatible with 5G/4G services.
