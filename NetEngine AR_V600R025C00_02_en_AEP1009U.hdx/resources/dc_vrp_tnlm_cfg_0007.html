Applying a Tunnel Policy

Context

Only after a tunnel policy is applied, the system can select tunnels for services based on the tunnel policy.

The tunnel policy application method varies according to the VPN type. Determine which method to use based on the VPN type.

Procedure

  • Apply a tunnel policy to an IPv4 L3VPN.
    1. Enter the system view.

      system-view

    2. Enter the VPN instance view.

      ip vpn-instance vpn-instance-name

    3. Enter the VPN instance IPv4 address family view.

      ipv4-family

    4. Apply a tunnel policy to the VPN instance IPv4 address family.

      tnl-policy policy-name

  • Apply a tunnel policy to an IPv6 L3VPN.
    1. Enter the system view.

      system-view

    2. Enter the VPN instance view.

      ip vpn-instance vpn-instance-name

    3. Enter the VPN instance IPv6 address family view.

      ipv6-family

    4. Apply a tunnel policy to the VPN instance IPv6 address family.

      tnl-policy policy-name
