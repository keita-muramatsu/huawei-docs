Connection

The LTE whip antenna is directly connected to the LTE antenna port of the device.

Appearance and Structure

Figure 1 Appearance of the LTE Whip Antenna

An LTE whip antenna is delivered with a router or card that provides the LTE function. It is used on an LTE antenna interface to provide LTE access.
