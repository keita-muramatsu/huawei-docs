Understanding Binary Session Logs

Encapsulation in binary format has a limited impact on the device performance, but the transmission efficiency is high. This encapsulation format is applicable to logs that are large in quantity, for example, session logs.

Figure 1 shows a session log in binary format. From this data, you cannot obtain the specific log information. Therefore, a log server that supports this format is required to parse the log information.
