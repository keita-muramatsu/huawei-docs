Understanding AP Upgrade

APs can be upgraded on a WAC in the following modes:
  • Automatic upgrade: mainly used when APs have not gone online on a WAC.

    Typically, automatic upgrade parameters are configured before APs go online. APs will then be upgraded automatically during the onboarding process.

    An online AP will upgrade automatically after the automatic upgrade parameters are configured and the AP is restarted in any mode.

  • In-service upgrade: mainly used when APs are already online on the WAC and carry WLAN services.

    In-service upgrade can be batch performed for APs of the same type or in the same group.

  • Scheduled upgrade: mainly used when APs are already online on the WAC and carry WLAN services.

    You can configure AP upgrade in a specified time period, such as off-peak hours.

Compared with the automatic upgrade mode, the in-service upgrade mode reduces the service interruption time. Scheduled upgrade is more flexible than in-service upgrade and allows you to specify the upgrade time in advance.
