BGP Route Processing

Figure 1 shows how BGP processes routes. BGP routes arise from two sources: routes imported from other protocols and routes learned from BGP peers. To reduce the size of the BGP routing table, you can summarize BGP routes. In addition, you can configure routing policies, and apply them to route import, reception, or advertisement to filter routes or modify route attributes.
