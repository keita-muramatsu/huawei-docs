Re-distributing a License

Context

If an incorrect email address is entered during entitlement distribution, the entitlement can be re-distributed.

When re-distributing multiple entitlements, ensure that the contract numbers and the recipient company types and names are consistent.

Procedure

  1. Obtain the revocation codes of the deactivated and target devices.
  2. Log in to the ESDP at https://esdp.huawei.com/sdp2.
  3. On the top navigation bar, choose Entitlement Management > My Entitlements and click Distributed Entitlement. The Distributed Entitlement tab page is displayed.
  4. Enter a contract number and other required information, and click Search.

  5. Select an entitlement to be re-distributed and click Redistribute.

  6. Enter the information such as the email address of the recipient company and click OK.
