Overview of WLAN Service Survival

Definition

In direct forwarding scenarios on a WLAN, you can deploy a WLAN service survival policy to ensure uninterrupted WLAN services when an AP goes offline.

Purpose

In a WAC + Fit AP network architecture, control packets between the WAC and APs are forwarded through CAPWAP tunnels. If a CAPWAP link fails, online STAs connected to the AP on this link are forcibly disconnected and new STAs cannot access the AP. The WLAN service survival solution can address this issue by holding online STAs and allowing access of new STAs even upon a CAPWAP link disconnection, improving WLAN network reliability.
