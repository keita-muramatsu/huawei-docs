MPLS VPN

ARs can function as edge routers to work with core routers on backbone networks and connect to aggregation/backbone routers. They provide various routing features and L3VPN networking capabilities such as MPLS.

In a financial scenario, the enterprise's HQ and branch need to communicate through L3VPN private lines. ARs can achieve this goal by using IPv4 L3VPN over MPLS. In this way, users in the same VPN can communicate with each other, but users in different VPNs cannot.

  • ARs as provider edges (PEs): By connecting to backbone provider devices (Ps) on the MPLS backbone network, ARs help transmit enterprise data to the MPLS backbone network, as shown in Figure 1.
    Figure 1 ARs as PEs
  • ARs as customer edges (CEs): By connecting to PEs, ARs connect enterprise users to the MPLS backbone network. As shown in Figure 2, CE1 is responsible for transmitting traffic of VPN a and VPN b in a branch. Similarly, CE2 is responsible for transmitting traffic of VPN a and VPN b in the HQ.
