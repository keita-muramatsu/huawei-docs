Associating an IPsec SA with an IKE SA

Prerequisites

Association between an IPsec SA and an IKE SA can be configured only for the following types of IPsec that use IKEv1 negotiation:

  • ACL-based IPsec (involving ISAKMP IPsec policies and template IPsec policies)
  • Route-based IPsec (involving IPsec profiles)

Context

During IKEv1 negotiation, an IKE SA is established during phase 1, and an IPsec SA is established during phase 2. The two SAs can be deleted separately, if the IKE SA is deleted but the corresponding IPsec SA still exists, traffic forwarding will be effected. After association between an IPsec SA and an IKE SA is configured, the existence of the IPsec SA depends on the IKE SA. That is, if the IKE SA is deleted, the IPsec SA is also deleted.

Association between an IPsec SA and an IKE SA can be configured in the system view and the IKE peer view. The configuration in the IKE peer view takes precedence over that in the system view. If the configuration is not performed in the IKE peer view, the configuration in the system view is used.

Procedure

  • Associate an IPsec SA with an IKE SA globally.
    1. Enter the system view.

      system-view

    2. Associate an IPsec SA with an IKE SA globally.

      ikev1 phase1-phase2 sa dependent

      By default, association between an IPsec SA and an IKE SA is enabled globally. The existence of the IPsec SA depends on the IKE SA during IKEv1 negotiation.

  • Associate an IPsec SA with an IKE SA on a specific IKE peer.
    1. Enter the system view.

      system-view

    2. Enter the IKE peer view.

      ike peer peer-name

    3. Associate an IPsec SA with an IKE SA on the IKE peer.

      ikev1 phase1-phase2 sa dependent

      By default, association between an IPsec SA and an IKE SA is enabled on a specific IKE peer. The existence of the IPsec SA depends on the IKE SA during IKEv1 negotiation.
