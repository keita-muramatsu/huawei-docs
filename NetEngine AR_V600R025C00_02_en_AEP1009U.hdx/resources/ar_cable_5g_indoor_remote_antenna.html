5G Indoor Remote Antenna

Connection

The 5G indoor remote antenna is directly connected to the 5G antenna port of the device.

Appearance and Structure

Figure 1 Appearance of the 5G Indoor Remote Antenna
  • 5G device: 5G indoor remote antennas are not included in the installation accessory package and need to be purchased separately. When 5G indoor remote antennas (part number: 27013849) are configured:
    • The device-to-antenna ratio is 1:2.
    • RF jumpers (part number: 04133435) are mandatory and the device-to-jumper ratio is 1:8.
    • The distance between a 5G indoor remote antenna and its connected device can be extended as needed using RF extension cables (part number: 04133554). The device-to-extension cable ratio is 1:8, and the maximum loss of RF extension cables (part number: 04133554) is 3.2 dB.
  • 5G card: 5G indoor remote antennas (part number: 27013849) are provided for the cards by default. RF extension cables (part number: 04133554) can be configured based on the actual situation. The card-to-extension cable ratio is 1:8.
