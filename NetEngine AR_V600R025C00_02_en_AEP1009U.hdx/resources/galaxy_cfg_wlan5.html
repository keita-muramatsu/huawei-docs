WLAN Configuration (FAT AP)

An AR model whose name contains W (for example, AR5710-S8T1XWE) has a built-in Fit AP module, which can provide the wireless radio access function. For details about logs and MD-CLIs supported by built-in AP modules of AR routers, see NetEngine AR5700 Series Built-in AP V600R025C00 Maintenance Guide.
