Overview of WLAN Multicast

Definition

Multicast is one of the three modes for IP transmission. Multicast on a wireless local area network (WLAN) involves functions related to multicast data processing on access points (APs), such as Internet Group Management Protocol (IGMP) snooping on APs.

Purpose

On a WLAN, wireless stations (STAs) carry various multicast services, such as streaming television, distance education, telemedicine, and real-time video or audio conferencing. As for traffic forwarding, the STAs receive multicast data wirelessly from APs.

WLAN multicast functions can be configured to forward and control multicast data on the wireless side, thereby better supporting multicast services of STAs.
