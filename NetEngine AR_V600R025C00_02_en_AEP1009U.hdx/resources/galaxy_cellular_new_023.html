Logging the Signal Strength

Context

The signal strength determines the quality of links, but signal changes cannot be intuitively perceived. To overcome this, the device can be configured to log signal strength changes.

The signal to interference plus noise ratio (SINR) and reference signal received power (RSRP) are important indicators for measuring the signal strength.

  • The SINR is the ratio of signal power to noise power, measured in dB. It indicates the relative strength of signal power to noise power, that is, the clarity and reliability of signals. A higher SINR indicates that the signal is stronger and less noisy, resulting in better signal quality and more reliable data transmission.
  • The RSRP is the power of a reference signal received by a user device, measured in dBm. It is used for evaluating the quality of signals between user devices and base stations. A larger RSRP value indicates stronger power of received signals and better signal quality.

You can configure the SINR and RSRP thresholds, based on which the device logs signal strength changes.

  • If the strength of received signals exceeds the thresholds 10 times in a row, the device records a log, indicating that the signal strength recovers.
  • If the strength of received signals does not exceed the thresholds 10 times in a row, the device records a log, indicating that the signal strength deteriorates.

Procedure

  1. Enter the system view.

    system-view

  2. Enable the device to record cellular logs.

    cellular log enable

    By default, the function of recording cellular logs is enabled.

  3. (Optional) Adjust the thresholds for the signal strength in cellular logs.

    • Adjust the SINR threshold for determining the 5G signal strength.
      cellular log nr-sinr-threshold sinr-threshold

      By default, the SINR threshold for determining the 5G signal strength is 10 dB.

      This command is supported only on devices that support the 5G function.

    • Adjust the RSRP threshold for determining the 5G signal strength.
      cellular log nr-rsrp-threshold rsrp-threshold

      By default, the RSRP threshold for determining the 5G signal strength is –110 dBm.

      This command is supported only on devices that support the 5G function.

    • Adjust the SINR threshold for determining the LTE signal strength.
      cellular log lte-sinr-threshold sinr-threshold

      By default, the SINR threshold for determining the LTE signal strength is 10 dB in cellular logs.

      This command is supported only on devices that support the 4G function.

    • Adjust the RSRP threshold for determining the LTE signal strength.
      cellular log lte-rsrp-threshold rsrp-threshold

      By default, the RSRP threshold for determining the LTE signal strength is –110 dBm in cellular logs.

      This command is supported only on devices that support the 4G function.
