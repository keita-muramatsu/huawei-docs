Understanding BGP4+

Traditional BGP4 can manage only IPv4 routing information, and cannot transmit inter-AS routing information for other network layer protocols, such as IPv6.

In order to support IPv6, BGP4 requires the additional ability of mapping IPv6 information to the network layer reachable information (NLRI) attribute and the Next_Hop attribute.

BGP4+ introduces two NLRI attributes:

  • MP_REACH_NLRI: indicates the multiprotocol reachable NLRI, which is used to advertise reachable routes and next hop information.

  • MP_UNREACH_NLRI: indicates the multiprotocol unreachable NLRI, which is used to withdraw unreachable routes.

The Next_Hop attribute of BGP4+ is in the format of an IPv6 address, and can be an IPv6 global unicast address or the link-local address of a next hop.

Using multiple protocol extensions of BGP, BGP4+ is applicable to IPv6 networks without changing the messaging and routing mechanisms of BGP.

The fundamentals of BGP4+ are the same as those of BGP. For details, see "BGP Configuration" in CLI Configuration Guide > IP Routing Configuration.
