(Optional) Configuring QUIC-based Application Identification

Prerequisites

You can update a service awareness signature database to obtain the latest predefined applications.

Context

Quick UDP Internet Connections (QUIC) is a UDP-based transport layer protocol developed by Google. It delivers faster data transmission and higher performance while providing the same level of security as TLS/SSL through encryption and multiplexing technologies. QUIC uses a dedicated encryption mode, meaning that the device cannot directly decrypt packets. To allow the device to identify QUIC-based applications, you need to configure QUIC decryption. The QUIC decryption process involves decrypting QUIC Initial packets to extract the TLS handshake information they contain.

Procedure

  1. Enter the system view.

    system-view

  2. Enter the SA view.

    sa

  3. Enable QUIC decryption.

    quic decrypt enable
