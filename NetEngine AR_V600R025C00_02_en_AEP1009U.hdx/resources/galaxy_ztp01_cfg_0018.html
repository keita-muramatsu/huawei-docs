Verifying Email-based Deployment

Procedure

  • Enter the deployment URL in the address bar of the browser. If the deployment is successful, the deployment terminal displays a message indicating that the network registration is successful. If the deployment fails, the deployment terminal displays a message indicating that the deployment fails and the failure cause. On the Portal page indicating that the email-based deployment fails, click One-Click Collection to collect email-based deployment failure information or rectify the fault based on the failure cause, as shown in Figure 1.
