CAPWAP Link Recovery

After a CAPWAP link recovers, online STAs are processed in different ways based on the specific survival policies, as listed in the following table.

Table 1 Methods for processing online STAs after the CAPWAP link recovers

Survival Policy

Processing Method

AP-offline service continuity
  • If the AP has not restarted during the offline period: STAs that are online before the AP goes offline will not be brought offline, but STAs that go online during the AP offline period will be brought offline.
  • If the AP has restarted during the offline period: All STAs are considered new STAs that go online during the AP offline period and therefore will be brought offline.

AP-offline backup service VAP

The AP automatically disables the AP-offline backup service VAP. Then all STAs go offline from the AP-offline backup service VAP and need to go online again on the original VAP.
