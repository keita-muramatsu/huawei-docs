Configuring Parent and Child Traffic Policies

Context

Hierarchical CAR HQoS is configured in parent and child traffic policy mode. Parent and child traffic policies are configured through MQC. The parent traffic policy is used to differentiate users, and the child traffic policy is used to differentiate services of users. You can specify CAR actions for users and services in the traffic behaviors of parent and child traffic policies, respectively, and bind the child traffic policy to a traffic behavior of the parent traffic policy to implement hierarchical CAR.

When configuring CAR actions for parent and child traffic policies, you need to configure the cir-percentage and pir-percentage parameters. The two parameters specify bandwidth percentage values.

  • In the parent traffic policy, their base values are both the CIR value of the CAR action defined on an interface. (The CIR value is configured in Applying Hierarchical CAR HQoS on an Inbound Interface.)
  • In the child traffic policy, their base values are both the CIR value of the CAR action defined in the parent traffic policy. (The CIR value is configured in the CAR action of the parent traffic policy.)

Given this, before configuring parent and child traffic policies, you need to plan the bandwidth allocation at each level in hierarchical CAR.

Procedure

  1. Enter the system view.

    system-view

  2. Configure a child traffic policy.
    1. Create a traffic classifier and enter the traffic classifier view.

      traffic classifier classifier-name [ type { and | or } ]

    2. Select proper traffic classification rules as required. For details, see "Configuring a Traffic Classifier" in MQC Configuration.
    3. Exit the traffic classifier view.

      quit

    4. Create a traffic behavior and enter the traffic behavior view.

      traffic behavior behavior-name

    5. Configure the CAR action of the child traffic policy in the traffic behavior.

      car cir pct cir-percentage [ pir pct pir-percentage ] hierarchical-car [ cbs cbs-value pbs pbs-value ] share mode color-aware

    6. Exit the traffic behavior view.

      quit

    7. Create a traffic policy and enter the traffic policy view.

      traffic policy policy-name

    8. Bind a traffic classifier to a traffic behavior in the traffic policy.

      classifier classifier-name behavior behavior-name [ precedence precedence-value ]

    9. Exit the traffic policy view.

      quit

  3. Configure a parent traffic policy.
    1. Create a traffic classifier and enter the traffic classifier view.

      traffic classifier classifier-name [ type { and | or } ]

    2. Select proper traffic classification rules as required. For details, see "Configuring a Traffic Classifier" in MQC Configuration.
    3. Exit the traffic classifier view.

      quit

    4. Create a traffic behavior and enter the traffic behavior view.

      traffic behavior behavior-name

    5. Configure the CAR action of the parent traffic policy in the traffic behavior.

      car cir pct cir-percentage [ pir pct pir-percentage ] hierarchical-car [ cbs cbs-value pbs pbs-value ] share mode color-aware

    6. Bind the child traffic policy to the traffic behavior to associate the child traffic policy with the parent traffic policy.

      traffic-policy policy-name

      The value of policy-name is the name of the child traffic policy configured in step 2.

    7. (Optional) Apply an intelligent traffic policing policy.

      itp-policy policy-name

      For details about how to configure intelligent traffic policing, see (Optional) Configuring Intelligent Traffic Policing.

      Apply an intelligent traffic policing policy after a child traffic policy is bound to the traffic behavior.

    8. Exit the traffic behavior view.

      quit

    9. Create a traffic policy and enter the traffic policy view.

      traffic policy policy-name

    10. Bind a traffic classifier to a traffic behavior in the traffic policy.

      classifier classifier-name behavior behavior-name [ precedence precedence-value ]

    11. Exit the traffic policy view.

      quit

Example

# Configure a child traffic policy.

  1. Create traffic classifiers service1 and service2 to classify different services (FTPS and DNS) by application protocol.
    <HUAWEI> system-view
[HUAWEI] traffic classifier service1
[HUAWEI-classifier-service1] if-match application FTPS
[HUAWEI-classifier-service1] quit
[HUAWEI] traffic classifier service2
[HUAWEI-classifier-service2] if-match application DNS
[HUAWEI-classifier-service2] quit
  2. Create traffic behaviors service1 and service2 and configure corresponding CAR actions. The CAR values in traffic behaviors service1 and service2 are 10% and 20% of the CAR value in the upper-level parent traffic policy, respectively.
    [HUAWEI] traffic behavior service1
[HUAWEI-behavior-service1] car cir pct 10 hierarchical-car share mode color-aware
[HUAWEI-behavior-service1] quit
[HUAWEI] traffic behavior service2
[HUAWEI-behavior-service2] car cir pct 20 hierarchical-car share mode color-aware 
[HUAWEI-behavior-service2] quit
  3. Create a child traffic policy named sub-policy1 and bind traffic classifiers to traffic behaviors in the traffic policy.
    [HUAWEI] traffic policy sub-policy1
[HUAWEI-trafficpolicy-sub-policy1] classifier service1 behavior service1
[HUAWEI-trafficpolicy-sub-policy1] classifier service2 behavior service2

# Configure a parent traffic policy.

  1. Create traffic classifiers vpn1 and vpn2 to classify different users based on QoS local IDs. (Assume that QoS local IDs have been specified for packets on the tunnel interface.)
    <HUAWEI> system-view
[HUAWEI] traffic classifier vpn1
[HUAWEI-classifier-vpn1] if-match qos-local-id 1
[HUAWEI-classifier-vpn1] quit
[HUAWEI] traffic classifier vpn2
[HUAWEI-classifier-vpn2] if-match qos-local-id 2
[HUAWEI-classifier-vpn2] quit
  2. Create traffic behaviors vpn1 and vpn2, configure corresponding CAR actions, and bind the child traffic policy to the traffic behaviors. The CAR values in traffic behaviors vpn1 and vpn2 are 10% and 20% of the CAR value in the upper-level traffic policy on an interface, respectively.
    [HUAWEI] traffic behavior vpn1
[HUAWEI-behavior-vpn1] car cir pct 10 hierarchical-car share mode color-aware
[HUAWEI-behavior-vpn1] traffic-policy sub-policy1
[HUAWEI-behavior-vpn1] quit
[HUAWEI] traffic behavior vpn2
[HUAWEI-behavior-vpn2] car cir pct 20 hierarchical-car share mode color-aware
[HUAWEI-behavior-vpn2] traffic-policy sub-policy1
[HUAWEI-behavior-vpn2] quit
  3. Create a parent traffic policy named hcar and bind traffic classifiers to traffic behaviors in the traffic policy.
    [HUAWEI] traffic policy hcar
[HUAWEI-trafficpolicy-hcar] classifier vpn1 behavior vpn1
[HUAWEI-trafficpolicy-hcar] classifier vpn2 behavior vpn2
