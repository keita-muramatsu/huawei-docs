Configuring ASBRs Not to Filter VPNv4 Routes Based on VPN Targets

Context

In inter-AS VPN Option B, ASBRs do not have VPN instances. If you want ASBRs to keep received VPNv4 routes, configure ASBRs not to filter VPNv4 routes based on VPN targets.

For configuration details, see Configuring ASBRs Not to Filter VPNv4 Routes Based on VPN Targets.
