Understanding an IPsec Policy and Policy Group

An IPsec policy is required for creating SAs and determines the methods used to protect specific data flows. The device supports the following IPsec policies:

  • Configuring an ISAKMP IPsec Policy
  • Configuring a Template IPsec Policy

For ISAKMP IPsec policies and template IPsec policies, dynamic keys are automatically generated through IKE negotiation.

When configuring an IPsec policy, you can reference an ACL and an IPsec proposal to associate ACL-defined data flows with the protection method defined in the IPsec proposal, and specify the SA negotiation mode, start and end points of an IPsec tunnel, key, and SA lifetime.

An IPsec policy is identified by a unique name and sequence number. IPsec policies with the same name and different sequence numbers form an IPsec policy group. An IPsec policy group can contain multiple ISAKMP IPsec policies (each IPsec policy has an advanced ACL referenced), but can contain only one template IPsec policy, as shown in Figure 1. In an IPsec policy group, an IPsec policy with a smaller sequence number has a higher priority.
