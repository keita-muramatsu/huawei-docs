IS-IS for IPv6

IPv6 networks require a dynamic routing protocol capable of providing accurate and valid routing information for IPv6 packet forwarding. IS-IS can meet this requirement with its good scalability. It supports IPv6 network layer protocols and is capable of discovering, generating, and forwarding IPv6 routes.

IETF defines the new contents of IS-IS to support IPv6. To process and calculate IPv6 routes, IS-IS uses two new TLVs and one network layer protocol identifier (NLPID).

The two TLVs are as follows:

  • TLV 232 (IPv6 Interface Address): is equivalent to the IP Interface Address TLV in IPv4, except that the original 32-bit IPv4 address is changed to a 128-bit IPv6 address.
  • TLV 236 (IPv6 Reachability): describes network reachability by defining information such as the route prefix and metric.

The NLPID is an 8-bit field that identifies network layer protocol packets, and the NLPID in IPv6 is 142 (0x8E). If IPv6 IS-IS is supported by a device, the routing information advertised by the device carries the NLPID field.

The fundamentals of IPv6 IS-IS are similar to those of IPv4 IS-IS. Unless otherwise specified, IS-IS stands for IPv4 IS-IS and IPv6 IS-IS. If differences between IPv4 IS-IS and IPv6 IS-IS are described, IPv4 IS-IS and IPv6 IS-IS are used for differentiation.
