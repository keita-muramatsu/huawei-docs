Configuring the Hold-off Period for Security Policy Backup-based Acceleration

Context

A device generates indexes for multiple security policies and uses an acceleration algorithm to quickly match security policies. In the case of a policy change (for example, a policy is created, modified, or deleted), policy indexes are re-created. The device backs up the current index, and the backup index is used for policy matching until the new index is generated, ensuring high processing performance. In this case, the new policy does not take effect immediately. If no modification is made to the policy during the acceleration hold-off period (10s by default), the device starts to generate a new policy index after the hold-off period, and the new policy takes effect after the new index is generated.

Procedure

  1. Enter the system view.

    system-view

  2. Adjust the hold-off period for enabling security policy backup-based acceleration after a policy is created, modified, or deleted.

    policy accelerate delay delay-time
