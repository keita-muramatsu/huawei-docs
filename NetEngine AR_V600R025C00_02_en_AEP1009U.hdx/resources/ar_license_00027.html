Others: Do I Need to Update the License When the device Software Is Updated?

If the management capability of the device remains unchanged after the device software upgrade, you do not need to apply for a new license. If the management scale is enlarged or new management components are added after the AR device software upgrade, you need to apply for a new license. The license application procedure is the same as that for initial license application. The ESDP will automatically combine control items in the original license and the new license.

When you apply for a new license due to device upgrade, ensure that the ESN in the new license is the same as that in the original license. If they are different, the ESDP cannot combine control items in the original license and the new license.
