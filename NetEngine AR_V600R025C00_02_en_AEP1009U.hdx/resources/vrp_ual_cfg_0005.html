Automatic NE Discovery

The master device automatically discovers NEs on the network through the UAL protocol.

The implementation process is as follows:
  1. The master device sends broadcast packets (carrying information about the master device such as the system MAC address, ESN, and device type) to other NEs every 30 seconds to check whether other NEs exist.
  2. Upon receiving the broadcast packet, an NE records the master device information and sends a unicast response packet (carrying information about the NE such as the system MAC address, ESN, and device type) to the master device.
  3. After receiving the response packet, the master device adds the NE to the device approval list. The NE becomes a member device of the master device. You can view the device approval list on WebMaster.
  4. The master device keeps sending broadcast packets every 30 seconds. If the NE does not respond to the broadcast packet three consecutive times, the master device sets the NE to offline.

Some NEs on the live network cannot respond to the packets from the master device. You can import information about these NEs in batches on WebMaster. Then the master device proactively adds these NEs to the device approval list.
