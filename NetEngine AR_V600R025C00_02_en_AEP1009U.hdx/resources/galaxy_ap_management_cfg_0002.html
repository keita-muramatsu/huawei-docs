Overview of AP Management

Definition

Access point (AP) management involves AP onboarding and AP configuration. In the WLAN access controller (WAC) + Fit AP networking, after APs go online on a WAC, you can manage and configure the APs on the WAC.

Purpose

The traditional Fat AP networking applies only to small and micro WLANs. On a large-scale WLAN, the WAC + Fit AP networking is typically used. This networking involves a large number of APs, and managing AP one by one is inefficient. A solution to this is AP management, which enables a WAC to centrally manage, control, and configure multiple APs, greatly improving WLAN O&M efficiency and reducing O&M costs.
