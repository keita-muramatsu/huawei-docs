Creating a VAP Profile

Context

After creating a VAP profile, you can configure parameters in the VAP profile and apply it in the WLAN view, AP group view, AP view, AP radio view, or AP group radio view to generate VAPs. VAPs provide wireless access services for STAs. Parameter settings in the VAP profile determine different wireless service capabilities available to STAs.

Procedure

  1. Enter the system view.

    system-view

  2. Enter the WLAN view.

    wlan

  3. Create a VAP profile and enter its view.

    vap-profile name profile-name
