After a Traffic Policy Is Configured with a Traffic Classifier in Which an IP Prefix List Is Specified, the Action in the Corresponding Traffic Behavior Is Performed on All Routes

If the IP prefix list specified in an if-match rule of the traffic classifier does not exist, all routes are matched by default. As a result, the action in the corresponding traffic behavior is performed on all routes.
