Overview of Intra-chassis High Availability

Definition

As the number of network users increases exponentially, device reliability becomes crucial. Intra-chassis high reliability allows a device to provide reliability through internal component backup, such as 1:1 redundancy backup for main control boards. For example, if a component fails, an active/standby switchover is performed to ensure service continuity.

Purpose

Intra-chassis high availability is configured to ensure uninterrupted services and normal services for users.

Benefits

Intra-chassis high availability improves device stability and service continuity on the live network.
