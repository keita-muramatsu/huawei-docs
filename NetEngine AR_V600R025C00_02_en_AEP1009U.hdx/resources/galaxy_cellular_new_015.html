Adjusting Cellular Modem Parameters

Prerequisites

Basic cellular functions have been configured, and the device can connect to a cellular network. While using the cellular network, you may need to adjust the network parameters. This chapter describes three adjustable cellular modem parameters: PLMN, cellular operating frequency band, and cellular dial-up interval.

The PLMN, cellular operating frequency band, and cellular dial-up interval are independent of each other. You can configure any of them as needed.
