Product Characteristics

This is a class A product, which may cause radio interference in a living environment. You may need to take appropriate preventive measures against its interference.

Huawei NetEngine AR series routers (ARs for short) are the next-generation routing and gateway devices that provide a variety of features such as SD-WAN, routing, switching, VPN, and security. They include AR5700, AR6700, and AR8000 series routers.

Carrier-Class Reliability

  • ARs support hot swappable power modules and optical modules, ensuring carrier-class reliability.
  • ARs comply with telecommunication standards to provide reliable and high-quality services for enterprise customers.
  • ARs provide attack defense mechanisms to defend against attacks from the network.
  • ARs support in-service patching so that the system software can be upgraded during system operation.

Service Integration Capability

ARs integrate various services of routers, switches, and wireless devices.

Hardware Extensibility

ARs provide a high port density and flexible slot combinations, allowing enterprise customers to connect to LAN, WAN, or wireless networks. ARs provide cost-effective enterprise network solutions.

Remote Maintenance Capability

In addition to one-stop deployment, plug-and-play capability, and remote commissioning functions, ARs can manage the customer premises equipment (CPE) remotely. The remote maintenance function improves maintenance efficiency and greatly reduces maintenance costs.
