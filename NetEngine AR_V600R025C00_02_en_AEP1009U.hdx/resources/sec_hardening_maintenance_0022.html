Local Attack Defense

In addition to receiving large numbers of service packets on a network, device CPUs may also receive many attack packets. If a CPU needs to process large numbers of attack packets for a long time, service and even system interruptions will occur. Similarly, processing large numbers of service packets will increase CPU usage and lower device performance, adversely affecting services.

To ensure that device CPUs can properly process and respond to valid services, the local attack defense function is designed for packets sent to CPUs, which helps protect devices against attacks and ensure normal service operation if an attack occurs.
