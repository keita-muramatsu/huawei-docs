NSR Configuration

As networks rapidly develop and applications become diversified, various value-added services, such as Internet Protocol television (IPTV) and video conferencing, have been widely deployed. Because network interruption may affect many services, resulting in significant loss, it is becoming increasingly important to ensure the reliability of network infrastructure.

In real-world applications, many non-technical factors can cause network failures and service interruptions. An effective way to enhance system reliability is to improve fault tolerance capabilities of the system, speed up fault recovery, and reduce the impact of faults on services.
