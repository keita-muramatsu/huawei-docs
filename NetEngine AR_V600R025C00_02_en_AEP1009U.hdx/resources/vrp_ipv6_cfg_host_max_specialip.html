Disabling a Device from Forwarding Layer 3 Packets with a Special IPv6 Address as the Source or Destination Address

Context

By default, a device forwards Layer 3 packets with a special IPv6 address as the source or destination address. To disable the device from forwarding Layer 3 packets with a special IPv6 address as the source or destination address, perform this configuration.

Procedure

  1. Enter the system view.

    system-view

  2. Disable the device from forwarding Layer 3 packets with a special IPv6 address as the source or destination address.

    ipv6 forward special-address [ unspecified-address | loopback-address ] * disable
