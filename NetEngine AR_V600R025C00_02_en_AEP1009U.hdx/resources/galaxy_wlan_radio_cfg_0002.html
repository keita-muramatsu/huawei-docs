Overview of RRM

Definition

Wireless local area network (WLAN) technology uses high-frequency (2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, or 6 GHz) radio signals as transmission media to achieve long-range coverage. Radio resource management (RRM) enables APs to dynamically adjust channels and transmit power to balance STA access load on the APs based on the surrounding radio environment. This function helps adjust radio coverage, reduces radio signal interference, and enables a WLAN to quickly adapt to changes in the radio environment.

Purpose

Radio signals are attenuated during transmission in the air, which degrades the service quality of Internet access for STAs. To address this, the RRM function is available to ensure high service quality for STAs and maintain an optimal radio resource utilization, thereby improving users' Internet access experience.
