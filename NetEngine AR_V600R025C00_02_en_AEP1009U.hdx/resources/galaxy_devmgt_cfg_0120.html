Enabling Transparent Transmission of the fast-open Option in TCP Packets

Context

After transparent transmission of the fast-open option is enabled, the device transparently transmits the fast-open option to the peer device to speed up TCP connection establishment.

Procedure

  1. Enter the system view.

    system-view

  2. Enable transparent transmission of the fast-open option in TCP packets.

    tcp-proxy fast-open-option bypass

    By default, transparent transmission of the fast-open option in TCP packets is disabled.
