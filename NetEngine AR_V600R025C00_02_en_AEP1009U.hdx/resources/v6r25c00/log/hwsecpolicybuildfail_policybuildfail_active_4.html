POLICY/4/hwSecPolicyBuildFail_active

Message

POLICY/4/hwSecPolicyBuildFail_active: The policy does not take effect, and traffic cannot be matched based on the configured rule.

Description

The policy does not take effect and traffic cannot be matched based on the configured rule.

Parameters

None

Possible Causes

1. The memory is insufficient.

2. Failed to obtain the application list.

Procedure

Collect trap, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support.
