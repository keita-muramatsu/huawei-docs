DEVM/4/hwBoardWarning_active

Message

DEVM/4/hwBoardWarning_active: The board experienced a minor fault. (EntPhysicalIndex=[EntPhysicalIndex], EntPhysicalName=[EntPhysicalName], EntityType=[EntityType], EntityTrapFaultID=[EntityTrapFaultID], Reason=[Reason])

Description

A minor fault occurs on the board.

Parameters

Parameter Name Parameter Meaning

EntPhysicalIndex

Entity index.

EntPhysicalName

Entity name.

EntityType

Actual NE type

1: MPU

2: LPU

3: SFU

4: PIC

5: CFX_CARD

6: OFC

EntityTrapFaultID

Fault code.

Reason

Alarm cause.

Possible Causes

  • Cause 1: A small number of internal CRC error packets are generated on the SFU.
  • Cause 2: Packet loss occurred in a queue on an SFU.
  • Cause 3: Packet loss occurred in a queue on the FDR module of an LPU.
  • Cause 4: All links on the SFU are down.
  • Cause 5: The buffer usage of the SFU is high. Check its bandwidth.
  • Cause 6: Packet loss occurs on forward engine ingress interface.
  • Cause 8: Link access to the forwarding engine is abnormal.
  • Cause 9: The volume of forwarded traffic is close to the maximum forwarding capability of the device.
  • Cause 10: The CPU failed to send packets.
  • Cause 11: The port between LSW and Coprocessor is Down.
  • Cause 12: The PLL status is unlocked on the port between LSW and Coprocessor.
  • Cause 13: CRC error packets are generated on the port between LSW and Coprocessor.
  • Cause 15: The bandwidth usage of internal communication interfaces exceeded 80%.
  • Cause 16: Packet loss occurred on internal communication interfaces.
  • Cause 17: The bandwidth of links between an SFU and an LPU was insufficient.
  • Cause 18: Access to the forwarding engine is unstable.
  • Cause 19: Frequent erase and write operations are performed on the disk.
  • Cause 20: The disk will soon become invalid.
  • Cause 21: The data disk will soon become invalid.
  • Cause 46: The liquid valve was not working properly.
  • Cause 47: The liquid valve communication was abnormal.
  • Cause 48: Opening adjustment of the liquid valve was abnormal.

Procedure

  • Cause 1: A small number of internal CRC error packets are generated on the SFU.

    1. If the fault persists for a long time, collect trap, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support.

  • Cause 2: Packet loss occurred in a queue on an SFU.

    1. Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 3: Packet loss occurred in a queue on the FDR module of an LPU.

    1. Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 4: All links on the SFU are down.

    1. Remove and insert the SFU. If the fault persists, collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 5: The buffer usage of the SFU is high. Check its bandwidth.

    1. Add a SFU. If the fault persists, collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 6: Packet loss occurs on forward engine ingress interface.

    1. Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 8: Link access to the forwarding engine is abnormal.

    1. Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 9: The volume of forwarded traffic is close to the maximum forwarding capability of the device.

    1. Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 10: The CPU failed to send packets.

    1. Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 11: The port between LSW and Coprocessor is Down.

    1. Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 12: The PLL status is unlocked on the port between LSW and Coprocessor.

    1. Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 13: CRC error packets are generated on the port between LSW and Coprocessor.

    1. Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 15: The bandwidth usage of internal communication interfaces exceeded 80%.

    1. Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 16: Packet loss occurred on internal communication interfaces.

    1. Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 17: The bandwidth of links between an SFU and an LPU was insufficient.

    1. Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 18: Access to the forwarding engine is unstable.

    1. Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 19: Frequent erase and write operations are performed on the disk.

    1. Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 20: The disk will soon become invalid.

    1. Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 21: The data disk will soon become invalid.

    Collect trap, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support.

    Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

    Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.
