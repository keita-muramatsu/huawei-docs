HRP/4/AUTHENTICATION_KEY

Message

HRP/4/AUTHENTICATION_KEY: Failed to verify the validity of HRP packets. The authentication keys or validity check status on the active and standby devices may be inconsistent.

Description

The validity check of the HRP packet fails. The possible cause is that the authentication key and/or validity check status of the active and standby devices are different.

Parameters

None

Possible Causes

After receiving an HRP packet from the peer device, the local device performs validity check. When the authentication keys on the active and standby devices are different, this log is triggered.

Procedure

Check whether the hrp authentication-key and hrp checksum enable command configurations on the active and standby devices are consistent.
