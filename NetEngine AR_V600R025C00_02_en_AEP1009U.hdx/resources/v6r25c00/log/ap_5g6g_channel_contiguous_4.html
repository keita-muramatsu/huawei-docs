WLAN/4/AP_5G6G_CHANNEL_CONTIGUOUS

Message

WLAN/4/AP_5G6G_CHANNEL_CONTIGUOUS: The 5G radio of the AP works on a high-frequency channel but its 6G radio works on the low-frequency channel. This degrades user experience. (MAC=[MAC],ApID=[ApID],Sysname=[Sysname],5GChannel=[Channel5G],5GBandwidth=[Bandwidth5G],6GChannel=[Channel6G],6GBandwidth=[Bandwidth6G],Reason=[Reason])

Description

If the 5 GHz radio of an AP works on a high frequency band and the 6 GHz radio works on a low frequency band, user experience is poor.

Parameters

Parameter Name Parameter Meaning

MAC

MAC address of an AP.

ApID

AP ID.

Sysname

AP name.

Channel5G

Effective 5 GHz channel.

Bandwidth5G

Effective 5 GHz bandwidth.

Channel6G

Effective 6 GHz channel.

Bandwidth6G

Effective 6 GHz bandwidth.

Reason

Reason. The possible reasons include user configurations or insufficient calibration channels.

Possible Causes

For an AP that supports both 5 GHz and 6 GHz frequency bands, if the 5 GHz radio works on channels 149 to 165 and the 6 GHz radio works on channels 1 to 13 (80 MHz bandwidth) or channels 1 to 29 (160 MHz bandwidth), interference between 5 GHz and 6 GHz channels is triggered.

Procedure

1. Run the display current-configuration command to check whether DCA and DBS are enabled on the target AP.

The following information in the command output indicates that DCA and DBS are enabled:

calibrate auto-channel-select enable

calibrate auto-bandwidth-select enable

  • If so, go to Step 2.
  • If not, go to Step 3.

2. Run the dca-channel { 5g | 6g } channel-set channel-value command to add a calibration channel set for the 5 GHz and 6 GHz radios of the target AP.

3. Run the channel { 20mhz | 40mhz-minus | 40mhz-plus | 80mhz | 160mhz } channel command to manually adjust the channel and bandwidth of the target AP to prevent radios from using 5 GHz high-frequency channels and 6 GHz low-frequency channels.

4. Check whether the log persists.

  • If so, go to Step 5.
  • If not, go to Step 6.

5. Collect trap, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support.

6. End.
