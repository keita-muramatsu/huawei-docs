HRP/2/DEVICE_MODEL_CHECK

Message

HRP/2/DEVICE_MODEL_CHECK: HRP negotiation fails due to the models of the two devices are different.

Description

The models of the two devices are different. As a result, HRP negotiation fails and hot standby cannot be established.

Parameters

None

Possible Causes

The device models at the two ends are different.

Procedure

The active and standby nodes use devices of the same model.
