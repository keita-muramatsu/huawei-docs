HRP/2/ENCRYPT_KEY

Message

HRP/2/ENCRYPT_KEY: The encryption configuration of HRP is different between two devices.

Description

The HRP encryption configurations on the two devices are different.

Parameters

None

Possible Causes

The hrp encryption enable command configurations on the active and standby devices are inconsistent.

Procedure

1. Configure the same hrp encryption enable command on the active and standby devices.
