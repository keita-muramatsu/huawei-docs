WLAN/4/hwApCsiNoAvailableRadioTrap_active

Message

WLAN/4/hwApCsiNoAvailableRadioTrap_active: Ap no available radio for csi measurement notify. (APMAC=[APMAC], APID=[APID], APName=[APName], UnavailableReason=[UnavailableReason])

Description

No CSI measurement radio was available on an AP.

Parameters

Parameter Name Parameter Meaning

APMAC

MAC address of an AP.

APID

AP ID.

APName

Indicates the AP name.

UnavailableReason

Indicates the reason for no available radio.

Possible Causes

Possible causes:

1) AP does not support CSI measurement: AP models do not support CSI measurement.

2) AP does not have the required number of antennas for CSI measurement: Some antennas of the RF may be turned off due to power consumption reduction or energy saving of the AP.

3) Services are not enabled on the AP radio that meets the CSI measurement requirements: The radio is not enabled or no VAP is enabled on the radio.

Procedure

1. If the AP does not support CSI measurement, replace it with an AP that supports CSI measurement.

2. If the AP does not have a radio that meets the CSI measurement requirement for the number of antennas, disable energy saving and change the power supply mode to reduce power consumption.

3. If the AP meets the CSI measurement requirement for the number of antennas and the radio of antennas is not enabled, enable the radio, create a VAP on the radio, and enable the VAP.
