WLAN/4/hwRadioAntennaGainIsUnlawfulTrap_active

Message

WLAN/4/hwRadioAntennaGainIsUnlawfulTrap_active: The EIRP is unlawful due to high antenna gain. (APMAC=[hwWlanRadioInfoApMac], RadioID=[hwWlanRadioID], APName=[hwWlanApName], ConfigAntennaGain=[hwWlanRadioActualAntennaGain], LegitimateAntennaGain=[hwWlanRadioLegitimateAntennaGain], APID=[hwWlanRadioInfoApId], LegitimateEIRP=[hwWlanRadioLegitimateEirp])

Description

The AP power failed to meet local laws and regulations due to high antenna gain.

Parameters

Parameter Name Parameter Meaning

hwWlanRadioInfoApMac

MAC address of an AP.

hwWlanRadioID

Radio ID of an AP.

hwWlanApName

Name of an AP.

hwWlanRadioActualAntennaGain

Configured antenna gain.

hwWlanRadioLegitimateAntennaGain

Maximum legitimate antenna gain of the current AP in the current state.

hwWlanRadioInfoApId

AP ID.

hwWlanRadioLegitimateEirp

Legitimate channel power.

Possible Causes

The current antenna gain is invalid.

Procedure

1. Collect trap, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support.

2. End.
