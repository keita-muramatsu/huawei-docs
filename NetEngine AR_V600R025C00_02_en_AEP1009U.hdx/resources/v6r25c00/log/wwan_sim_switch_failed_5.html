WWAN/5/WWAN_SIM_SWITCH_FAILED

Message

WWAN/5/WWAN_SIM_SWITCH_FAILED: The SIM switch failed. (Interface=[interface-name], OriginSIMID=[originsimid], NewSIMID=[newsimid], AT-result=[at-result], Reason=[switch-reason])

Description

The SIM card failed to be switched.

Parameters

Parameter Name Parameter Meaning

interface-name

Cellular interface name.

originsimid

ID of the SIM card before the switching.

newsimid

ID of the SIM card after the switching.

at-result

AT command execution result.

switch-reason

Reason for switching the SIM card.

Possible Causes

Cause 1: The AT command timed out.

Cause 2: The modem failed to return the AT command.

Card switching method 1: Run the sim switch to command to manually switch the SIM card.

Card switching method 2: Switch the SIM card through the APN profile.

Card switching method 3: The SIM card is switched during automatic switchback.

Card switching mode 4: The SIM card is automatically switched when no service is detected.

Procedure

Cause 1: The AT command timed out.

Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support engineers.

Cause 2: The modem failed to return the AT command.

Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support engineers.
