hwCBQoSMultiPolicyStatisticsClassifierTable

A table of class based policy statistics.

The indexes of the table are hwCBQoSIfApplyMultiPolicyIfIndex,hwCBQoSVlanApplyMultiPolicyVlanId,hwCBQoSIfApplyMultiPolicyDirection,hwCBQoSMultiPolicyStaPolicyIndex,hwCBQoSMultiPolicyStatClassifierIndex.

Integer32

read-only

hwCBQoSMultiPolicyStatClassifierIndex

Integer32

read-only

This object indicates the index of a traffic classifier.

The actually supported value range is 0..2047

1.3.6.1.4.1.2011.5.25.32.1.1.5.6.8.1.3

hwCBQoSMultiPolicyStatClassifierName

OCTET STRING{(1,31)}

read-only

This object indicates the traffic classifier name.

The value is a string of 1 to 31 case-sensitive characters without spaces and question marks; and must start with letters or digits.
