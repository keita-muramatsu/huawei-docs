isisISAdjIPAddrTable

This table contains the set of IP Addresses of neighboring Intermediate Systems as reported in received IIH PDUs.

The indexes of the table are isisSysInstance,isisCircIndex,isisISAdjIndex,isisISAdjIPAddrIndex.
