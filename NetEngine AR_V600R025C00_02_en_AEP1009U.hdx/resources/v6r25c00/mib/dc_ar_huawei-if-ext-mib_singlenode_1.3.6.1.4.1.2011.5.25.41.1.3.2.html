hwTrunkNextIndex

Integer32

read-only

The nextindex of Trunk interface, that means its position

The actually supported value range is 0..1023

The value should be set to the default; and the specific value is controlled by the PAF item SPEC_RES_IFMTRUNK_INTF_MAX_NUM. You can view it using the display paf command.
