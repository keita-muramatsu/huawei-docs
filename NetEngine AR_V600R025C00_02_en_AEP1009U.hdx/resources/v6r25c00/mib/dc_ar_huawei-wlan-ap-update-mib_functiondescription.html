Functions Overview

HUAWEI-WLAN-AP-UPDATE-MIB is used to manage AP upgrade features, including AP upgrade mode, FTP server, SFTP server, configuration of maximum number of connections, AP upgrade progress query, upgrade of one single AP or multiple APs, restart after upgrade, and upgrade cancellation.
