hwOpticalModuleInfoTable

This table describes optical module information, including the fiber type, wavelength, transmission distance, vendor information, temperature, bias current, receive optical power, transmit optical power, optical module type, rate, fiber interface, lower threshold of the receive power, upper threshold of the receive power, lower threshold of the transmit power, upper threshold of the transmit power, and whether the optical module is pluggable.

The index of the table is entPhysicalIndex.
