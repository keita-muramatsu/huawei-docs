mplsOutSegmentLdpLspTable

A table of LDP LSP's which map to the mplsOutSegmentTable in the MPLS-LSR-STD-MIB.

The indexes of the table are mplsLdpEntityLdpId,mplsLdpEntityIndex,mplsLdpPeerLdpId,mplsOutSegmentLdpLspIndex.
