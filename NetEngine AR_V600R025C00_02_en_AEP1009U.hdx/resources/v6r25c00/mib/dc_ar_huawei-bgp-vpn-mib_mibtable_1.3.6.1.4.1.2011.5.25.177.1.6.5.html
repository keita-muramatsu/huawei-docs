hwBgpDynPeerSessionExtTable

BGP Peer Session Table.

This table contains BGP dynamic peer session informations.

The indexes of the table are hwBgpDynPeerSessionExtProcessId,hwBgpDynPeerSessionExtVrfId,hwBgpDynPeerSessionExtRemoteAddrType,hwBgpDynPeerSessionExtRemoteAddr.
