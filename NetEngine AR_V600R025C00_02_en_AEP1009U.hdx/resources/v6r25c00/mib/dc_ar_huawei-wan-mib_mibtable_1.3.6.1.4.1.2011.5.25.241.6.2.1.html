hwConnectionDownReason

OCTET STRING{(0,64)}

read-only

This object indicates the reason of the connection down, which might be 1. site tunnel route is deleted 2. DH group does nto match 3. DH group is changed 4. TNP is deleted 5. TNP status changed to down 6. TNP weight is changed 7. KA detection failed 8. User reset

This object is implemented as defined in the corresponding MIB files.
