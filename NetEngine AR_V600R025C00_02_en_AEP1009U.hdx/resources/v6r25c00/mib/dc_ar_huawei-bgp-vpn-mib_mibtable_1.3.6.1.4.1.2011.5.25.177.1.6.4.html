hwBgpDynPeerStatisticTable

BGP dynamic peer statistic table. This table contains, one entry per BGP dynamic peer, information about the connections with BGP peers.

The indexes of the table are hwBgpDynProcessId,hwBgpDynPeerVrfInstanceId,hwBgpDynPeerAddr.
