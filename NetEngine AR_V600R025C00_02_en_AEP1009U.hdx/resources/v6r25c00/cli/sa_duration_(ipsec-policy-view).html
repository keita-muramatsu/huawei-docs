sa duration (IPSec profile view/IPSec policy template view /ISAKMP IPSec policy view)

Function

The sa duration command configures the hard lifetime of IPsec SAs in an IPsec policy or IPsec profile.

The undo sa duration command cancels the configuration.

By default, the hard lifetime of IPsec SAs is not configured in an IPsec policy or profile. The system uses the global hard lifetime of IPsec SAs.

Format

sa duration { traffic-based kilobytes | time-based seconds }

undo sa duration { traffic-based | time-based }

Parameters

Parameter Description Value
traffic-based kilobytes

Specifies the traffic-based SA hard lifetime.

It is recommended that the traffic volume be equal to or larger than the size of IPsec traffic forwarded in 1 hour.

The value is 0 or an integer in the range from 256 to 200000000, in KB.
time-based seconds

Specifies the time-based SA hard lifetime.

When a large number of IPsec tunnels are established between two devices, you are advised to set the IPsec SA hard lifetime to a value larger than or equivalent to 1800s.

The value is an integer ranging from 30 to 604800, in seconds.

Views

IPsec profile view, IPsec policy template view, ISAKMP IPsec policy view

Default Level

2: Configuration level

Usage Guidelines

Usage Scenario

When an IPsec SA is established through dynamic IKE negotiation, you can configure the SA lifetime to update the SA in real time. This reduces the risk of SA cracking and enhances security. The lifetime can be measured by time or traffic:

  • A time-based lifetime refers to a period from the time an SA is established to the time the SA expires.
  • A traffic-based lifetime refers to the maximum volume of traffic that can be processed by an SA.

The lifetime can also be classified as follows:

  • Hard lifetime: indicates the lifetime of an IPsec SA. When two devices negotiate an IKE SA, the actual IKE SA hard lifetime is the smaller of the two hard lifetime values configured on the two devices.
  • Soft lifetime: indicates the time after which a new IPsec SA is negotiated to replace the existing IPsec SA before the hard lifetime of the existing IPsec SA is about to expire.

    Before an IPsec SA is about to expire, IKE will negotiate a new IPsec SA for the remote end. The remote end uses the new IPsec SA to protect IPsec communication immediately after the new IPsec SA has been negotiated. If service traffic is being transmitted, the existing IPsec SA will be deleted immediately. If no service traffic is transmitted, the existing IPsec SA will be deleted after 10s or the hard lifetime expires.

    If both the time-based lifetime and traffic-based lifetime are set for an IPsec SA, the IPsec SA becomes invalid when either lifetime expires.

Precautions

You can run the ipsec sa global-duration command to configure the SA lifetime globally or in a specific IPsec policy or IPsec profile. If no IPsec SA lifetime is configured for a specific IPsec policy or IPsec profile, the global IPsec SA lifetime is used. If both the global IPsec SA lifetime and IPsec SA lifetime for a specific IPsec policy or IPsec profile are configured, the IPsec SA lifetime configured for a specific IPsec policy or IPsec profile takes effect.

In IKEv1 negotiation:

  • After the IPsec SA soft lifetime expires, the responder cannot initiate IPsec SA renegotiation.
  • After the IKE SA of the initiator is deleted and the IPsec SA soft lifetime expires, the initiator cannot initiate IPsec SA renegotiation.

In IKEv2 negotiation:

  • After the ike negotiate compatible command is run in the IKE peer view on the responder and the IPsec SA soft lifetime expires, the responder cannot initiate IPsec SA renegotiation.
  • After the encapsulation-mode auto command is run in the IPsec proposal view on the responder and the IPsec SA soft lifetime expires, the responder cannot initiate IPsec SA renegotiation.
  • After the IKE SA of the initiator or responder is deleted and the IPsec SA soft lifetime expires, the initiator or responder cannot initiate IPsec SA renegotiation.

Example

# Set the IPsec SA hard lifetime in an IPsec profile to 20000 KB. 
<HUAWEI> system-view
[HUAWEI] ipsec profile profile1
[HUAWEI-ipsec-profile-profile1] sa duration traffic-based 20000
# Set the IPsec SA hard lifetime in IPsec policy policy2 to 20000 KB. 
<HUAWEI> system-view
[HUAWEI] ipsec policy policy2 1 isakmp
[HUAWEI-ipsec-policy-isakmp-policy2-1] sa duration traffic-based 20000
# Set the IPsec SA hard lifetime in IPsec policy policy1 to 7200 seconds. 
<HUAWEI> system-view
[HUAWEI] ipsec policy policy1 1 isakmp
[HUAWEI-ipsec-policy-isakmp-policy1-1] sa duration time-based 7200
# Set the IPsec SA hard lifetime in an IPsec profile to 7200 seconds. 
<HUAWEI> system-view
[HUAWEI] ipsec profile profile1
[HUAWEI-ipsec-profile-profile1] sa duration time-based 7200
