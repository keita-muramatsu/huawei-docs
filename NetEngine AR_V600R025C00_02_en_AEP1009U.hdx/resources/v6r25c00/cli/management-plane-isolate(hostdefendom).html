management-plane isolate disable

Function

The management-plane isolate disable command disables traffic isolation between the service and management planes.

The undo management-plane isolate disable command enables traffic isolation between the service and management planes.

By default, traffic isolation between the service and management planes is enabled.

Format

management-plane isolate disable

undo management-plane isolate disable

Parameters

None

Views

System view

Default Level

3: Management level

Usage Guidelines

Usage Scenario

By default, traffic isolation between the service and management planes is enabled on a device, so that IP packets received by service interfaces are not forwarded through the management interface, and IP packets received by service interfaces and destined for the management interface cannot access the device.

If you want a device to forward service-plane IP packets through the management plane, run the management-plane isolate disable command to disable traffic isolation between the service and management planes.

After traffic isolation between the service and management planes is disabled using the management-plane isolate disable command, service-plane IP packets can pass through the management plane. That is, packets received by service interfaces can only access the IP address of the management interface, rather than being forwarded through the management interface.

Precautions

For devices that do not support management interfaces, traffic isolation between the service plane and management plane does not take effect.

Example

# Disable traffic isolation between the service and management planes.
<HUAWEI> system-view
[HUAWEI] management-plane isolate disable
