frequency

Function

The frequency command configures the working frequency band for a radio.

The undo frequency command restores the default working frequency band of radios on all AP in an AP group (in the AP group radio view) or restores the working frequency band of radios on a single AP to that configured in the AP group radio view (in the AP radio view).

By default, radio 0 in the AP group radio view operates on the 2.4 GHz frequency band, radio 1 on the 5 GHz frequency band, and radios 2, 3, and 4 on the 6 GHz frequency band. If radio 2, 3, or 4 does not support the 6 GHz frequency band, it operates on the default frequency band. There is no default value of the operating frequency band for radios in the AP radio view. The configuration in the AP group radio view is used.

Format

frequency { 2.4g | 5g | 6g }

undo frequency

Parameters

Parameter Description Value
2.4g

Specifies the 2.4 GHz frequency band.

-
5g

Specifies the 5 GHz frequency band.

-
6g

Specifies the 6 GHz frequency band.

-

Views

AP radio view, AP group radio view

Default Level

2: Configuration level

Usage Guidelines

Usage Scenario

For APs that support frequency band switching, a radio can work on only one frequency band at a time. You can configure the operating frequency band for the AP based on the frequency bands supported by STAs.

Precautions

When working in dual-5G mode, some APs support only low-frequency channels (36–64) or high-frequency channels (100–165) on the 5 GHz frequency band.

  • AirEngine 6776-56TP: radio 1 on high-frequency bands and radio 2 on low-frequency bands
  • AirEngine 6776I-X6TH: radio 1 on high-frequency bands and radio 2 on low-frequency bands
  • AirEngine 6776I-X7TH: radio 1 on high-frequency bands and radio 2 on low-frequency bands
  • AirEngine 6776I-X6ETH: radio 1 and radio 2 on full-frequency bands
  • AirEngine 5776-56T: radio 1 on high-frequency bands and radio 2 on low-frequency bands
  • AirEngine 5776I-X6EH: radios 0 and 1 on full-frequency bands
  • AirEngine 6776I-X6ETH-K: radio 1 and radio 2 on full-frequency bands

Changing the working frequency band of radios will delete their channel, power, and antenna gain configurations. If an AP uses an external antenna, run the antenna-gain antenna-gain command to reconfigure the antenna gain to be consistent with the gain of the external antenna connected to the AP.

If the working frequency band configured for AP radios using this command is the same as that of the actual working frequency band, the AP will not restart. Otherwise, the AP restarts after this command is executed.

The configuration in the AP radio view has a higher priority than that in the AP group radio view.

If the configured frequency band is not supported by an AP, the default frequency band takes effect.

If two radios of an AP both work on the 5 GHz frequency band, ensure that the operating channels of the two 5 GHz radios are separated by at least one channel to avoid interference. If two radios of an AP work on the 5 GHz and 6 GHz frequency bands, respectively, pay attention to channel planning as follows:

For the AirEngine 6776I-X7TH: Do not use a 5 GHz high-frequency channel (149–165) and a 6 GHz low-frequency channel (80 MHz channels 1–13, 160 MHz channels 1–29, 320 MHz channels 1–61) at the same time.

For example, if 40 MHz plus channels 36, 44, 52, and 60 are supported on the 5 GHz frequency band and one radio is deployed to work on channel 36, it is recommended that channel 52 or 60 be configured for the other radio. Channel 44 is not recommended in this case.

Example

# Configure radio 0 of AP 0 to operate on the 5 GHz frequency band.
<HUAWEI> system-view
[HUAWEI] wlan
[HUAWEI-wlan] ap-id 0
[HUAWEI-wlan-ap-0] radio 0
[HUAWEI-wlan-ap-0-radio-0] frequency 5g
Warning: Modifying the frequency band will delete the channel, power, and antenna gain configurations of the current radio on the AP and reboot the AP. Continue?[Y/N]:Y
