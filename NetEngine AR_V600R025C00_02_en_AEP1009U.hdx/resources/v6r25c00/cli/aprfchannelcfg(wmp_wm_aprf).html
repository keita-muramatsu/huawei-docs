channel

Function

The channel command configures the working bandwidth and channel for all AP radios in an AP group (AP group radio view) or a single AP radio (AP radio view) in an AP group.

The undo channel command restores the default working bandwidth and channel of all specified radios in an AP group (AP group radio view) or restores the working bandwidth and channel of a specified radio on an AP (AP radio view) to those configured in the AP group radio view.

By default, the working bandwidth for all AP radios in an AP group is 20 MHz, their working channels are not configured, and the working bandwidth and channel for radios on a single AP are not configured but are the same as that configured in the AP group to which the AP belongs.

Format

channel { 20mhz | 40mhz-minus | 40mhz-plus } channel

channel bandwidth channel

undo channel

Parameters

Parameter Description Value
20mhz

Sets the working bandwidth of a radio to 20 MHz.

-
40mhz-minus

Sets the working bandwidth of a radio to 40 MHz Minus.

-
40mhz-plus

Sets the working bandwidth of a radio to 40 MHz Plus.

-
channel

Specifies the working channel for a radio. The channel is selected based on the country code and radio mode.

The parameter is an enumeration value. The value range is determined based on the country code and radio mode.
bandwidth

Sets the working bandwidth of a radio to 80 MHz, 160 MHz, or 320 MHz.

The value is of the enumerated type, which can be 80mhz, 160mhz, or 320mhz.

Views

AP radio view, AP group radio view

Default Level

2: Configuration level

Usage Guidelines

Usage Scenario

Different radios support different channels, and the channels may vary according to countries. Select the channels according to the actual situation.

Precautions

  • The channel parameter settings must match the frequency band of a radio. For details about mappings between channels and frequency bands, see WLAN Country Codes and Channels Compliance. You can obtain this table at Huawei technical support website.
  • Ensure that the configured channels are supported by STAs; otherwise, the STAs cannot detect radio signals.
  • If an AP detects radar signals on a channel, the channel cannot be configured as the radio channel of the AP in 30 minutes. However, the channel can still be configured as the radio channel of other APs that do not detect radar signals on it.
  • The configuration in the AP radio view has a higher priority than that in the AP group radio view.
  • If an AP works in dual-5G mode, configure the two 5 GHz radios to operate on non-adjacent channels.

    For example, if 40 MHz plus channels 36, 44, 52, and 60 are supported on the 5 GHz frequency band and one radio is deployed to work on channel 36, it is recommended that channel 52 or 60 be configured for the other radio. Channel 44 is not recommended in this case.
  • The 80 MHz, 160 MHz, and 320 MHz channel widths take effect only for 5 GHz and 6 GHz radios.
  • When automatic channel selection is enabled, radios operate on the automatically selected channels rather than the manually configured ones. This ensures that the radios operate in the optimal channel environment.
  • The channel command configuration takes effect only after automatic channel selection is disabled by using the calibrate auto-channel-select disable command.
  • For the 6 GHz frequency band, you are advised to preferentially configure the preferred scanning channels (PSCs) defined in Wi-Fi 6E, including channels 5, 21, 37, 53, 69, 85, 101, 117, 133, 149, 165, 181, 197, 213, and 229. Otherwise, the STA access speed may be slow.

Example

# Set the working bandwidth to 20 MHz and channel to 6 for radio 0 of AP 1.
<HUAWEI> system-view
[HUAWEI] wlan
[HUAWEI-wlan] ap-id 1
[HUAWEI-wlan-ap-1] radio 0
[HUAWEI-wlan-ap-1-radio-0] channel 20mhz 6
Warning: This action may cause service interruption. Continue?[Y/N]y
