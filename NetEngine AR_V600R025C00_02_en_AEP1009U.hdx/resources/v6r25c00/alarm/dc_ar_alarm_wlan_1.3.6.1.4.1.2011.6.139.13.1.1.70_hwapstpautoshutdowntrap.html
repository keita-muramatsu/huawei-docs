Impact on the System

When STP detects a local loopback, it determines that a network storm occurs and will trigger shutdown of the local interface to prevent the storm from affecting services on the entire network; however, services on the local interface will be interrupted.

Possible Causes

After STP detects a loopback on an interface, the device shuts down the interface to prevent a loop.

Procedure

1. Check whether local loopback is configured on the interface where the alarm is generated.

  • If so, go to Step 4.
  • If not, go to Step 2.

2. Check whether a hub is incorrectly connected on the network connected to the interface.

  • If so, go to Step 5.
  • If not, go to Step 3.

3. Collect trap, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support.

4. Delete configurations that cause the local loopback.

5. Remove the self-looped cable.

6. End.
