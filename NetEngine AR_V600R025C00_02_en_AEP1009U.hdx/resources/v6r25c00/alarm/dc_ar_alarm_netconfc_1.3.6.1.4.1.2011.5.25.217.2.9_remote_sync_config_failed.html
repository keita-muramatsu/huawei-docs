Impact on the System

The configurations of the local and remote devices are inconsistent.

Possible Causes

Configurations fail to be synchronized from the local device to the peer device.

Procedure

1. View the NETCONF/6/NCA_OPER_RPC_REQ and NETCONF/6/NCA_OPER_RPC_RSP logs to locate the location where the configuration fails to be delivered and the cause.

2. Modify the configuration that fails to be delivered on the local end and deliver it again.

  • If the delivery is successful, no further action is required.
  • If the delivery fails, go to Step 3.

3. Collect log and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.
