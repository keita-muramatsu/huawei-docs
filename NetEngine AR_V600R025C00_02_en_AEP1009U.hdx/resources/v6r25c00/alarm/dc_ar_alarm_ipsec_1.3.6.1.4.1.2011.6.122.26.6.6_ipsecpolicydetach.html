IPSEC_1.3.6.1.4.1.2011.6.122.26.6.6 hwIPSecPolicyDetach

Trap Buffer Description

An IPSec policy is cancelled on an interface. (IfIndex=[interface-index], PolicyName=[policy-name], VsysName=[vsys-name], InterfaceName=[interface-name])

An IPsec policy is no longer applied to a specific interface.

Impact on the System

If a tunnel exists, the running tunnel is disconnected.

If no tunnel exists, services are not affected.

Possible Causes

When the application of an IPsec policy is deleted from an interface, the alarm is generated.

This message is informational only, and no action is required.
