WLAN_1.3.6.1.4.1.2011.6.139.16.1.1.1.1 hwRadioChannelChangedTrap

Trap Buffer Description

Channel of radio is changed. (APMAC=[hwWlanRadioInfoApMac], RadioID=[hwWlanRadioID], APName=[hwWlanRadioInfoApName], Channel1=[hwWlanRadioActualChannel], Channel2=[hwWlanRadioSecondActualChannel], CauseId=[hwWlanRadioChannelChangedReason], CauseStr=[hwWlanRadioChannelChangedReasonStr], PreChannel1=[hwWlanRadioPreActualChannel], PreChannel2=[hwWlanRadioPreSecondActualChannel], APID=[hwWlanRadioInfoApId], NewChannelHasRadarChannel=[hwRadioChannelChangedHasRadarChannel])

The AP channel was changed.

Trap Buffer Parameters

Parameter Description

hwWlanRadioInfoApMac

MAC address of an AP.

hwWlanRadioID

Radio ID.

hwWlanRadioInfoApName

Name of an AP.

hwWlanRadioActualChannel

Actual channel.

hwWlanRadioSecondActualChannel

Actual second channel.

hwWlanRadioChannelChangedReason

Channel change reason.

  • 1 - The channel was changed due to an unknown reason.
  • 2 - The channel was changed due to Dynamic Frequency Selection (DFS).
  • 3 - The channel was changed due to manual configurations.
  • 4 - The channel was changed due to radio calibration.
  • 5 - The channel was changed due to the notification of a 3GPP base station or the avoidance of invalid 3GPP channels.
  • 6 - The channel was changed to avoid conflict with the IoT card channel.
  • 7 - The channel was changed due to Mesh network establishment.
  • 8 - The channel was changed because the device reallocates a channel based on the DFS mechanism.
  • 9: channel change caused by radar channel switchback.
  • 10 - The channel was changed due to AP synchronization.

hwWlanRadioChannelChangedReasonStr

Character string description of the channel change reason.

hwWlanRadioPreActualChannel

Channel before the change.

hwWlanRadioPreSecondActualChannel

Second channel before the change.

hwWlanRadioInfoApId

AP ID.

hwRadioChannelChangedHasRadarChannel

Whether radar channels exist in the channels after the change.

Impact on the System

The change of the AP's operating channel may affect the other APs.

Possible Causes

  • The radio was calibrated.
  • Users changed the channel.
  • Radar signals conflicted.
  • The channel changed in WDS mode.
  • The channel changed in Mesh mode.

Procedure

1. No action is required.
