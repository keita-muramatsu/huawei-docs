Impact on the System

Links at some sites may be disconnected.

Possible Causes

1. The size of sites is too large. The device needs to establish connections with all sites, which beyonds the number of the device can support.

Procedure

1. Adjust the topology between sites on the controller page, connect the local site to the hub site, and forward packets through the hub site. Check whether the clear alarm is generated.

If so, go to Step 3.

If not, go to Step 2.

2. Collect alarm and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

3. End.
