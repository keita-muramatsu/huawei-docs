Impact on the System

  • Cause 1: The block memory usage exceeded the threshold

    1. Services such as forwarding, user access, and KPIs on the board may be affected or even interrupted.

    2. If the performance deteriorates continuously, the board may be reset due to block memory exhaustion.

  • Cause 2: The usage of client asynchronous messages exceeded the threshold

    1. Services such as forwarding and user access on the board may be affected or even interrupted.

    2. If the performance deteriorates continuously, the board may be reset due to the exhaustion of asynchronous messages on the client.

  • Cause 3: The usage of subscription messages exceeded the threshold

    1. Services such as forwarding, user access, and KPIs on the board may be affected or even interrupted.

    2. If the performance deteriorates continuously, the board may be reset due to subscription message exhaustion.

  • Cause 4: The usage of client connections exceeded the threshold

    1. Services such as forwarding, user access, and KPIs on the board may be affected or even interrupted.

    2. If the performance deteriorates continuously, the board may be reset due to client connection exhaustion.

  • Cause 5: The usage of client objects exceeded the threshold

    1. Services such as forwarding and user access on the board may be affected or even interrupted.

    2. If the performance deteriorates continuously, the board may be reset due to client object exhaustion.

  • Cause 6: The memory usage of the server tablespace exceeds the threshold

    1. Services such as forwarding, user access, and KPIs on the board may be affected or even interrupted.

    2. If the performance deteriorates continuously, the board may be reset because the server tablespace memory is exhausted.

  • Cause 7: The memory usage of subscription messages exceeded the threshold

    1. Services such as forwarding, user access, and KPIs on the board may be affected or even interrupted.

    2. If the performance deteriorates continuously, the board may be reset since the memory is exhausted by subscription messages.

  • Cause 8: The heap memory usage exceeded the threshold

    1. Services such as forwarding, user access, and KPIs on the board may be affected or even interrupted.

    2. If the performance deteriorates continuously, the board may be reset due to heap memory exhaustion.

  • Cause 32: The usage of client asynchronous messages in the YANG DB exceeded the threshold

    1. Services such as forwarding, user access, and KPIs on the board may be affected or even interrupted.

    2. If the performance deteriorates continuously, the board may be reset because the client asynchronous messages of the YANG DB are exhausted.

  • Cause 34: The usage of client connections in the YANG DB exceeded the threshold

    1. Services such as forwarding, user access, and KPIs on the board may be affected or even interrupted.

    2. If the performance deteriorates continuously, the board may be reset because the client connections of the YANG DB are exhausted.

  • Cause 36: The memory usage of the server tablespace in the YANG DB exceeds the threshold

    1. Services such as forwarding and user access on the board may be affected or even interrupted.

    2. If the performance deteriorates continuously, the board may be reset because the server tablespace memory of the YANG DB is exhausted.
