Impact on the System

This alarm is generated when the number of times that packets are retransmitted on the interface exceeds the alarm threshold. The possible cause is that the network is busy. Route synchronization and convergence may be slowed down.

Possible Causes

The number of OSPFv3 LSA retransmissions on an interface exceeds the limit.

Procedure

1. Run the display ospfv3 retrans-list command to check information about LSAs that are being retransmitted.

2. Collect alarm information and LSA retransmission information, and contact technical support engineers.

3. End.
