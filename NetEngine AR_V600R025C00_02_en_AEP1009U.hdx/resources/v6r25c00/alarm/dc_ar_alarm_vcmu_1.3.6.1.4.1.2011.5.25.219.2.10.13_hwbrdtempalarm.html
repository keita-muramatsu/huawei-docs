Impact on the System

  • Cause 1: The card temperature was too high.

    Services are not affected temporarily, but the subcard temperature needs to be lowered as soon as possible.

  • Cause 2: The board temperature was too high.

    Services are not affected temporarily, but the board temperature needs to be lowered as soon as possible.

  • Cause 3: The board temperature fell below the lower limit.

    Services are not affected temporarily, but the board temperature needs to be increased as soon as possible.

  • Cause 4: The subcard temperature fell below the lower limit.

    Services are not affected temporarily, but the subcard temperature needs to be increased as soon as possible.

Possible Causes

  • Cause 1: The card temperature was too high.
  • Cause 2: The board temperature was too high.
  • Cause 3: The board temperature fell below the lower limit.
  • Cause 4: The subcard temperature fell below the lower limit.

Procedure

  • Cause 1: The card temperature was too high.

    1. Check whether the air filter of the fan is blocked.

    • If so, clean the air filter.
    • If not, go to Step 2.

    2. Check whether the ambient temperature is too high.

    • If so, lower the ambient temperature.
    • If not, go to step 3.

    3. Check whether the fan is faulty.

    • If so, rectify the fan fault.
    • If not, go to Step 4.

    4. Check whether filler panels are installed in vacant slots.

    • If so, go to Step 5.
    • If not, insert filler panels.

    5. If a high temperature alarm or historical alarm is generated, collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 2: The board temperature was too high.

    1. Check whether the air filter of the fan is blocked.

    • If so, clean the air filter.
    • If not, go to Step 2.

    2. Check whether the ambient temperature is too high.

    • If so, lower the ambient temperature.
    • If not, go to step 3.

    3. Check whether the fan is faulty.

    • If so, rectify the fan fault.
    • If not, go to Step 4.

    4. Check whether filler panels are installed in vacant slots.

    • If so, go to Step 5.
    • If not, insert filler panels.

    5. If a high temperature alarm or historical alarm is generated, collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 3: The board temperature fell below the lower limit.

    1. Adjust the ambient temperature or increase the distance between the device and the air conditioner.

    2. Collect trap, log, and configuration information, and contact Huawei technical support personnel.

  • Cause 4: The subcard temperature fell below the lower limit.

    1. Adjust the ambient temperature or increase the distance between the device and the air conditioner.

    2. Collect trap, log, and configuration information, and contact Huawei technical support personnel.
