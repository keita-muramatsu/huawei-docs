L3VPN_1.3.6.1.4.1.2011.5.25.145.34.4 hwIpv6RouteThresholdExceedClear

Trap Buffer Description

The number of IPv6 routes in the VPN instance fell below the alarm threshold multiplied by the maximum value. (VPN instance name=[vpnName], number of existing routes=[current], alarm threshold=[midThreshold])

Impact on the System

Services will not be affected.

Possible Causes

Cause 1: Routes were deleted by a specific protocol.

Cause 2: Configuration of the routing-table limit command was deleted or modified to increase the limit on the maximum number of routes so that the number of the VPN IPv6 routes was lower than the middle threshold.

Procedure

  • Collect alarm information and configuration information, and then contact technical support personnel.
