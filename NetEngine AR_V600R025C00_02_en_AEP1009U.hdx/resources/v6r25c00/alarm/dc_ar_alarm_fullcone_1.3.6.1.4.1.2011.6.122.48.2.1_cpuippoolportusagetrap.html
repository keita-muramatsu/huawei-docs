Impact on the System

100% of the ports for the address pool on a CPU are already assigned. Packets loss occurred because no more ports can be assigned.

Possible Causes

The port usage of the full cone NAT address pool on a CPU of the device exceeded the threshold.

Procedure

1. Run the disp firewall server-map full-cone command in the system view.

2. Check whether the reclaim and aging of 3-tuple servermap nodes are correct.
