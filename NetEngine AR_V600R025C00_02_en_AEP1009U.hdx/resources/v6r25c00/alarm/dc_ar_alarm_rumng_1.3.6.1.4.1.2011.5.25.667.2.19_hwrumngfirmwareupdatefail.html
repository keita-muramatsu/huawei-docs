Impact on the System

This alarm is generated when the central device fails to upgrade the RU. This alarm needs to be manually cleared.

Possible Causes

Cause 1: UpgradeTimeOut: The RU upgrade timed out.

Cause 2: upgrade is break: The upgrade is interrupted. A typical scenario is that the RU is reset during the upgrade.

Cause 3: no signature: The firmware of the new version does not have a signature.

Cause 4: No Need Upgrade: The RU does not need to be upgraded.

Procedure

1. If the Reason field in the alarm information is UpgradeTimeOut, check whether the communication between the central device and RU is normal.

2. If the Reason field in the alarm information is upgrade is break, the RU will be upgraded again, and no action is required.

3. If the Reason field in the alarm information is no signature, generally, the signature of the firmware patch is incorrect. In this case, contact the firmware patch provider to check whether the signature in the patch is correct.

4. If the Reason field in the alarm information is No Need Upgrade, the firmware of the RU does not need to be upgraded to the firmware version of the device, and no action is required.
