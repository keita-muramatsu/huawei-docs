Impact on the System

Services of the new STA are unavailable.

Possible Causes

The IP address of the new STA conflicts with that of an existing STA.

Procedure

1. If the IP address of the STA is statically configured, reconfigure the IP address of the STA. Alternatively, configure the STA to obtain an IP address through DHCP. Check whether the alarm is cleared.

If so, go to Step 3.

If not, go to Step 2.

2. Collect trap, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support.

3. End.
