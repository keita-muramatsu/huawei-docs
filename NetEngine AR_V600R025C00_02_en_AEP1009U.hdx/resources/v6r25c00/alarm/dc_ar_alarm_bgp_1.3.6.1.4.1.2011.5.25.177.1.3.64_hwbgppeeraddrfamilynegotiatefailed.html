Impact on the System

The peer relationship fails to be established in the specified address family, routes are unreachable, and BGP forwarding services are interrupted.

Possible Causes

The address family negotiation has been successful, but the address family negotiation fails when the BGP peer relationship is established.

Procedure

1. Check whether the peer relationship fails to be established due to its own configuration.

  • If yes, go to Step 2.
  • If no, go to Step 3.

2. Cancel the configuration that causes the failure to establish the peer relationship.

3. Check whether the BGP connection is reset.

  • If the BGP connection is not reset, go to Step 4.
  • If the BGP connection is reset, wait for a while and check whether the connection is restored. If the connection is not restored, go to Step 4.

4. Collect alarm and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.
