Impact on the System

The AP may fail to be powered on due to insufficient power supply.

Possible Causes

The AP's power module cannot be used due one of the following reasons:

  • 136961: The power module fails to obtain voltage information from the I2C interface.
  • 136962: An overvoltage occurs.
  • 136963: An undervoltage occurs.

Procedure

1. Replace the AP power module. Then, check whether the alarm persists.

  • If so, go to Step 2.
  • If not, go to Step 3.

2. Collect trap, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support.

3. End.
