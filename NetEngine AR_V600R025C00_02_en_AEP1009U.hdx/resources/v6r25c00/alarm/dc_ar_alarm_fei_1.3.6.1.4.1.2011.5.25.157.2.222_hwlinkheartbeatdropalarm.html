FEI_1.3.6.1.4.1.2011.5.25.157.2.222 hwLinkHeartbeatDropAlarm

Trap Buffer Description

The SAID PING function detected that the packet loss rate reached or exceeded the threshold. (InterfaceIfIndex=[InterfaceIfIndex], InterfaceName=[InterfaceName], SendInterfaceName=[SendInterfaceName], RecvInterfaceName=[RecvInterfaceName])

The SAID ping detected that the packet loss rate on a link reached or exceeded the threshold.

This alarm is supported only on AR6700-H series, AR8100 series, AR8700 series.
