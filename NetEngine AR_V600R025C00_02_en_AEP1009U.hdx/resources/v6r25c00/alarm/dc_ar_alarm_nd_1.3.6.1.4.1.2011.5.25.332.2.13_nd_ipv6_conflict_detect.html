Impact on the System

If this log is generated, conflicting IPv6 addresses exist on ND neighbors on the network. If the conflict is not eliminated in a timely manner, faults such as route flapping and service or traffic interruption may occur.

Possible Causes

A local NB entry exists on the device. When the device receives an NS/NA message other than an unsolicited multicast NA message, the source IPv6 address of the message is the same as an IPv6 address in the local dynamic NB entry, but the source MAC address is different from the MAC address in the entry.

Procedure

1. Check the devices configured with the same IPv6 address on the network. Change the IPv6 addresses of the interfaces based on the IPv6 address plan to resolve the IPv6 address conflict.

2. Check whether a loop exists on the network. If a loop exists, remove it.
