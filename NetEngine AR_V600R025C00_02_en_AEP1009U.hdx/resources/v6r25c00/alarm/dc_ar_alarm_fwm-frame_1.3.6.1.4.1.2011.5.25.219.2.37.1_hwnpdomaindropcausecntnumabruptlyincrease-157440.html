FWM-FRAME_1.3.6.1.4.1.2011.5.25.219.2.37.1 hwNPDomainDropCauseCntNumabruptlyAlarm-157440

Trap Buffer Description

Causes drop count continuously increase. (EntityPhysicalIndex=[EntityPhysicalIndex], EntPhysicalName=[EntPhysicalName],EntityTrapFaultID=[1],LpuSlotId=[LpuSlotId],LpuFeId=[LpuFeId],DropCauseId=[DropCauseId]).

If the key drop cause count on the NP suddenly increases and exceeds the preset threshold, an alarm is reported. If the count falls below the threshold, the alarm is cleared.

Impact on the System

When this alarm is generated, service packets may have been discarded unexpectedly.

Possible Causes

The alarm is generated when the packet loss rate of corresponding to Cause meets either of the following conditions:

1. The packet loss rate increase by 10 times within a consecutive of two periods (5s a period) and the value exceeds 10000 pps.

2. The packet loss rate doubles within a consecutive of 10 periods (5s a period) and the value exceeds 2000 pps.

Procedure

1. Power cycle the faulty board. After the board is re-registered, check whether the alarm is cleared.

  • If so, go to Step 4.
  • If not, go to Step 2.

2. Replace the board. After the new board is registered, check whether the alarm is cleared.

  • If so, go to Step 4.
  • If not, go to Step 3.

3. Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support.

4. End.
