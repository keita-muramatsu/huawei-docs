Impact on the System

When the number of ports allocated to a public IP address of a CPU reached 100%, no new port can be allocated. As a result, packet loss occurred.

Possible Causes

Ports allocated to a global IP address in the full cone NAT address pool on a CPU of the device exceeded the threshold.

Procedure

1. Run the disp firewall server-map full-cone command in the system view.

2. Check whether the reclaim and aging of 3-tuple servermap nodes are correct.
