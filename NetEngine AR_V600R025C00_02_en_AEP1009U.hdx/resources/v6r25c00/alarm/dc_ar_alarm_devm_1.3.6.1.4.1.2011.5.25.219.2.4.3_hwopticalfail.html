Impact on the System

  • Cause 1: The cable or optical module cannot be identified.

    Services may be interrupted.

  • Cause 2: The license of the PON module is not activated.

    Services may be interrupted.

  • Cause 3: Some lanes of the optical module are faulty.

    The port rate decreases, and services may be affected.

Possible Causes

  • Cause 1: The cable or optical module cannot be identified.
  • Cause 2: The license of the PON module is not activated.
  • Cause 3: Some lanes of the optical module are faulty.

Procedure

  • Cause 1: The cable or optical module cannot be identified.

    1. Check whether the cable or optical module is faulty. If yes, replace the cable or optical module to clear the alarm.

    2. If the alarm persists after the replacement, collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 2: The license of the PON module is not activated.

    1. Replace the module with another module or activate the license of the PON module.

    2. If the alarm persists, collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 3: Some lanes of the optical module are faulty.

    1. Replace the optical module, peer optical module, or cable.

    2. If the alarm persists, collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.
