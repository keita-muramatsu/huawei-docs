RUMNG_1.3.6.1.4.1.2011.5.25.667.2.9 hwRuPortCrcErrorRising

Trap Buffer Description

Remote unit CRC error is rising. (RemoteUnitEsn=[RemoteUnitEsn], InterfaceName=[InterfaceName], CrcErrorStatistics=[CrcErrorStatistics], CrcErrorThreshold=[CrcErrorThreshold], CrcErrorInterval=[CrcErrorInterval](s))

Impact on the System

Some service data on the port is discarded.

Possible Causes

  • Cause 1: transceiver fault
  • Cause 2: Fiber failure

Procedure

  • Cause 1: transceiver fault

    Replace the optical module and check whether the alarm is cleared.

    • If so, no further action is required.
    • If not, go to Step 2.
  • Cause 2: Fiber failure

    Replace the optical fiber and check whether the alarm is cleared.

    • If so, no further action is required.
    • If not, collect alarm and configuration information, and contact technical support.
