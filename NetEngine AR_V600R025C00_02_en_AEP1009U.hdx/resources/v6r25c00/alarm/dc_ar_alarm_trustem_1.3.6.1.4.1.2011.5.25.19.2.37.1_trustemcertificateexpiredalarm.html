TRUSTEM_1.3.6.1.4.1.2011.5.25.19.2.37.1 hwTrustemCertificateExpiredAlarm

The trustem certificate already expired. (PhysicalIndex=[PhysicalIndex], PhysicalName=[PhysicalName], CertificateName=[hwTrustemCertificateName], CertificateType=[hwTrustemCertificateType], BeginTime=[hwTrustemCertificateBeginTime], EndTime=[hwTrustemCertificateEndTime])

This alarm is generated when the trustem certificate expires.

Impact on the System

Users cannot pass trustem security authentication.

Possible Causes

The trustem certificate has expired.

Procedure

1. Check whether the trusted certificate can be replaced.

  • If yes, go to step 2.
  • If not, go to step 3.

2. Replace the certificate with a valid one.

  • If the alarm is cleared, go to step 4.
  • If the alarm persists, go to Step 3.

3. Collect log and configuration information, and contact Huawei technical support personnel.

4. End.
