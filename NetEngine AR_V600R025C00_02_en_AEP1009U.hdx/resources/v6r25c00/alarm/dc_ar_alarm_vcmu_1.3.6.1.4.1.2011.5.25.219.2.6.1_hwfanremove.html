Impact on the System

The heat dissipation performance of the device is affected, but the temperature of the device increases. Services are not affected in a short period of time, but the fault needs to be rectified immediately.

Possible Causes

A fan module was removed.

Procedure

1. Check whether a fan module is installed in a slot.

  • If so, go to step 2.
  • If not, insert the fan module.

2. Check whether the fan module is properly inserted.

  • If the fan module is properly inserted, replace the fan module.
  • If not, install the fan module properly.
