Impact on the System

The process failed to be started.

Possible Causes

  • Cause 1: This alarm is generated when the APPDB request of a process times out.
  • Cause 2: The process configured with the fault alarm policy is repeatedly started for more than three times within 10 minutes.

Procedure

  • Cause 1: This alarm is generated when the APPDB request of a process times out.

    1. In normal cases, the number of restored configurations affects the delivery of the APPDB during device startup. Wait until the configuration restoration is complete and check whether the alarm is cleared.

    If so, go to Step 3.

    If not, go to Step 2.

    2. Check whether the remaining disk space is sufficient.

    If so, clear the space, wait for a while, and check whether the alarm is cleared.

    If not, go to Step 3.

    3. Collect alarm and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 2: The process configured with the fault alarm policy is repeatedly started for more than three times within 10 minutes.

    1. For processes created during dynamic deployment, delete the processes and create them again.

    2. Collect alarm and configuration information, and contact technical support.
