Impact on the System

The device is powered off.

When this event occurs, two identical alarms may be generated.

Possible Causes

Cause 1: The power cable is loose.

Cause 2: The external power supply line is faulty.

Cause 3: The power module of the device is faulty.

Procedure

1. Check whether the indicator on the power module is steady green.

  • If so, go to Step 2.
  • If not, go to Step 3.

2. Run the display device power command to check whether the power supply is normal.

  • If so, the event may be caused by abnormal fluctuation or instability of the external power supply. The power supply can withstand the abnormal fluctuation or instability, but the external power supply may have potential risks. You are advised to go to Step 4 to check the power supply.
  • If not, go to Step 5.

3. Check whether the power cable is loose.

  • If so, secure the power cable.
  • If not, go to Step 4.

4. Check whether the external power supply is normal.

  • If so, rectify the external power supply fault.
  • If not, go to Step 5.

5. Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.
