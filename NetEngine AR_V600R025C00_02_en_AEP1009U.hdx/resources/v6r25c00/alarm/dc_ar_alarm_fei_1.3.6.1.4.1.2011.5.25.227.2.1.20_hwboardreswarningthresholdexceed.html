FEI_1.3.6.1.4.1.2011.5.25.227.2.1.20 hwBoardResWarningThresholdExceed

Trap Buffer Description

The number of forwarding resources reaches the alarm threshold. (Slot = [hwFwdResLackSlotStr], Threshold = [hwFwdResThreshold], Reason = [hwFwdResLackReasonId], Description = [hwReasonDescription])

The remaining forwarding resources on the device were insufficient, and the amount of forwarding resources exceeded the alarm threshold.
