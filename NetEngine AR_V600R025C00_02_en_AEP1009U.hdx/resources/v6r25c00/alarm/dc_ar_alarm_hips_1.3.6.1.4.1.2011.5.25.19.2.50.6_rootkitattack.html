HIPS_1.3.6.1.4.1.2011.5.25.19.2.50.6 hwHipsRootkitAttack

Trap Buffer Description

Rootkit has been detected. (eventCategory=[event-category], occurTime=[occurTime], result=[result], rootkitName=[rootkitName], detectionType=[detectionType], detectedThreat=[detectedThreat], detectionSource=[detectionSource], slot=[slot], card=[card], cpu=[cpu], barcode=[barcode])

Rootkit malicious files are detected. Rootkit is a tool used by attackers to hide their traces and retain root access permissions during attacks. It can hide files, processes, network connections, and kernel modules, reside for a long time, and have the highest permissions.
