The Portal quiet max user alarm is cleared. (SpecOfUserNum=[SpecOfUserNum], CurrentThreshold=[CurrentThreshold], LowerThreshold=[LowerThreshold], UpperThreshold=[UpperThreshold], ProcessName=[ProcessName])
