Possible Causes

  • Cause 1: The on-card firmware is damaged.
  • Cause 2:

Procedure

  • Cause 1: The on-card firmware is damaged.

    1. Remove the subcard that reports the alarm and check whether the alarm is cleared.

    · If so, return the subcard to the factory for repair.

    · If not, go to Step 2.

    2. Collect alarm, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

    3. End.

  • Cause 2:

    1. Wait until the SPI bus recovers. If the corresponding clear alarm is not reported within 10 minutes, go to step 2.

    2. Collect log files and contact technical support engineers.
