PKT-REP_1.3.6.1.4.1.2011.5.25.241.15.2.3 hwPacketRepInstanceLimitExceed

Trap Buffer Description

The packet replication instance has reached 100% of the maximum number. (slot=[hwPacketRepTrapSlot], CPU=[hwPacketRepTrapCpu], PacketRepInstanceMaxNumber=[PacketRepInstanceMaxNumber])

The number of packet replication instances reaches the maximum.

This alarm is supported only on AR5710-S52T2XE4, AR6710-L8T3TS1X2-T, AR6710-L26T2X4, AR6710-L50T2X4-T, AR6700-H series, AR6710-L8T3TS1X2, AR6710-L50T2X4, AR8100 series, AR5710-S8TWDSE-LTE4EA, AR8700 series, AR5710-S28T2S2XE4, AR5710-S8P2XE-NRGL, AR5710-S8T2XE-NRGL, AR6710-L14T2X4, AR5710-S8P1XDSE-2LTE4EA, AR5710-S8T1XWE-NRGL, AR6710-L26T2X4-T, AR5700-H series, AR5710-S8T2XE-LTE4EA-T, AR5710-S8T1XWE, AR5710-S8T2XE.

Impact on the System

Packets that exceed the packet replication specifications cannot be restored, because packet replication anti-loss protection is not provided for them.

Possible Causes

The number of packet replication instances requiring replication or dereplication at the current site exceeds the upper limit supported by the device.

Procedure

Adjust the traffic matching rules on the encoding end to reduce traffic of low-priority services, or disable the packet replication function at low-priority sites.
