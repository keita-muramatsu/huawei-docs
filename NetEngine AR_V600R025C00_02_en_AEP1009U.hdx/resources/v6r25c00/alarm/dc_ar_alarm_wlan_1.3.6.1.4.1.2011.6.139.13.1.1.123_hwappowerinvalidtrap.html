Impact on the System

The AP may fail to be powered on due to insufficient power supply, and cannot work properly. The fault needs to be rectified quickly.

Possible Causes

The power module was installed properly but not switched on.

Procedure

1. Switch on the power module. Then, check whether the alarm persists.

  • If so, go to Step 2.
  • If not, go to Step 3.

2. Collect trap, log, and configuration information, and contact technical support.

3. End.
