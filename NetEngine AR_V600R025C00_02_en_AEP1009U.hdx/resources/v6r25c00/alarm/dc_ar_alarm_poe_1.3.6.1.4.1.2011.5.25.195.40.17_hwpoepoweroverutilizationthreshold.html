POE_1.3.6.1.4.1.2011.5.25.195.40.17 hwPoePowerOverUtilizationThreshold

Trap Buffer Description

PoE power consumption has exceeded the upper limit. (DeviceID=[DeviceID], ConsumingPower=[ConsumingPower], PowerThreshold=[PowerThreshold])

PSE power consumption has exceeded the upper limit.

This alarm is supported only on AR5710-S52T2XE4, AR5710-S8T2X-LTE4EA, AR5710-S8T2X, AR5710-S8P2X, AR5710-S8T2S, AR5710-S10T1X2, AR5710-S8TWDSE-LTE4EA, AR5710-S28T2S2XE4, AR5710-S8P2XE-NRGL, AR5710-S8T2XE-NRGL, AR5710-S8P1XDSE-2LTE4EA, AR5710-S8T1XWE-NRGL, AR5710-S8T2XE-LTE4EA-T, AR5710-S8T1XWE, AR5710-S8T2XE.

Impact on the System

If the alarm persists, all the PDs may be powered off because of insufficient power supply.

Possible Causes

The total power consumption of all the PDs is greater than or equal to the alarm threshold.

Procedure

1. Check whether the power consumption of the PDs is within the accepted range.

  • If so, go to Step 2.
  • If not, go to Step 3.

2. Run the poe power-threshold command to change the alarm threshold for PoE power consumption percentage.

3. Replan the network based on the system external power supply capability. Run the poe priority command to increase the priority of important PDs to prevent their power from being occupied by other PDs. Power off the PDs that are not required to ensure system reliability.
