Impact on the System

When detecting a loopback of protocol packets, the device considers that a network storm occurs and blocks the port to prevent the network storm from affecting services on the entire network. However, blocking the port interrupts the services accessed by the port.

Possible Causes

  • Cause 1: Local loopback is configured on the port.
  • Cause 2: A self-loop occurs on the network connected to the port.

Procedure

  • Cause 1: Local loopback is configured on the port.

    1. Check whether local loopback is configured on the port where the log is generated.

    • If so, go to Step 2.
    • If not, go to Step 3.

    2. Delete the local loopback configuration, and then check whether the alarm is cleared. If the alarm persists, go to Step 3.

    3. Collect alarm and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.

  • Cause 2: A self-loop occurs on the network connected to the port.

    1. Check the networking of the network connected to the port and check whether a self-loop occurs on the hub or hub.

    • If so, go to Step 2.
    • If not, go to Step 3.

    2. Remove the self-looped network cable and check whether the alarm is cleared. If the alarm persists, go to Step 3.

    3. Collect alarm and configuration information, and contact technical support personnel.
