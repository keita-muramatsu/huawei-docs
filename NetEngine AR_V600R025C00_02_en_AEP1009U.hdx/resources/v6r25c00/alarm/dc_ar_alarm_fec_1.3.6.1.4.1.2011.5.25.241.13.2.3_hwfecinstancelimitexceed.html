FEC_1.3.6.1.4.1.2011.5.25.241.13.2.3 hwFecInstanceLimitExceed

Trap Buffer Description

The fec instance has reached 100% of the maximum number. (FecInstanceMaxNumber=[FecInstanceMaxNumber])

The number of FEC instances reached the maximum value.

This alarm is supported only on AR5710-S52T2XE4, AR6710-L8T3TS1X2-T, AR6710-L26T2X4, AR6710-L50T2X4-T, AR6700-H series, AR6710-L8T3TS1X2, AR6710-L50T2X4, AR8100 series, AR5710-S8TWDSE-LTE4EA, AR8700 series, AR5710-S28T2S2XE4, AR5710-S8P2XE-NRGL, AR5710-S8T2XE-NRGL, AR6710-L14T2X4, AR5710-S8P1XDSE-2LTE4EA, AR5710-S8T1XWE-NRGL, AR6710-L26T2X4-T, AR5700-H series, AR5710-S8T2XE-LTE4EA-T, AR5710-S8T1XWE, AR5710-S8T2XE.

Impact on the System

If packet loss occurs, packets exceeding the specification cannot be restored because they are not protected by the FEC anti-packet loss function.

Possible Causes

The number of FEC instances requiring FEC encoding or decoding at the current site exceeds the upper limit supported by the device.

Procedure

1. Adjust the traffic matching rules on the FEC encoding side to reduce low-priority service traffic, or disable the FEC function for some low-priority sites.
