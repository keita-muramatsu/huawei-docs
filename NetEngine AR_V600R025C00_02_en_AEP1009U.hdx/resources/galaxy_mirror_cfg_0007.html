Understanding Port Mirroring

Port mirroring copies packets received or sent by a specified port to an observing port. Local port mirroring copies packets to a local observing port that is directly connected to a monitoring device.

On the network show in Figure 1, the mirrored port on DeviceA copies the received or sent packets to the local observing port, which then sends the copy of the original packets to the monitoring device.
